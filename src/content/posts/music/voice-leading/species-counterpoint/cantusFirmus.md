Exercises in strict voice-leading, or species counterpoint, begin with a single, well formed musical line called the *cantus firmus* (fixed voice, or fixed melody; pl. *cantus firmi*). *Cantus firmus* composition gives us the opportunity to engage the following fundamental musical traits:

严格声部进行（即种类对位）的练习，始于一条单一而结构良好的音乐线条，称为*固定旋律*（cantus firmus，固定声部或固定旋律；复数作 *cantus firmi*）。写作*固定旋律*让我们有机会处理以下基本音乐特性：

- smoothness
- independence and integrity or melodic lines
- variety
- motion (towards a goal)

- smoothness／平滑流畅
- independence and integrity or melodic lines／各旋律线条的独立性与完整性
- variety／多样性
- motion (towards a goal)／运动（朝向某一目标）

Our first exercises in strict voice-leading will be to compose good, well formed *cantus firmi*. The first step is to perform and analyze model *cantus firmi*, such as the following *cantus firmus* in C major, composed by Heinrich Schenker.

我们在严格声部进行中的第一批练习，是写作良好而结构规整的*固定旋律*。第一步是演奏并分析范例*固定旋律*，例如下面这条由海因里希·申克写作的 C 大调*固定旋律*。

A number of others are provided [here](/media/music/graphics/counterpoint/cantusFirmi.pdf). Performing these is a helpful practice to develop an internal sense of the sound and feel of a well formed *cantus*, and many of the characteristics of well formed *cantus firmi* carry over into other musical styles. (These model *cantus firmi* can also be used as the starting points for our two-voice exercises.)

另有若干范例提供在[此处](/media/music/graphics/counterpoint/cantusFirmi.pdf)。演奏这些范例是有益的练习，可以培养对结构规整的*固定旋律*之音响与感觉的内在把握，而结构规整的*固定旋律*的许多特征也会延续到其他音乐风格之中。（这些范例*固定旋律*也可以用作我们二声部练习的起点。）

From these *cantus*, notice how the general musical characteristics of smoothness, melodic integrity, variety, and motion towards a goal are worked out in specific characteristics. The following characteristics are typical of all well formed *cantus firmi*:

从这些*固定旋律*中请注意，平滑流畅、旋律完整性、多样性与朝目标运动这些一般音乐特性，是如何落实为具体特征的。以下特征是一切结构规整的*固定旋律*所典型的：

- length of about 8–16 notes
- arhythmic (all whole notes; no long or short notes)
- begin and end on *do*
- approach final tonic by step (usually *re*–*do*, sometimes *ti*–*do*)
- all note-to-note progressions are [melodic consonances](/posts/music/fundamentals/intervals/)
- range (interval between lowest and highest notes) of no more than a tenth, usually less than an octave
- a single climax (high point) that appears only once in the melody
- clear logical connection and smooth shape from beginning to climax to ending
- mostly stepwise motion, but with some leaps (mostly small leaps)
- no repetition of “motives” or “licks”
- any large leaps (fourth or larger) are followed by step in opposite direction
- no more than two leaps in a row; no consecutive leaps in the same direction (Fux’s F-major *cantus* is an exception, where the back-to-back descending leaps outline a consonant triad.)
- the leading tone progresses to the tonic
- in minor, the leading tone only appears in the penultimate bar; the raised submediant is only used when progressing to that leading tone

- length of about 8–16 notes／长度约为 8–16 个音
- arhythmic (all whole notes; no long or short notes)／无节奏（全部为全音符；没有长音或短音）
- begin and end on *do*／以 *do* 开始并结束
- approach final tonic by step (usually *re*–*do*, sometimes *ti*–*do*)／以级进接近最终的主音（通常是 *re*–*do*，有时是 *ti*–*do*）
- all note-to-note progressions are [melodic consonances](/posts/music/fundamentals/intervals/)／所有相邻音之间的进行都是[旋律协和音程](/posts/music/fundamentals/intervals/)
- range (interval between lowest and highest notes) of no more than a tenth, usually less than an octave／音域（最低音与最高音之间的音程）不超过十度，通常小于一个八度
- a single climax (high point) that appears only once in the melody／只有一个高潮（最高点），且在旋律中仅出现一次
- clear logical connection and smooth shape from beginning to climax to ending／从开始到高潮再到结束，具有清晰的逻辑联系与平顺的形态
- mostly stepwise motion, but with some leaps (mostly small leaps)／以级进为主，但有一些跳进（多为小跳）
- no repetition of “motives” or “licks”／不重复“动机”或“乐句套路”
- any large leaps (fourth or larger) are followed by step in opposite direction／任何大跳（四度或更大）之后都要朝相反方向级进
- no more than two leaps in a row; no consecutive leaps in the same direction (Fux’s F-major *cantus* is an exception, where the back-to-back descending leaps outline a consonant triad.)／连续跳进不超过两次；不得有同方向的连续跳进（富克斯的 F 大调*固定旋律*是例外，其中前后相接的下行跳进勾勒出一个协和三和弦。）
- the leading tone progresses to the tonic／导音进行到主音
- in minor, the leading tone only appears in the penultimate bar; the raised submediant is only used when progressing to that leading tone／在小调中，导音只出现在倒数第二小节；升高的小六度音级只在向该导音进行时使用

## Melodic tendencies｜旋律倾向

The characteristics listed above are fairly detailed, and some of them are specific to strict species counterpoint. However, taken together, they express in detail some general tendencies of melodies in a variety of styles.

上面列出的特征相当细致，其中一些是严格种类对位所特有的。然而，把它们合起来看，它们详细地表达了多种风格中旋律的一些一般倾向。

[David Huron](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL5851060W/Sweet_Anticipation) identifies five general properties of melodies in Western music that connect to the basic principles of perception and cognition listed above, but play out in slightly different specific ways in musical styles. They are:

[David Huron](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL5851060W/Sweet_Anticipation) 指出了西方音乐旋律的五种一般属性，它们与上文所列的感知与认知基本原理相关联，但在不同音乐风格中会以略有差异的具体方式体现。它们分别是：

- **pitch proximity** – the tendency for melodies to progress by steps more than leaps and by small leaps more than large leaps. An expression of smoothness and melodic integrity.
- **step declination** – the tendency for melodies to move by *descending* step more than ascending. Possibly an expression of goal-oriented motion, as we tend to perceive a move down as a decrease in energy (movement towards a state of rest).
- **step inertia** – the tendency for melodies to change direction less frequently than they continue in the same direction. (I.e., the majority of melodic progressions are in the same direction as the previous one.) An expression of smoothness and, at times, goal-oriented motion.
- **melodic regression** – the tendency for melodic notes in extreme registers to progress back towards the middle. An expression of motion towards a position of rest (with non-extreme notes representing “rest”). Also an expression simply of the statistical distribution of notes in a melody: the higher a note is, the more notes there are below it for a composer to choose from, and the less notes there are above it.
- **melodic arch** – the tendency for melodies to ascend in the first half of a phrase, reach a climax, and descend in the second half. An expression of goal-orientation and the rest–motion–rest pattern. Also, a combination of the above rules in the context of a musical phrase.

- **pitch proximity** – the tendency for melodies to progress by steps more than leaps and by small leaps more than large leaps. An expression of smoothness and melodic integrity.／**音高邻近性**——旋律更倾向于级进而非跳进，更倾向于小跳而非大跳。这是平滑流畅与旋律完整性的表现。
- **step declination** – the tendency for melodies to move by *descending* step more than ascending. Possibly an expression of goal-oriented motion, as we tend to perceive a move down as a decrease in energy (movement towards a state of rest).／**级进下倾**——旋律以*下行*级进移动的倾向强于上行。这可能是目标导向运动的表现，因为我们倾向于把向下移动感知为能量的降低（朝向静止状态的移动）。
- **step inertia** – the tendency for melodies to change direction less frequently than they continue in the same direction. (I.e., the majority of melodic progressions are in the same direction as the previous one.) An expression of smoothness and, at times, goal-oriented motion.／**级进惯性**——旋律改变方向的频率低于沿同一方向继续的频率。（即：大多数旋律进行与上一次进行的方向相同。）这是平滑流畅的表现，有时也是目标导向运动的表现。
- **melodic regression** – the tendency for melodic notes in extreme registers to progress back towards the middle. An expression of motion towards a position of rest (with non-extreme notes representing “rest”). Also an expression simply of the statistical distribution of notes in a melody: the higher a note is, the more notes there are below it for a composer to choose from, and the less notes there are above it.／**旋律回归**——处于极端音区的旋律音倾向于向中间回归。这是朝静止位置运动的表现（非极端音区的音即代表“静止”）。它同时也只是旋律中音高统计分布的一种表现：一个音越高，作曲家可从中选择的、位于其下方的音就越多，位于其上方的音就越少。
- **melodic arch** – the tendency for melodies to ascend in the first half of a phrase, reach a climax, and descend in the second half. An expression of goal-orientation and the rest–motion–rest pattern. Also, a combination of the above rules in the context of a musical phrase.／**旋律拱形**——旋律倾向于在乐句前半上行，达到高潮，再在后半下行。这是目标导向以及“静止—运动—静止”模式的表现。它同时也是上述各条规律在一个乐句语境中的综合。

## Practice exercise｜练习

Before composing a *cantus firmus* from scratch, try building a well formed *cantus* around the following skeleton. Length, starting pitch, penultimate pitch, ending pitch, and climax have been provided. Create a smooth, consonant melodic line that exemplifies the characteristics listed above—both the specific characteristics of strict species *cantus firmi* and the general characteristics of tonal melodies. Click the staff to hear the melody. Be sure to listen each time you make a change.

在从零开始写作一条*固定旋律*之前，试着围绕下面这个骨架构建一条结构规整的*固定旋律*。长度、起始音高、倒数第二个音、结束音高与高潮均已给出。请创作一条平滑、协和的旋律线条，体现上文所列的特征——既包括严格种类*固定旋律*的具体特征，也包括调性旋律的一般特征。点击谱面即可听到旋律。每次改动都务必聆听。
