*Basso continuo* (It. for “continuous bass” or “thoroughbass”) is essentially a chordal version of first-species counterpoint. However, instead of composing a single line above a cantus firmus, one composes a succession of chords (performed in the right hand) above a bass line (performed in the left hand). *Basso continuo* writing, also referred to as *realizing a figured bass*, gives no consideration to melody, only to the use of proper chords and the smoothest voice-leading possible. Thus, *basso continuo* style is a simple place to begin engaging the “fundamental musical problems” that arise when more than two lines are combined.

*Basso continuo*（意语，意为“连续低音”或“通奏低音”）实质上是第一类对位的和弦化版本。不过，它不是在固定旋律之上写作单一旋律线，而是在一条低音线（由左手演奏）之上写作一串和弦（由右手演奏）。*Basso continuo* 写作——亦称*实现数字低音*（realizing a figured bass）——完全不着眼于旋律，只着眼于使用恰当的和弦以及尽可能平滑的声部进行。因此，*basso continuo* 风格是一个简单的起点，用以着手处理当两条以上的线条结合时所出现的“基础音乐问题”。

## Chord voicing｜和弦声部配置

In strict keyboard-style writing, there are four voices: the bass line (which is usually a given in *basso continuo* style), and three *upper voices*: the *melody* or *soprano*, the *alto*, and the *tenor* (from highest to lowest). Since all three upper voices must be played by a single hand, they should never span more than an octave.

在严格的键盘织体写作中，共有四个声部：低音线（在 *basso continuo* 风格中通常是给定不变的），以及三个*上方声部*：*旋律*或*女高音*、*女低音*与*男高音*（由高到低）。由于三个上方声部必须由单手演奏，它们的跨度绝不应超过一个八度。

The melody always has an upward-pointing stem. Alto and tenor share a downward-pointing stem. If the alto and tenor share a note, that note receives a single downward-pointing stem. (See m. 1 of the example below.) If melody and alto share a note, that notehead is double-stemmed. (See m. 4 of the example below.)

旋律的符干总是朝上。女低音与男高音共用朝下的符干。若女低音与男高音共用一个音，该音只写一个朝下的符干。（见下例第 1 小节。）若旋律与女低音共用一个音，该音符头带两个符干。（见下例第 4 小节。）

When choosing the notes to place in the upper voices above a figured bass, use the bass and figures to determine the pitch classes present in the chord. (When realizing an *unfigured bass*, you must determine appropriate figures before realizing.) If the chord is a four-note chord, use each chord member once, including the bass (exceptions will be noted later). If a chord has three pitch-classes (a triad, for instance), use each pitch-class once, and “double” one of them according to the following principles:

在为数字低音上方的上方声部选择音时，要用低音与记号来确定和弦中所含的音级。（在实现*无数字低音*时，必须先确定恰当的记号，然后再实现。）若和弦是四音和弦，则每个和弦成员各用一次，其中包括低音（例外情况后文说明）。若和弦含有三个音级（例如三和弦），则每个音级各用一次，并按以下原则“重复”其中之一：

- If the figure is 6/4, 5/3, or other chord of the fifth, double the bass pitch class.／若记号是 6/4、5/3 或其他五度和弦，则重复低音音级。
- If the figure is 6/3 and the bass is a *fixed scale degree* (*do*, *re*, *fa*, or *sol*), double the bass pitch class.／若记号是 6/3 且低音是*固定音级*（*do*、*re*、*fa* 或 *sol*），则重复低音音级。
- If the figure is 6/3 and the bass is a *variable scale degree* (*mi*/*me*, *la*/*le*, or *ti*/*te*) or a chromatically altered pitch, double one of the upper voices at the octave or unison.／若记号是 6/3 且低音是*可变音级*（*mi*/*me*、*la*/*le* 或 *ti*/*te*）或经过半音变化的音高，则在高八度或同度上重复某个上方声部。
- Generally, do not double a variable scale degree or a chromatically altered pitch.／一般来说，不要重复可变音级或经过半音变化的音高。

In *basso continuo* style, if the chord is properly voiced (correct pitch classes and correct doublings), two key principles of voice-leading will ensure good counterpoint between the voices most of the time:

在 *basso continuo* 风格中，若和弦的声部配置恰当（音级正确、重复音正确），那么两条关键的声部进行原则在大多数情况下都能确保各声部之间具有良好的对位：

- *The law of the shortest way* (a term coined by composer Arnold Schoenberg): move each voice as little as possible. Prefer repetition to steps, steps to leaps, and one leap at a time to several voices leaping at the same time.／*最短路径法则*（由作曲家阿诺德·勋伯格创用的术语）：让每个声部尽可能少动。宁取重复而不取级进，宁取级进而不可跳跃，宁取一次只有一个声部跳跃而不可多个声部同时跳跃。
- *Move the right hand in contrary or oblique motion to the bass.* When the bass leaps by fourth or fifth, though, this rule can be ignored.／*使右手相对低音作反向或斜向进行。*不过，当低音作四度或五度跳进时，此规则可以忽略。

In some cases, these rules cannot be followed absolutely (such as when a functional dissonance must be resolved, or when a melody makes it impossible—two cases to be considered later). In all cases, observe the following:

在某些情况下，这些规则无法被绝对遵循（例如当功能不协和音必须解决时，或当旋律使规则无法践行之时——这两种情形后文再议）。在任何情况下，都要遵守以下各点：

- No parallel fifths or octaves between any pair of voices.／任何一对声部之间都不得出现平行五度或平行八度。
- No contrary fifths or octaves between outer voices.／外声部之间不得出现反向五度或反向八度。
- Do not approach an octave between the outer voices by similar motion unless the melody moves by step. (All other direct/hidden fifths and octaves are permissible.)／不得以同向进行接近外声部之间的八度，除非旋律作级进。（其他所有直接/隐伏五度与八度均允许。）
