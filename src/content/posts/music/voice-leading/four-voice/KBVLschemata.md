*Keyboard-style voice-leading schemata*

Following are a number of *schemata* for keyboard-style voice-leading. Some of these are patterns that are common enough to warrant special attention (and memorization). Others require non-default voice-leading or doublings. In any case, these are worth memorizing to assist both in composition/arranging and in recognition of standard patterns. (Some will also come in handy for chorale-style voice-leading.)

以下是若干用于键盘织体声部进行的*程式*（schemata）。其中一些模式常见到值得特别留意（并记诵）。另一些则要求非默认的声部进行或重复音。无论如何，这些都值得记诵，以便在写作/编配以及辨识标准模式时有所助益。（其中一些在众赞歌织体的声部进行中也同样有用。）

## Voice exchange｜声部交换

A *voice exchange* occurs when the melody and bass lines exchange pitches over the course of a simple contrapuntal prolongation. For example, in the progression **T1 D2 T3**, the bass begins on *do* and ends on *mi*. In a voice exchange, the melody would reverse this, starting on *mi* and ending on *do*. This common pattern can use a **V6/4**, **V4/3**, or a **VII6/3** chord for **D2**:

*声部交换*出现在旋律与低音线在一个简单的对位延展过程中互换音高之时。例如，在进行 **T1 D2 T3** 中，低音始于 *do* 而终于 *mi*。在声部交换中，旋律则反其道而行，始于 *mi* 而终于 *do*。这一常见模式可为 **D2** 使用 **V6/4**、**V4/3** 或 **VII6/3** 和弦：

![声部交换（V6/4）](/media/music/graphics/harmony/VE64.png)

![声部交换（VII6/3）](/media/music/graphics/harmony/VE63.png)

Using this voice exchange pattern will ensure smooth voice-leading throughout the prolongation.

使用这一声部交换模式，将确保整个延展过程中的声部进行平滑流畅。

Note that the *fa* in the **D2** passing chord (a functional dissonance) of the second example does *not* resolve down to *mi*. This is permissible because *mi* is required of the bass line but cannot be doubled, and because the smooth outer-voice counterpoint and stepwise inner-voice motion counteract any harshness perceived by the unresolved dissonance.

请注意，第二例中 **D2** 经过和弦里的 *fa*（一个功能性不协和音）*并未*下行解决到 *mi*。这是允许的，因为低音线需要 *mi* 而又不能重复它，并且平滑的外声部对位与级进的内声部运动抵消了由未解决不协和音所可能感知到的任何生硬感。

The voice exchange can also be used with a **D4** substitution chord. Note that the upper voices will be exactly the same as using a **V6/4** for **D2**.

声部交换也可以配以 **D4** 替代和弦使用。请注意，其上方声部将与为 **D2** 使用 **V6/4** 时完全相同。

![D4 替代和弦](/media/music/graphics/harmony/D4i.png)

These patterns can be used in major or minor, transposed to any key, and the **D2** voice exchanges can be used in reverse, as well—**T3 D2 T1**. They can also be used to prolong S and D: **S4 T5 S6** or **D5 S6 D7**, for example.

这些模式可用于大调或小调，也可移调到任何调，而 **D2** 的声部交换还可反向使用——**T3 D2 T1**。它们也可用于延展 S 与 D：例如 **S4 T5 S6** 或 **D5 S6 D7**。

## Parallel tenths｜平行十度

*Do–re–mi* in the bass is also frequently accompanied by *mi–fa–sol* in the melody, making *parallel tenths*.

低音中的 *do–re–mi* 也常配以旋律中的 *mi–fa–sol*，从而构成*平行十度*。

![平行十度](/media/music/graphics/harmony/ParallelTenths.png)

Note here that, like the *fa* in the **D2** voice exchange, the *fa* in the melody is an unresolved functional dissonance. In this case, the voice leading once again is so smooth that it overrides the need for the functional dissonance to resolve.

请注意，此处的 *fa* 与 **D2** 声部交换中的 *fa* 一样，是旋律中一个未解决的功能性不协和音。在此情形下，声部进行又一次平滑到足以压过功能性不协和音需要解决的要求。

This pattern can also be used in major or minor, transposed to any key, and in reverse. It almost always uses a **V4/3** for **D2**.

这一模式也可用于大调或小调、可移调到任何调，也可反向使用。它几乎总是为 **D2** 使用 **V4/3**。

## Champagne progression｜香槟进行

While **T(1 D2p 3)** is a perfectly acceptable way to accompany *mi–fa–sol* in the melody, a more interesting (and also more involved) way to harmonize that melody is what theorist Gene Biringer called the *champagne progression*: **T1 S6 T3**. (He called it this because it is “the progression you use when you want to impress a date.”)

虽然 **T(1 D2p 3)** 是为旋律中的 *mi–fa–sol* 配伴奏的完全可接受的方式，但为那条旋律配和声还有一种更有趣（也更复杂）的方式，即理论家 Gene Biringer 所称的*香槟进行*：**T1 S6 T3**。（他如此命名，是因为它是“你想在约会对象面前露一手时所用的进行”。）

The champagne progression is very nice, but must be treated carefully. *Only* use it with *mi–fa–sol* (or *me–fa–sol*) in the melody, and *always* use the following voice-leading (note the non-standard doubling of the bass—*la*/*le*—in the **S6** chord).

香槟进行非常动听，但必须谨慎处理。*只*在旋律为 *mi–fa–sol*（或 *me–fa–sol*）时使用它，并且*始终*采用如下的声部进行（注意 **S6** 和弦中对低音的非标准重复——*la*/*le*）。

![香槟进行](/media/music/graphics/harmony/Champagne.png)

The standard champagne progression (above) uses a **I6** chord for **T3**. Following is a variant using **III** for **T3**, which Biringer dubbed “pink champagne,” because it is especially nice. It should also be especially *rare*, or it loses its punch.

标准的香槟进行（上图）为 **T3** 使用 **I6** 和弦。以下是一种为 **T3** 使用 **III** 的变体，Biringer 称之为“粉红香槟”，因为它格外动听。但它也应格外*罕见*，否则便会失去力度。

![粉红香槟进行](/media/music/graphics/harmony/PinkChampagne.png)

The champagne progression should only be used to prolong tonic function.

香槟进行只应用于延展主功能。

## Deceptive resolution｜欺骗性解决

A *deceptive resolution* occurs When a **D5** (**V** or **V7**) chord does not progress, as expected, to **I** to form an authentic cadence, but instead progresses to **VI**. In the deceptive resolution, it is important for *ti* to resolve to *do*—as it would in an authentic cadence—not *la*/*le*. This fulfills its role as a tendency tone, helps the “deception” to work, and avoids the dissonant augmented second *ti*–*le* in minor. This results in a non-standard doubling of VI (*do*, rather than the bass).

*欺骗性解决*出现在 **D5**（**V** 或 **V7**）和弦不按预期进行到 **I** 以构成正格终止，而是进行到 **VI** 之时。在欺骗性解决中，重要的是 *ti* 要解决到 *do*——如其在正格终止中那样——而不是 *la*/*le*。这既履行了它作为倾向音的角色，又有助于“欺骗”效果得以成立，还避免了小调中不协和的增二度 *ti*–*le*。其结果是对 VI 的非标准重复（重复 *do*，而非低音）。

![欺骗性解决](/media/music/graphics/harmony/deceptive.png)

![欺骗性解决（V7）](/media/music/graphics/harmony/deceptive7.png)

## Leaving out the fifth｜省略五音

When *ti*–*do* appears in the melody of an authentic cadence involving **V7**, it is impossible to fully voice both chords *and* resolve the functional dissonance.

当 *ti*–*do* 出现在涉及 **V7** 的正格终止旋律中时，既要完整地配置两个和弦的声部，*又*要解决功能性不协和音，是不可能的。

![V7 的难题](/media/music/graphics/harmony/D5problem.png)

It is imperative to resolve this functional dissonance, as that resolution is an important contributor to the goal-oriented motion and the releasing of harmonic tension into the cadential arrival. In order to do so, either leave out the fifth of the **I** chord (and triple the bass):

必须解决这一功能性不协和音，因为该解决对于有目标指向的运动、以及把和声张力释放到终止到达点上，都是重要的贡献者。为做到这一点，或者省略 **I** 和弦的五音（并把低音重复三次）：

![省略 I 的五音](/media/music/graphics/harmony/T1dropFifth.png)

or leave out the fifth of the **V7** chord, and double the bass:

或者省略 **V7** 和弦的五音，并重复低音：

![省略 V7 的五音](/media/music/graphics/harmony/D5dropFifth.png)

The incomplete **V7** is preferable to the incomplete **I**, but voice-leading into the **V7** will usually dictate which option you choose.

不完整的 **V7** 比不完整的 **I** 更可取，但进入 **V7** 的声部进行通常会决定你选择哪一种。

## Leaving out the third｜省略三音

When an **S4** progresses into a **D5**, and both are *seventh chords*, it can be impossible to prepare and resolve the functional dissonances of both chords while fully voicing them.

当 **S4** 进行到 **D5**，且二者皆为*七和弦*时，可能在完整配置两个和弦声部的同时，又要准备并解决二者的功能性不协和音，这是做不到的。

![S4 的难题](/media/music/graphics/harmony/S4problem.png)

Instead, leave *la*/*le* out of the **S4** chord and double the bass. This will retain the trigger and bass note *fa*, as well as the two pitches making the dissonance (*do* and *re* or *mi*/*me* and *fa*), and will allow a second *fa* in an upper voice that can prepare the seventh of the **D5** chord.

取而代之的做法是：将 *la*/*le* 从 **S4** 和弦中省略，并重复低音。这样便保留了触发音兼低音 *fa*，以及构成不协和的两个音高（*do* 与 *re*，或 *mi*/*me* 与 *fa*），并允许在上方声部中再出现一个 *fa*，用以准备 **D5** 和弦的七音。

![省略 S4 的三音](/media/music/graphics/harmony/S4dropThird.png)
