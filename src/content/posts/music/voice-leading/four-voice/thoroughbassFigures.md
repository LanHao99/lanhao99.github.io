A *thoroughbass* (It. *Basso continuo*, Ger. *Generalbaß*, also called a *figured bass*) is a harmonic shorthand of a musical passage or work. It is composed of a bass line, and chord symbols—called *figures*. It is called a “thorough” bass or “continuous” bass line because it includes the lowest sounding note at any given moment, *regardless of the instrument or voice sounding the note*. It usually corresponds to a single instrument or vocal part, but not always.

*Thoroughbass*（意 *Basso continuo*，德 *Generalbaß*，亦称 *figured bass* 数字低音）是某段音乐或某部作品的和声速记法。它由一条低音线以及称为 *figures*（记号）的和弦符号组成。之所以称为“thorough”低音或“continuous”低音线，是因为它在任何时刻都包含实际发声的最低音，*无论该音由何种乐器或人声唱奏*。它通常对应单一乐器或声乐声部，但并非总是如此。

[![巴赫 BWV 1033 谱例](/media/music/graphics/Bach1033.png)](/media/music/graphics/Bach1033.png)

*J.S. Bach, Flute Sonata in C Major, ii., BWV 1033. The upper part is played by the flute, the lower part is the* basso continuo *line, played by a keyboardist who uses the numbers below the staff (figures) to guide the chords played above this bass line.*

*J.S. 巴赫《C 大调长笛奏鸣曲》第二乐章，BWV 1033。上方声部由长笛演奏，下方声部为* basso continuo *（通奏低音）线，由键盘演奏者奏出，他依据谱表下方的数字（记号）来指导在该低音线上方所奏的和弦。*

The historical origin of the thoroughbass part was in church settings where a piece for 6–8 singers was to be performed by one or two voices with a keyboard instrument. The keyboardist, rather than play the 4–7 remaining parts, would transcribe the lowest note and shorthand figures to indicate the (simple) intervals present above that lowest voice. This would allow the keyboardist to play one or two of the more important lines, and fill the rest of the texture with blocked or arpeggiated chords. (Think seventeenth-century lead sheet.) A good keyboardist, who knew their harmony and voice-leading, could simply follow the bass line without figures (an *unfigured bass*) and listen to the melody, improvising the rest. Less experienced keyboardists, however, could manage otherwise complicated pieces by reading a bass line and memorizing a small number of figures and basic voice-leading rules. (You can read a more detailed explanation of its history [here｜此处](/posts/music/voice-leading/four-voice/bassoContinuo-history/).)

通奏低音声部的历史渊源见于教会场合：为 6–8 位歌者而写的作品，实际仅由一两个人声配以一件键盘乐器演出。键盘演奏者并不弹奏余下的 4–7 个声部，而是记下最低音，并用速记记号标示该最低声部上方所出现的（简单）音程。这样键盘演奏者便可奏出其中一两个较重要的线条，并以柱式和弦或琶音和弦填充织体的其余部分。（可参想十七世纪的“主旋律谱”。）一位深谙和声与声部进行的好键盘手，完全可以只跟低音线而不看记号（即 *unfigured bass* 无数字低音），同时聆听旋律，其余部分即兴发挥。然而经验较浅的键盘手，只要读懂低音线并记住少量记号与基本声部进行规则，也能应付原本复杂的作品。（关于其历史更详细的说明，可读[here｜此处](/posts/music/voice-leading/four-voice/bassoContinuo-history/)。）

Coming after species counterpoint in our studies, *basso continuo* exercises provide a new, more complicated environment in which to practice mediating the demands of smoothness, independence of lines, tonal fusion (now considering triads and seventh chords), variety, and motion. New considerations of performability are introduced, and the presence of dissonances within the core harmonies themselves will call for new approaches to harmonic dissonance in this style.

在我们的学习次序中，*basso continuo*（通奏低音）练习紧接分类对位之后，它提供了一个新的、更复杂的环境，用以练习调和以下各项要求：流畅、线条独立、调性融合（此时要考虑三和弦与七和弦）、变化以及运动。这里引入了关于可演奏性的新考量，而核心和声内部本身即存在不协和音，这将要求用新的方式处理此风格中的和声不协和。

We will use thoroughbass lines for a number of purposes in this book:

在本书中，我们会在若干方面使用通奏低音线：

- harmonic “reductions” of pieces and passages with dense textures or complicated voice-leading／对织体密集或声部进行复杂的作品与段落作和声“缩编”
- shorthand representations of stock harmonic patterns／对常用和声模式的速记式表示
- the harmonic basis for model composition exercises (akin to the *cantus firmus* of species counterpoint)／为范例写作练习提供和声基础（类似于分类对位中的 *cantus firmus* 固定旋律）

Thoroughbass is a simple, and foundational, concept. Master it early, and subsequent activities will be much easier.

通奏低音是一个简单而基础的概念。尽早掌握它，后续的活动便会容易得多。

Note on figure placement: Thoroughbass figures can appear above or below the bass line. Both are common, but in this book, we generally place them *above* the bass line. This connects them to our habits of interval analysis during species counterpoint, keeps figures separate from other harmonic symbols we will place below the bass line, and make typesetting in notation software easier when both figures and other symbols are in play simultaneously.

关于记号位置的说明：通奏低音记号既可写在低音线的上方，也可写在下方。两者都常见，但在本书中，我们一般把它们写在低音线的*上方*。这样可将它们与我们在分类对位中分析音程的习惯联系起来，使记号与我们放在低音线下方的其他和声符号相区分，并且在记号与其他符号同时出现时，便于在记谱软件中排版。

## Figures｜记号

In general, a thoroughbass figure indicates the *simple intervals above the bass* for all pitch classes present in the chord.

一般来说，通奏低音记号标示出和弦中所有音级相对于低音的*简单音程*。

The largest number typically found in thoroughbass figures is 7. In general, *compound intervals* (an octave or larger) are reduced to their *simple interval* equivalent. A tenth becomes a third, a thirteenth becomes a sixth, etc.

通奏低音记号中通常可见的最大数字为 7。一般来说，*复音程*（八度或更大）会化简为与之等值的*简单音程*。十度化为三度，十三度化为六度，依此类推。

The most common chords in tonal music are [*triads* and *seventh chords*｜*三和弦*与*七和弦*](/posts/music/fundamentals/triads/). The following figures apply to these chords:

调性音乐中最常见的和弦是[*triads* and *seventh chords*｜*三和弦*与*七和弦*](/posts/music/fundamentals/triads/)。下列记号即适用于这些和弦：

![数字低音记号全表](/media/music/graphics/harmony/figuresFull.png)

- 5/3: use a fifth and a third above the bass (one note of the chord will be doubled)／5/3：在低音上方使用五度与三度（和弦中的一个音将被重复）
- 6/3: use a sixth and a third above the bass (one note of the chord will be doubled)／6/3：在低音上方使用六度与三度（和弦中的一个音将被重复）
- 6/4: use a sixth and a fourth above the bass (one note of the chord will be doubled)／6/4：在低音上方使用六度与四度（和弦中的一个音将被重复）
- 7/5/3: use a seventh, a fifth, and a third above the bass／7/5/3：在低音上方使用七度、五度与三度
- 6/5/3: use a sixth, a fifth, and a third above the bass／6/5/3：在低音上方使用六度、五度与三度
- 6/4/3: use a sixth, a fourth, and a third above the bass／6/4/3：在低音上方使用六度、四度与三度
- 6/4/2: a use a sixth, a fourth, and a second above the bass／6/4/2：在低音上方使用六度、四度与二度

These figures are so common, that most of them have shortcuts:

这些记号极为常见，因此其中大多数都有简写：

![数字低音记号缩写](/media/music/graphics/harmony/figuresAbbrev.png)

- no figure = 5/3／无记号 = 5/3
- 6 = 6/3／6 = 6/3
- 6/4 is never abbreviated／6/4 从不简写
- 7 = 7/5/3／7 = 7/5/3
- 6/5 = 6/5/3／6/5 = 6/5/3
- 4/3 = 6/4/3／4/3 = 6/4/3
- 4/2 (or just 2) = 6/4/2／4/2（或仅作 2）= 6/4/2

Other shortcuts generally follow two simple rules:

其他简写一般遵循两条简单规则：

- Assume a fifth is present above the bass unless there is a “6” in the figure.／除非记号中出现“6”，否则假定低音上方有五度。
- Assume a third is present above the bass unless there is a “4” or a “2” in the figure.／除非记号中出现“4”或“2”，否则假定低音上方有三度。

## Unfamiliar figures and chords｜不熟悉的记号与和弦

Only seven figures are given above. If you see a figure you do not recognize, simply follow the intervals (using the two shortcut rules). Likewise, if analyzing a chord that is not a triad or seventh chord, simply label the *simple* intervals you see/hear above the bass, from top to bottom in descending order: 7/6/3 or 5/4, for example. In time, you will become familiar with a number of other harmonic possibilities, and their corresponding figures.

上文只给出了七个记号。若遇到不认识的记号，只需按音程来推求（运用那两条简写规则）。同样，若要分析一个并非三和弦或七和弦的和弦，只需把你在低音上方看到/听到的*简单*音程自上而下按降序标出，例如 7/6/3 或 5/4。假以时日，你会熟悉若干其他和声可能性及其对应的记号。

## Chords of the fifth and chords of the sixth｜五度和弦与六度和弦

All chords can be categorized as either a *chord of the fifth* or a *chord of the sixth*. This distinction will be important for our study of voice-leading.

所有和弦都可归为*五度和弦*或*六度和弦*。这一区分对我们的声部进行研究将很重要。

A *chord of the fifth* contains a fifth above the bass, but no sixth above the bass.

*五度和弦*在低音上方含有五度，但不含六度。

A *chord of the sixth* contains a sixth above the bass.

*六度和弦*在低音上方含有六度。

## Chromatic alteration｜半音变化

If a note is chromatically altered (different than the key signature), the figure must be altered as well. Since bass notes are already present in the bass, a chromatic alteration in the bass will not make it into the figure. However, any other alteration in the upper voices (such as a raised leading tone in minor) must be reflected in the figure. To do so, simply put a sharp, flat, or natural to the left of the appropriate number.

若某音被半音变化（与调号不同），记号也必须随之变化。由于低音音高本就已在低音线上出现，低音上的半音变化不会进入记号。然而，上方声部中的任何其他变化（如小调中升高的导音）都必须在记号中体现。做法很简单：在相应数字的左侧加上升号、降号或还原号即可。

Of course, there are some shortcuts. For example, draw a line (a “slash”) through a number to denote that it is raised by half-step (can substitute both for sharp or for natural). Also, when altering the third above the bass, simply use the sharp, flat, or natural and leave out the “3.”

当然，也有一些简写。例如，在数字上画一道斜线（“slash”）表示该音升高半音（可同时替代升号与还原号）。此外，当要变化低音上方的三度时，直接写升号、降号或还原号而省去“3”即可。

![半音变化的数字低音记号](/media/music/graphics/harmony/figuresChrom.png)

In general, if there is a shortcut available, use it. The shortcuts are more standard than the corresponding full notation.

一般来说，若有简写可用，就用简写。这些简写比相应的完整写法更为标准。

Keep in mind that some chords have abbreviated figures. For example, it is common for the leading tone to be the third above the bass in a 5/3 chord. In such a situation, a bass note that otherwise would have no figure needs a sharp or a natural for its thoroughbass figure.

请记住，有些和弦使用缩写记号。例如，在 5/3 和弦中，导音常常正是低音上方的三度。在这种情况下，一个本可完全不带记号的低音音高，就需要为其通奏低音记号添上升号或还原号。
