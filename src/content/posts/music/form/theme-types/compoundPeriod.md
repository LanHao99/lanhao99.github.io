The compound period (also called the *16-bar period* because its typical form is 16 bars long) is made of *two themes* instead of *two phrases*. Like an [8-bar period](/posts/music/form/theme-types/period/), the two halves of the compound period exhibit [antecedent](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/#antecedent) and [consequent](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/#consequent) function. To distinguish them, we will call these “large antecedents” and “large consequents.”

复合乐段（也称*十六小节乐段*，因其典型形式长十六小节）由*两个主题*而非*两个乐句*构成。与[八小节乐段](/posts/music/form/theme-types/period/)一样，复合乐段的两半分别表现[前句](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/#antecedent)与[后句](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/#consequent)功能。为加以区分，我们将它们称为“大前句”与“大后句”。

The thematic types available to the two halves of the compound period must be conducive to the functions of antecedent and consequent. Thus,

复合乐段的两半所能采用的各主题类型，必须有助于实现前句与后句的功能。因此，

- the initiating phrase of the *large antecedent*, usually four bars long, will act as a basic idea - either presentation, antecedent, or compound basic idea;／*大前句*的开启乐句（通常长四小节）将充当基本乐思——或为呈示，或为前句，或为复合基本乐思；
- the concluding half, also typically four bars long, will act as a contrasting idea and will always be a continuation;／结束的一半（同样通常长四小节）将充当对比乐思，并且总是继续；
- the *large consequent* should return the BI from the first theme (often varied), and its concluding CI will end with a strong cadence.／*大后句*应让第一主题的基本乐思回归（常常有所变化），其结尾的对比乐思将以强终止式结束。

These functional requirements tend to result in three types of compound period.

这些功能要求往往导致三种类型的复合乐段。

## A Compound Period Comprised of Two Sentences｜由两个乐句式构成的复合乐段

Both sentences in the compound period will have the same basic idea, each exhibiting presentation function. In the first sentence, the presentation is followed by a continuation that ends with a weak cadence. In the second, the continuation culminates in a strong cadence, most commonly a PAC.

复合乐段中的两个乐句式具有相同的基本乐思，各自表现呈示功能。在第一个乐句式中，呈示之后跟着一个以弱终止式结束的继续。在第二个中，继续达到终点，形成强终止式，最常见的是 PAC。

![莫扎特 D 大调钢琴奏鸣曲 K. 284 第二乐章谱例](/media/music/graphics/form/k284-ii.png)

*Compound Period (Sent. + Sent.): Mozart, Piano Sonata in D major, K. 284, II, mm. 1–8*

*复合乐段（乐句式 + 乐句式）：莫扎特《D 大调钢琴奏鸣曲》K. 284 第二乐章，第 1–8 小节*

The large antecedent is constructed as a sentence whose basic idea is a presentation and whose contrasting idea is a continuation. Following the HC in m. 8, the next phrase restates the presentational basic idea in varied form. The final four measures culminate in a PAC.

大前句被构建为一个乐句式：其基本乐思是呈示，其对比乐思是继续。第 8 小节的 HC 之后，下一乐句以变化的形式再次陈述呈示性的基本乐思。最后四小节达到终点，形成 PAC。

## A Compound Period Comprised of Two Hybrids｜由两个混合主题构成的复合乐段

If the initiating phrase is composed of an antecedent, rather than a presentation, the 8-bar antecedent phrase will be a hybrid of the [“antecedent + continuation”](/posts/music/form/theme-types/hybridThemes/#hybrid-1) variety.

若开启乐句由前句而非呈示构成，则该八小节的前句将是[“前句 + 继续”](/posts/music/form/theme-types/hybridThemes/#hybrid-1)这一类型的混合主题。

![由两个混合主题 1 构成的十六小节乐段结构图](/media/music/graphics/ClassicalThemes/16period-hybrid1.svg)

![莫扎特 F 大调钢琴奏鸣曲 K. 332 谱例](/media/music/graphics/form/k332.png)

*Compound Period (Hybrid 1 + Hybrid 1): Mozart, Piano Sonata in F major, K. 332, I, mm. 41–56*

*复合乐段（混合主题 1 + 混合主题 1）：莫扎特《F 大调钢琴奏鸣曲》K. 332 第一乐章，第 41–56 小节*

In this example the large antecedent begins with a small antecedent, comprising mm. 41-44, that acts as a basic idea. In the large consequent, this basic idea returns (mm. 49-52) in varied form.

在此例中，大前句以小前句开始（第 41–44 小节），后者充当基本乐思。在大后句中，该基本乐思以变化的形式回归（第 49–52 小节）。

By contrast, if the initiating phrase is composed of a [“compound basic idea”](/posts/music/form/theme-types/hybridThemes/#the-compound-basic-idea), the large antecedent will be a type-3 hybrid: compound basic idea + continuation. This type of compound period is *very similar* to the previous variety. The only distinction is that the initiating phrase will not culminate in a cadence, thus giving the theme’s initiation a looser quality.

相反，若开启乐句由[“复合基本乐思”](/posts/music/form/theme-types/hybridThemes/#the-compound-basic-idea)构成，则大前句将是第 3 类混合主题：复合基本乐思 + 继续。这一类型的复合乐段与前一类型*十分相似*。唯一区别在于开启乐句不以终止式达到终点，从而使主题的开启具有较松散的性质。
