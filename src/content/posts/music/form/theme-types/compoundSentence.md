The compound (or 16-bar) sentence expands both the presentation and continuation phrases of a regular [sentence](/posts/music/form/theme-types/sentence/) to a length of eight bars each.

复合乐句式（或称十六小节乐句式）将常规[乐句式](/posts/music/form/theme-types/sentence/)的呈示乐句与继续乐句各自扩展到八小节的长度。

Each 2-bar basic idea is replaced by a 4-bar [compound basic idea](/posts/music/form/theme-types/hybridThemes/#the-compound-basic-idea) in the expanded presentation.

在扩展的呈示中，每个两小节的基本乐思都被一个四小节的[复合基本乐思](/posts/music/form/theme-types/hybridThemes/#the-compound-basic-idea)取代。

A “typical” continuation would have approximately four bars of continuation function followed by four bars of cadential function, but in a compound sentence, this varies greatly. The continuation is commonly shortened or [expanded](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/internalExpansions/).

“典型”的继续大约会有四小节的继续功能，随后是四小节的终止功能，但在复合乐句式中，这种情况变化很大。继续常被缩短或[扩展](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/internalExpansions/)。

![复合乐句式结构图](/media/music/graphics/ClassicalThemes/16sentence.svg)

The eight bars that comprise the continuation of the main theme below are constructed from two compound basic ideas, the first beginning on tonic and moving to dominant (mm. 1-4), and the second beginning with dominant harmony and moving back to tonic (mm. 5-8).

下列主主题的继续由八小节构成，它们由两个复合基本乐思搭建而成：第一个从主音开始并走向属音（第 1–4 小节），第二个从属和声开始并返回主音（第 5–8 小节）。

Initially, the continuation seems as if it will end after only 4 bars, in m. 12. But the clarinet is missing at the the downbeat of m. 12, and instead begins a link to a [four-bar repetition](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/internalExpansions/#phrase-expansion). In this repetition (mm. 13-16), the clarinet melody from mm. 9–12 is played by the piano, who cadences clearly with a PAC at the downbeat of m. 16.

起初，继续看上去只会在 4 小节后、即第 12 小节结束。但单簧管在第 12 小节强拍上并未出现，转而开始一段通向[四小节重复](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/internalExpansions/#phrase-expansion)的连接。在这次重复中（第 13–16 小节），第 9–12 小节的单簧管旋律由钢琴演奏，钢琴在第 16 小节强拍上以 PAC 明确收束。

![莫扎特 K. 498 三重奏谱例](/media/music/graphics/form/k498.png)

*Compound Sentence: Mozart, Trio in E-flat major for Clarinet, Viola, and Piano, K. 498, I, mm. 1-16*

*复合乐句式：莫扎特《降 E 大调单簧管、中提琴与钢琴三重奏》K. 498 第一乐章，第 1–16 小节*
