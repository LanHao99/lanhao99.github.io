*The period*

A period is one type of theme, like the [sentence](/posts/music/form/theme-types/sentence/), common to the Classical style.

乐段式是一种主题类型，与[乐句式](/posts/music/form/theme-types/sentence/)一样，在古典风格中十分常见。

The period is generally eight measures long and contains two four-measure phrases, called *antecedent* and *consequent*.

乐段式通常长八小节，包含两个四小节的乐句，分别称为*前句*与*后句*。

![乐段式谱例](/media/music/graphics/ClassicalThemes/period.svg)

The period is characterized by balance and symmetry. Its antecedent phrase is initiated by a basic idea that recurs at the beginning of the consequent phrase. Unlike the sentence, which exhibits a single cadence, the period contains *two cadences*, a weak one to end the *antecedent* and a strong one to end the *consequent*.

乐段式的特征是平衡与对称。其前句由一个基本乐思开启，该乐思在后句开头再次出现。与只呈现一个终止式的乐句式不同，乐段式包含*两个终止式*：一个较弱的终止式结束*前句*，一个较强的终止式结束*后句*。

## Antecedent phrase (mm. 1–4)｜前句（第 1–4 小节）

Unlike the sentence, which contains a basic idea followed by a repetition, the two measure basic idea that begins the a period’s antecedent is always followed by a two-measure *contrasting idea* (CI). That contrasting idea supports a cadential progression that ends the antecedent with a weak cadence, either a HC or an IAC.

乐句式包含一个基本乐思及紧随其后的重复，乐段式则不同：开启乐段式前句的两小节基本乐思之后，总是跟着一个两小节的*对比乐思*（CI）。该对比乐思支撑一个终止进行，以前句的弱终止式收束，或为 HC，或为 IAC。

![前句谱例](/media/music/graphics/form/antecedent.png)

*Antecedent: Haydn, Piano Trio in C major, Hob. XV:27, III, mm. 1-4*

*前句：海顿《C 大调钢琴三重奏》Hob. XV:27 第三乐章，第 1–4 小节*

<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/1BUSIhYdu0P8LBWXLpAx4g" width="100%" height="152" frameborder="0" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

Note the contrast created between the basic idea and contrasting idea. While the BI ascends, outlining the tonic triad with leaps to each of its members, the CI descends stepwise leading to a weak I:HC. The emphasis on tonic in the melody of the BI is accompanied by a tonic prolongation in the harmony (a variant of the [*Romanesca* schema](/posts/music/form/galant-schemata/schemataOpensAndCloses/)):

请注意基本乐思与对比乐思之间形成的对比。基本乐思上行，以跳进逐一勾画出主三和弦各音；对比乐思则级进下行，导向弱的 I:HC。基本乐思旋律对主音的强调，在和声上伴随主音的延长（[*Romanesca* 图式](/posts/music/form/galant-schemata/schemataOpensAndCloses/)的一种变体）：

> **I V6 VI III**
> or
> **T(1 D7p x6 3)**

Supporting the CI is an expanded cadential progression:

支撑对比乐思的是一个扩展的终止进行：

> **III IV V6/5/V V**
> or
> **T3 S(4 [+]) D5**

## Consequent phrase (mm. 5–8)｜后句（第 5–8 小节）

Consequent phrases always begin with a restatement of the BI, occasionally varied, and end with a CI. A consequent phrase’s CI often resembles the antecedent’s, but slightly altered to accommodate a stronger cadence. It is also common for a consequent phrase’s CI to be entirely new. While a sentence can close with a number of cadence types, the period’s consequent phrase always ends with a PAC:

后句总是以基本乐思的再次陈述开头（偶尔有所变化），并以一个对比乐思结束。后句的对比乐思常常与前句的对比乐思相似，但为容纳更强的终止式而稍作改动。后句的对比乐思完全是新的，也很常见。乐句式可以用多种终止式类型收束，而乐段式的后句则总是以 PAC 结束：

![海顿 C 大调钢琴三重奏 Hob. XV:27 谱例](/media/music/graphics/form/xv27.png)

*Antecedent + Consequent: Haydn, Piano Trio in C major, Hob. XV:27, III, mm. 1-8*

*前句 + 后句：海顿《C 大调钢琴三重奏》Hob. XV:27 第三乐章，第 1–8 小节*

In this example, the BI is restated exactly at the beginning of the consequent. The concluding CI is a slight variation of the end of the antecedent, altered here to create a PAC.

在此例中，基本乐思在后句开头被原样再次陈述。结尾的对比乐思是前句结尾的轻微变奏，此处经过改动以形成 PAC。
