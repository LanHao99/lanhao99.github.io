*The Small Ternary*

Like the smaller [sentence](/posts/music/form/theme-types/sentence/), [period](/posts/music/form/theme-types/period/), and [hybrid](/posts/music/form/theme-types/hybridThemes/) themes, the small ternary can act alone as the theme of a full-movement form. The small ternary is larger, however–constructed from themes just as the smaller theme types were constructed from phrases and sub-phrases. As a slightly larger structure, this theme type involves interesting questions about formal organization and perception–questions that stem from its historical pedigree among other things–that we will also find in the full-movement forms.

与更小的[乐句式](/posts/music/form/theme-types/sentence/)、[乐段式](/posts/music/form/theme-types/period/)和[混合主题](/posts/music/form/theme-types/hybridThemes/)一样，小三部曲式可以单独充当整体乐章曲式的主题。不过小三部曲式规模更大——它由主题构建而成，正如那些较小的主题类型由乐句与次级乐句构建而成。作为一个稍大的结构，这一主题类型涉及关于曲式组织与感知的有趣问题——这些问题部分源自其历史渊源——我们在整体乐章曲式中也会遇到。

This theme type is normally expressed through three [thematic functions](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/): “exposition (A),” “contrasting middle (B),” and “recapitulation (A).”

这一主题类型通常通过三种[主题功能](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/)表现：“呈示部（A）”、“对比中段（B）”与“再现部（A）”。

### Three thematic functions｜三种主题功能
The *exposition* and *recapitulation* are “tight-knit” functions usually expressed by one of the smaller theme types. Each ends with a cadence. Most commonly, a small-ternary’s *exposition* will end with a I:PAC, but the exposition may also modulate to a closely related key. In major-mode pieces, a typical modulation will yield an exposition ending with a V:PAC, while in the minor mode, the mediant (III:PAC) key area is more common.

*呈示部*与*再现部*是“紧凑”功能，通常由某一种较小的主题类型表现。两者各以终止式结束。最常见的是，小三部曲式的*呈示部*以 I:PAC 结束，但呈示部也可能转调到近关系调。在大调作品中，典型的转调会使呈示部以 V:PAC 结束；而在小调中，则更常见的是中音调区域（III:PAC）。

A *recapitulation* follows the *contrasting middle* and is marked by a return of thematic material from the *exposition*. In the simplest examples, a *recapitulation* may simply copy the *exposition.* But more commonly, some changes are made, including [expansions and contractions](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/internalExpansions/). Alterations are especially common–necessary, in fact–when the *exposition* cadences in a non-tonic key: *Recapitulation’s* invariably close with a I:PAC, and thus, if the exposition has modulated, the recapitulation must be altered to end in tonic.

*再现部*位于*对比中段*之后，其标志是*呈示部*主题材料的回归。在最简单的例子中，*再现部*可能直接复制*呈示部*。但更常见的是作出若干改动，包括[扩展与压缩](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/internalExpansions/)。当*呈示部*在非主调上终止时，改动尤为常见——实际上是必需的：*再现部*总是以 I:PAC 结束，因此，若呈示部已转调，再现部就必须加以改动以结束在主调。

Located between the *exposition* and *recapitulation,* the *contrasting middle* is a “loose” formal region that provides melodic and harmonic contrast. Because it is a looser region, it is not commonly expressed by a theme type. If anything, unpredictability is typical. Nonetheless, we can often characterize such passages in terms of “loosening devices” like fragmentation, sequence, and modulation. And as a means of providing harmonic contrast, *contrasting middles* tend to emphasize dominant harmony. To mark their end, provide contrast, and create anticipation for the return of tonic in the *recapitulation,* these sections invariably have dominant harmony at their close. This may be expressed as a I:HC, V:PAC, or in simpler cases, the entire *contrasting middle* may prolong dominant harmony through a “[standing on the dominant](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/externalExpansions/#suffix).”

*对比中段*位于*呈示部*与*再现部*之间，是提供旋律与和声对比的“松散”曲式区域。由于它较为松散，通常不由某种主题类型表现。要说有什么典型特征，那就是不可预测。尽管如此，我们常常可以用碎片化、模进、转调等“松弛手段”来描述这类段落。作为提供和声对比的手段，*对比中段*倾向于强调属和声。为标示其结束、提供对比，并为*再现部*中主音的回归制造期待，这些段落总是在收束处具有属和声。这可能表现为 I:HC、V:PAC，或在较简单的情形中，整个*对比中段*通过“[停留于属音](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/externalExpansions/#suffix)”来延长属和声。

### An example｜一个例子

![莫扎特 D 大调钢琴奏鸣曲 K. 284 谱例](/media/music/graphics/form/k284.png)

*Mozart, Piano Sonata in D major, K. 284, "Tema con variazione"*

*莫扎特《D 大调钢琴奏鸣曲》K. 284，"Tema con variazione"*

A prototypical example is given above–the “theme” of the variation movement closing Mozart’s Piano Sonata in D major, K. 284. Mozart’s *exposition* is a simple period whose second half modulates to the dominant, ending with a V:PAC. Following the repeat, the *contrasting middle* begins with a [*fonte*](/posts/music/form/galant-schemata/schemataContinuationPatterns/) sequence that leads to a fragmented, descending scalar passage that closes the section with a I:HC. (Note the motivic relationships to the *exposition*: the descending scale in m. 10 recalls the pickup to m. 7, and the *fonte* sets a melodic motive that resembles the first two beats of m. 7.) Finally, the recapitulation recalls the exposition. It presents only the *contrasting phrase*, however, altering it so that the theme ends with a I:PAC.

上文给出了一个典型例子——莫扎特《D 大调钢琴奏鸣曲》K. 284 末乐章变奏曲乐章的“主题”。莫扎特的*呈示部*是一个简单的乐段式，其后半转调到属调，以 V:PAC 结束。反复之后，*对比中段*以一个[*fonte*](/posts/music/form/galant-schemata/schemataContinuationPatterns/)模进开始，导向一段碎片化的下行音阶段落，以 I:HC 结束该部分。（请注意与*呈示部*的动机关联：第 10 小节的下行音阶令人想起第 7 小节的弱起，而 *fonte* 所设置的旋律动机与第 7 小节前两拍相似。）最后，再现部召回呈示部。但它只呈现了*对比乐句*，并加以改动，使主题以 I:PAC 结束。

### Binary characteristics｜二部曲式特征
Mozart’s theme gives us an example of one of the most common characteristics of the small ternary: the repeat signs. Those repeat signs divide the form into *two parts*, and therein lies one of the most discussed (and perhaps, most controversial and confusing) features of this thematic type. Because on the largest scale the three parts of this theme are nested within *two reprises*, many music theorists prefer to label these passages as *binaries*. Since we have chosen to emphasize the functional characteristics of classical form, of which there are three in this passage, the ternary understanding is more appropriate here. Throughout the history of music, we also find examples without repeats (or with perhaps only one repeat), and those examples establish the repeat signs as a non-obligatory feature.

莫扎特的主题为我们提供了小三部曲式最常见特征之一的例子：反复记号。这些反复记号把曲式划分为*两个部分*，而这正是这一主题类型最受讨论（也许还是最具争议、最令人困惑）的特征之一。由于在最大尺度上，这一主题的三个部分被嵌套在*两次反复*之内，许多音乐理论家倾向于把这类段落标为*二部曲式*。由于我们选择强调古典曲式的功能特征，而这一段落中恰有三个功能，因此在这里三部曲式的理解更为恰当。纵观音乐史，我们也能找到没有反复（或也许只有一次反复）的例子，这些例子表明反复记号并非必需特征。

But rather than settling the question, sophisticated analysts tend to understand the ambiguity present here. As a common hedge, many music theorists label passages like Mozart’s (those with an *exposition*, *contrasting middle*, *recapitulation*, *and* repeat signs) as “rounded binaries.” The “rounded” description refers to the return of the exposition material at the form’s end, which has the effect of “rounding out” the structure.

但精细的分析者并不急于解决这一问题，而是倾向于理解其中存在的模糊性。作为一种常见的折中做法，许多音乐理论家把莫扎特这类段落（即同时具有*呈示部*、*对比中段*、*再现部*与*反复记号*的段落）标为“圆融二部曲式”。“圆融”这一说法指的是呈示部材料在曲式结尾的回归，其效果是使结构“趋于圆融”。
