*The sentence*

The prototypical sentence is eight measures long and contains two four-measure phrases. The first of these is called the *presentation phrase* and the second is the *continuation phrase*.

典型乐句式长八小节，包含两个四小节的乐句。前者称为*呈示乐句*，后者称为*继续乐句*。

![乐句式谱例](/media/music/graphics/ClassicalThemes/sentence.svg)

## Presentation phrase (mm. 1–4)｜呈示乐句（第 1–4 小节）

The presentation phrase begins the sentence and has two primary components, one melodic and the other harmonic. Melodically, it contains two repeated *basic ideas* (BI). Harmonically, it prolongs the tonic, by means of either a [subsidiary harmonic progression](/posts/music/harmony/harmonicSyntax2/#subsidiary-harmonic-progressions") or [contrapuntal chords](/posts/music/harmony/harmonicSyntax2/#contrapuntal-prolongation--passing-chord).

呈示乐句开启乐句式，包含两个主要组成方面：一为旋律，一为和声。旋律上，它包含两个重复的*基本乐思*（BI）。和声上，它延长主音，手段或为[辅助性和声进行](/posts/music/harmony/harmonicSyntax2/#subsidiary-harmonic-progressions")，或为[对位和弦](/posts/music/harmony/harmonicSyntax2/#contrapuntal-prolongation--passing-chord)。

![莫扎特 K. 283 呈示乐句谱例](/media/music/graphics/form/k283-presentation.png)

*Presentation: Mozart, K. 283, i., mm. 1–4*

*呈示：莫扎特 K. 283 第一乐章，第 1–4 小节*

Here, the basic idea begins with the pickup to m. 1 and ends with the F-sharp on the downbeat of m. 2. It’s then repeated. Though a basic idea is often exactly repeated, this example shows that it need not be. Rather, a basic idea is defined more abstractly by its rhythm and melodic contour, and thus, varied repetitions like this are possible.

此例中，基本乐思从第 1 小节的弱起开始，结束于第 2 小节强拍上的升 F。随后它被重复。基本乐思虽然常常被原样重复，但此例表明并非必须如此。更确切地说，基本乐思是由其节奏与旋律轮廓以更抽象的方式界定的，因此像这样变化的重复是可能的。

Harmonically, these four measures prolong tonic. Measures two and three are contrapuntal chords that are surrounded by the tonic:

和声上，这四个小节延长了主音。第 2 与第 3 小节为对位和弦，其前后被主音包围：

> **I V4/3 V6/5 I**
> or
> **T(1 D(2 7)n 1)**

Altogether, the two expressions of the basic idea and the tonic prolongation in this phrase exhibit [*presentation function*](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/#presentation). Hence, the name “presentation phrase.”

总体而言，此乐句中基本乐思的两次表述与主音延长共同呈现了[*呈示功能*](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/#presentation)。故此得名“呈示乐句”。

## Continuation phrase (mm. 5–10)｜继续乐句（第 5–10 小节）

Continuation phrases acquire momentum and lead to the cadence that ends the sentence. Three types of cadence typically end a sentence: PAC, IAC, or HC.

继续乐句获得推动力，并导向结束乐句式的终止式。通常有三种终止式类型结束乐句式：PAC、IAC 或 HC。

Continuation phrases begin with [*continuation function*](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/#continuation-function), which has one or more (but not necessarily all) of the following five characteristics.

继续乐句以[*继续功能*](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/#continuation-function)开始，该功能具有以下五项特征中的一项或多项（但不必全部具备）。

- Fragmentation: a breakdown in the size of melodic units／碎片化：旋律单位规模的解体
- Liquidation: removal of “characteristic” melodic figures／消解：去除“特征性”旋律音型
- Sequential repetition／模进重复
- Accelerated surface rhythm／表层节奏加快
- Accelerated harmonic rhythm／和声节奏加快

![莫扎特 K. 283 呈示与继续谱例](/media/music/graphics/form/k283.png)

*Presentation + Continuation: Mozart, K. 283, I, mm. 1–10*

*呈示 + 继续：莫扎特 K. 283 第一乐章，第 1–10 小节*

This example exhibits all five characteristics. The basic idea creates a normative unit that is two measures in length. Beginning with the pickup to m. 5, that normative size is *fragmented* into one-measure units whose second half is *sequenced* one step lower. At m. 7 *increased surface rhythm* is matched with an *acceleration of harmonic rhythm* as the pianist’s sixteenth notes occur above a hemiola in the pianist’s left hand.

此例呈现了全部五项特征。基本乐思建立起一个两小节长度的规范单位。从第 5 小节的弱起开始，该规范规模被*碎片化*为一小节的单位，其后半部分*模进*低一个音级。至第 7 小节，钢琴的十六分音符出现在其左手的赫米奥拉节奏之上，*表层节奏加快*与*和声节奏加快*相应出现。

Sentences always end with cadential progressions that support [*cadential function*](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/#cadential-function). In this passage, while mm. 5–8 prolonged tonic by means of contrapuntal chords, mm. 8–10 employ a typical cadential progression:

乐句式总是以支撑[*终止功能*](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/#cadential-function)的终止进行结束。在此片段中，第 5–8 小节以对位和弦延长主音，第 8–10 小节则采用典型的终止进行：

> **I IV6 Cad.6/4 V7 I**
> or
> **T1 S6 D5 — T1**

And while mm. 5–8 employed fragments of the basic idea (the “characteristic” elements of the melody), mm. 8–10 *liquidates* those into elements unrelated to the basic idea (the “conventional” elements—scales and arpeggios, ending with a descending contour).

第 5–8 小节使用了基本乐思的碎片（旋律中的“特征性”要素），而第 8–10 小节则将其*消解*为与基本乐思无关的要素（“常规性”要素——音阶与琶音，以下行轮廓收束）。

## Phrase length｜乐句长度

Note the length of the phrases in this example: a four-bar presentation phrase is followed by a *six-bar* continuation phrase. The prototypical phrase is four measures, but this is commonly [expanded](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/internalExpansions/) or even compressed by composers. In the sentence, continuation phrases are more likely to undergo expansions or contractions than are presentation phrases.

请注意此例中各乐句的长度：四小节的呈示乐句之后跟着一个*六小节*的继续乐句。典型的乐句为四小节，但作曲家常常将其[扩展](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/internalExpansions/)甚至压缩。在乐句式中，继续乐句比呈示乐句更可能经历扩展或压缩。
