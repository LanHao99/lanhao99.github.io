*Hybrid themes*

Hybrid themes mixes the [functional features](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/) of [sentences](/posts/music/form/theme-types/sentence/) and [periods](/posts/music/form/theme-types/period/). William Caplin has identified four primary types:

混合主题融合了[乐句式](/posts/music/form/theme-types/sentence/)与[乐段式](/posts/music/form/theme-types/period/)的[功能特征](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/)。William Caplin 区分出四种主要类型：

## Hybrid 1｜混合主题 1

Hybrid 1 combines an [antecedent phrase](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/#antecedent) with a [continuation phrase](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/#continuation).

混合主题 1 将[前句](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/#antecedent)与[继续乐句](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/#continuation)组合在一起。

![混合主题 1 结构图](/media/music/graphics/ClassicalThemes/hybrid1.svg)

![莫扎特 C 大调钢琴奏鸣曲 K. 330 第二乐章谱例](/media/music/graphics/form/k330-ii.png)

*Antecedent + Continuation: Mozart, Piano Sonata in C major, K. 330 II, mm. 1–8*

*前句 + 继续：莫扎特《C 大调钢琴奏鸣曲》K. 330 第二乐章，第 1–8 小节*

<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/2l74mmkDhW5DxN7LNnkeSd" width="100%" height="152" frameborder="0" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

A prototypical example is found in the first eight measures of Mozart’s Piano Sonata in C major, II. The antecedent phrase contains a basic idea characterized by the repeated notes forming its anacrusis. This is followed by a contrasting idea formed from a scalar ascent leading to the phrase’s half cadence in m. 4. Following the antecedent phrase, the music begins to express continuation function, primarily through fragmentation and an increase in surface rhythm. In the middle of m. 6, the characteristic melodic motives are liquidated into conventional scalar figuration leading to a V:PAC.

一个典型例子见于莫扎特《C 大调钢琴奏鸣曲》第二乐章的前八小节。前句包含一个基本乐思，其特征是构成弱起的重复音。随后是一个对比乐思，由级进上行的音阶构成，导向第 4 小节乐句的半终止。前句之后，音乐开始表现继续功能，主要通过碎片化与表层节奏的加快。在第 6 小节中段，特征性旋律动机被消解为常规的音阶音型，导向 V:PAC。

## Hybrid 2｜混合主题 2

Hybrid 2 combines an antecedent phrase with a four-measure cadential progression.

混合主题 2 将前句与一个四小节的终止进行组合在一起。

![混合主题 2 结构图](/media/music/graphics/ClassicalThemes/hybrid2.svg)

![海顿 G 大调弦乐四重奏 Hob. III:66 第二乐章谱例](/media/music/graphics/form/haydn-III-66-ii.png)

*Antecedent + Cadential: Haydn, String Quartet in G major, Hob. III:66, II, mm. 1–8*

*前句 + 终止式：海顿《G 大调弦乐四重奏》Hob. III:66 第二乐章，第 1–8 小节*

<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/2Dyh8LMXHEv4Hxchepyubj" width="100%" height="152" frameborder="0" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

Here, the four measures subsequent to the antecedent support a single cadential progression:

此处，前句之后的四个小节支撑单一的终止进行：

> **I6 ii6/5 V I**

Notice that this four-measure phrase does not display any markers of [continuation function](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/#continuation).

请注意，这个四小节的乐句并不显示任何[继续功能](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/#continuation)的标志。

## The Compound Basic Idea｜复合基本乐思

Just as we sometimes find composers combining different features of the two primary thematic types, we also often find combinations of different types of phrase. The “compound basic idea,” or CBI, combines the melodic characteristics of the [antecedent function](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/#antecedent) with the harmonic characteristics of the [presentation](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/#presentation) function. Like an antecedent, it presents a basic idea followed by a contrasting one. But like a presentation, the compound basic idea simply prolongs tonic, without ending in a cadence.

正如我们有时会发现作曲家把两种主要主题类型的不同特征加以组合，我们也常常遇到不同类型乐句的组合。“复合基本乐思”（CBI）把[前句功能](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/#antecedent)的旋律特征与[呈示](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/#presentation)功能的和声特征结合在一起。与前句一样，它呈现一个基本乐思，随后跟一个对比乐思。但与呈示一样，复合基本乐思只是延长主音，并不以终止式结束。

## Hybrid 3｜混合主题 3

The third hybrid type strongly resembles the [first hybrid](/posts/music/form/theme-types/hybridThemes/#hybrid-1). Rather than beginning with an initiating antecedent, however, its first phrase is a [CBI](/posts/music/form/theme-types/hybridThemes/#the-compound-basic-idea). Following the CBI, Hybrid 3 concludes with a continuation that ends with a cadence.

第三种混合类型与[第一种混合主题](/posts/music/form/theme-types/hybridThemes/#hybrid-1)十分相似。但它并非以一个开启性的前句开始，其第一乐句是一个[CBI](/posts/music/form/theme-types/hybridThemes/#the-compound-basic-idea)。CBI 之后，混合主题 3 以一个以终止式结束的继续部分收束。

![混合主题 3 结构图](/media/music/graphics/ClassicalThemes/hybrid3.svg)

![贝多芬小提琴奏鸣曲 Op. 30 No. 2 谱例](/media/music/graphics/form/beethoven_op30.png)

*Compound Basic Idea + Continuation: Beethoven, Violin Sonata, Op. 30, No. 2, III, mm. 1–8*

*复合基本乐思 + 继续：贝多芬《小提琴奏鸣曲》Op. 30 No. 2 第三乐章，第 1–8 小节*

<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/2MeFF0iyjMts1juRwgDXAT" width="100%" height="152" frameborder="0" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

In this example, from Beethoven’s Violin Sonata, Op. 30, the melodic structure of the initiating phrase contains two contrasting ideas, each of which begins with the same dotted figure. However, unlike a typical antecedent, the phrase only prolongs tonic as the V6 on the last beat of m. 3 only decorates the tonic through a lower neighbor motion rather than creating cadential articulation. Thus, the phrase is best understood as a [“compound basic idea.”](/posts/music/form/theme-types/hybridThemes/#the-compound-basic-idea)

在此例中（出自贝多芬《小提琴奏鸣曲》Op. 30），开启乐句的旋律结构包含两个对比乐思，每个都以相同的附点音型开始。然而，与典型的前句不同，该乐句只是延长主音，因为第 3 小节最后一拍上的 V6 仅通过下方邻音运动装饰主音，并未造成终止式分句。因此，该乐句最好理解为[“复合基本乐思”](/posts/music/form/theme-types/hybridThemes/#the-compound-basic-idea)。

The concluding phrase is a typical continuation expressed through fragmentation, melodic sequence, and increased harmonic rhythm.

结束乐句是典型的继续，通过碎片化、旋律模进与和声节奏加快得以表现。

## Hybrid 4｜混合主题 4

Hybrid 4 resembles the period, with the exception that the first phrase is comprised of a “compound basic idea” rather than an antecedent. Following the [CBI](/posts/music/form/theme-types/hybridThemes/#the-compound-basic-idea), the concluding phrase expresses the function of a consequent, typically altering the return of the contrasting idea so that the theme ends with a strong cadence.

混合主题 4 与乐段式相似，区别在于其第一乐句由“复合基本乐思”而非前句构成。[CBI](/posts/music/form/theme-types/hybridThemes/#the-compound-basic-idea)之后，结束乐句表现后句的功能，通常会改动对比乐思的再现，使主题以强终止式结束。

![混合主题 4 结构图](/media/music/graphics/ClassicalThemes/hybrid4.svg)

![贝多芬弦乐四重奏 Op. 18 No. 2 谱例](/media/music/graphics/form/beethoven_18_2.png)

*Compound Basic Idea + Consequent: Beethoven, String Quartet in G major, Op. 18/2, IV., mm. 1–8*

*复合基本乐思 + 后句：贝多芬《G 大调弦乐四重奏》Op. 18/2 第四乐章，第 1–8 小节*

<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/5AevVo3SRuHLJIZpz0WT6N" width="100%" height="152" frameborder="0" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

The cello’s presentation of the initiating phrase contains two distinct melodic ideas. But this phrase’s conclusion, on the C4 in m. 4, is not sufficient to create cadential closure. When the remaining strings enter in m. 5, they work through the same basic and contrasting ideas, but are able to create a I:PAC to close the consequent phrase.

大提琴对开启乐句的呈示包含两个不同的旋律乐思。但该乐句在第 4 小节 C4 上的收束，不足以造成终止式收拢。当其余弦乐在第 5 小节进入时，它们再次处理相同的基本乐思与对比乐思，却能够形成 I:PAC 来结束后句。
