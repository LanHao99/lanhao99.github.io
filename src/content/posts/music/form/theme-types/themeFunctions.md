## Initiating Functions｜开启功能

### Presentation｜呈示

*Presentation function* comes at the *beginning* of a theme or phrase. It involves the *establishment of the primary melodic material* (usually through the use of one or more statements of a *basic idea*) and the *establishment of the tonality of the theme* (usually through *tonic prolongation*).

*呈示功能*出现在主题或乐句的*开头*。它涉及*主要旋律材料的建立*（通常通过一次或多次陈述*基本乐思*），以及*主题调性的建立*（通常通过*主音延长*）。

### Antecedent｜前句

*Antecedent function* comes at the *beginning* of a theme or phrase. It involves the statement of a *basic idea* followed by a *contrasting idea.* An *antecedent* should close with a weak cadence, usually a HC or IAC.

*前句功能*出现在主题或乐句的*开头*。它涉及陈述一个*基本乐思*，随后跟一个*对比乐思*。*前句*应以弱终止式收束，通常为 HC 或 IAC。

## Medial Functions｜中间功能

### Continuation｜继续

*Continuation function* comes in the *middle* of a phrase or theme. It typically involves the *breakdown of the primary melodic material* and *harmonic acceleration* towards the cadence.

*继续功能*出现在乐句或主题的*中间*。它通常涉及*主要旋律材料的解体*，以及朝向终止式的*和声加速*。

Following are important terms/concepts associated with continuation function. Not all need be present for a passage to express continuation function, but some should be.

以下是若干与继续功能相关的重要术语／概念。一段音乐要表现继续功能，并不需要全部具备，但应具备其中若干。

- *Fragmentation* – Breaking the melodic unit into smaller chunks (for example, following two-bar basic ideas in the presentation with one-bar melodic ideas). Note: fragmentation references the breakdown of the *size* of the units. Those units are not necessarily related melodically.／*碎片化*——把旋律单位拆解为更小的片段（例如，在呈示的两小节基本乐思之后接以一小节的旋律乐思）。注意：碎片化指的是单位*规模*的解体。这些单位在旋律上不一定相关。
- *Liquidation* – Gradually replacing the *characteristic* or unique parts of a melody with *conventional* or common elements.／*消解*——用*常规*或常见的要素，逐渐替换旋律中*特征性*或独特的成分。
- *Sequential repetition* – Repeating the same melodic or harmonic element two or three times, transposed to different pitch levels. This is often used in conjunction with fragmentation.／*模进重复*——将同一旋律或和声要素转到不同音高层面重复两至三次。这常与碎片化一起使用。
- *Acceleration of melodic rhythm* – Changing from predominately quarter notes and eighth notes in the melody to predominately eighth notes and sixteenth notes, for example.／*旋律节奏加快*——例如，从旋律以四分音符与八分音符为主，变为以八分音符与十六分音符为主。
- *Acceleration of harmonic rhythm* – Chord changes coming more frequently (changing from one chord per bar to two chords per bar, for example).／*和声节奏加快*——和弦变换变得更为频繁（例如，从每小节一个和弦变为每小节两个和弦）。

# Closing Functions｜收束功能

### Cadential｜终止式

*Cadential function* comes at the end of a theme or phrase. It typically involves a *cadential harmonic progression* and a *conventional, descending melodic pattern*.

*终止功能*出现在主题或乐句的结尾。它通常涉及一个*终止和声进行*，以及一个*常规的下行旋律型*。

A classical cadential progression *begins with the last chord of tonic prolongation* and *ends with a cadential arrival*. The [three typical types of cadential arrival](/posts/music/harmony/cadenceTypes/) in Classical music are the perfect authentic cadence (PAC), the imperfect authentic cadence (IAC), and the half cadence (HC).

古典终止进行*以主音延长的最后一个和弦开始*，并*以终止式到达结束*。古典音乐中[三种典型的终止式到达类型](/posts/music/harmony/cadenceTypes/)是完全正格终止（PAC）、不完全正格终止（IAC）与半终止（HC）。

A half-cadential progression will begin with the final **T** chord, progress (optionally) through **S**, and arrive on a cadential **D** chord (*always* **D5**: **V** or **V7**):

半终止进行将以最后的 **T** 和弦开始，（可选择地）经过 **S**，到达终止性的 **D** 和弦（*总是* **D5**：**V** 或 **V7**）：

> **T\_\_ (S\_\_) D5**

An authentic-cadential progression will begin with the final **T** chord, progress (optionally) through **S**, and end with the cadential **D T** progression (*always* **D5 T1**: **V(7) I**):

正格终止进行将以最后的 **T** 和弦开始，（可选择地）经过 **S**，并以终止性的 **D T** 进行结束（*总是* **D5 T1**：**V(7) I**）：

> **T\_ (S\_) D5 T1**

### Consequent｜后句

*Consequent function* resembles *antecedent function* in that it involves the presentation of a basic idea followed by a contrasting one. Unlike the *antecedent function*, however, *consequent function* brings completion to a thematic unit. Therefore, it ends with a strong cadence, typically a PAC.

*后句功能*与*前句功能*相似，都涉及呈现一个基本乐思，随后跟一个对比乐思。然而，与*前句功能*不同，*后句功能*使主题单位获得完成。因此，它以强终止式结束，通常为 PAC。
