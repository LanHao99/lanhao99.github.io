*The small binary*

The small binary often fulfills a structural role similar to that as the small ternary. It can be the main theme of any of the full-movement forms, but is most often found as the theme of a theme and variations, [rondo](/posts/music/form/rondo/thematicFunctionInRondo/), or the [minuet and trio](/posts/music/form/other/minuet/).

小二部曲式常常承担与小三部曲式相似的结构作用。它可以是任何整体乐章曲式的主主题，但最常见的是作为变奏曲、[回旋曲](/posts/music/form/rondo/thematicFunctionInRondo/)或[小步舞曲与三声中部](/posts/music/form/other/minuet/)的主题。

Small binaries have two parts, each of which is usually repeated. Unlike the [“rounded binary,”](smallTernary.html##Binary characteristics) small binaries *do not* include a recapitulation of the material heard in the *first part*.

小二部曲式有两个部分，每部分通常各自重复。与[“圆融二部曲式”](smallTernary.html##Binary characteristics)不同，小二部曲式*不*包含对*第一部分*所听材料的再现。

### First part｜第一部分
Typically constructed as tight-knit [period](/posts/music/form/theme-types/period/), [sentence](/posts/music/form/theme-types/sentence/), or [hybrid](/posts/music/form/theme-types/hybridThemes/), the *first part* ends with cadential articulation in the home key or a closely related one. Because a small binary is often smaller than that of the *small ternary,* it is not uncommon to find a I:HC at the close of the first part.

*第一部分*通常构建为紧凑的[乐段式](/posts/music/form/theme-types/period/)、[乐句式](/posts/music/form/theme-types/sentence/)或[混合主题](/posts/music/form/theme-types/hybridThemes/)，并以主调或近关系调中的终止式分句结束。由于小二部曲式往往比*小三部曲式*更小，在第一部分结尾出现 I:HC 并不罕见。

### Second part｜第二部分
The *second part* of a small binary quite often begins like a contrasting middle. The material that *follows* the *contrasting middle*, however, is not a *recapitulation*. If it were, the theme would be a [small ternary](/posts/music/form/theme-types/smallTernary/). Rather, this material takes on a variety of guises. Often, it resembles one of the common “after-phrases”–*continuation*, *consequent,* or *cadential.* In the classical style, the second part of a small binary will always close with a I:PAC.

小二部曲式的*第二部分*常常以类似对比中段的方式开始。然而，紧随*对比中段*之后的材料并不是*再现*。若是再现，该主题就成[小三部曲式](/posts/music/form/theme-types/smallTernary/)了。这些材料反而以多种面貌出现。它常常类似常见的“后句”——*继续*、*后句*或*终止式*。在古典风格中，小二部曲式的第二部分总是以 I:PAC 结束。

### An example｜一个例子

![莫扎特 A 大调小提琴奏鸣曲 K. 305 谱例](/media/music/graphics/form/k305.png)

*Mozart, Violin Sonata in A major, K. 305*

*莫扎特《A 大调小提琴奏鸣曲》K. 305*

In this small binary, the *first part* is created by a simple period that modulates to and closes in the dominant key with a V:PAC. A *contrasting middle* follows, where we see a typical [“standing on the dominant”](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/externalExpansions/#suffix) to support a prolongation of V7. Notice that the motivic material in the *contrasting middle* is quite similar to the motivic material we hear in the *first part.* This is typical of a small binary, whose *second part* does not contrast with the *first part* to such a high degree as in the *small ternary.* Following the *contrasting middle*, the final four measures of this example resemble a continuation: the melodic motive we hear in the *contrasting material* is fragmented and sequenced, and begins, with the pickup to the penultimate measure, a cadence.

在这个小二部曲式中，*第一部分*由一个简单的乐段式构成，它转调到属调并以 V:PAC 在属调上收束。随后是*对比中段*，其中我们看到典型的[“停留于属音”](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/externalExpansions/#suffix)以支撑 V7 的延长。请注意，*对比中段*中的动机材料与我们在*第一部分*听到的动机材料十分相似。这是小二部曲式的典型特征：其*第二部分*与*第一部分*的对比程度不如*小三部曲式*那样强烈。*对比中段*之后，此例最后四小节类似继续：*对比材料*中的旋律动机被碎片化并模进，并从倒数第二小节的弱起开始一个终止式。
