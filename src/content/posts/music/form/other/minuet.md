*Minuet Form*

A classical minuet movement typically contains a *main minuet*, followed by a *trio, \_followed by a \_da capo* repeat of the main minuet (usually performed without taking the repeats). The movement, then, has a large-scale ABA’ form: *minuet–trio–minuet da capo*.

古典小步舞曲乐章通常包含一个*主小步舞曲*，其后是*三声中部，\_再接以\_da capo*（从头反复）的主小步舞曲再现（通常演奏时不作反复）。因此，这一乐章具有宏观的 ABA' 形式：*小步舞曲—三声中部—小步舞曲 da capo*。

Both the main minuet and the trio tend to be *small ternary* structures. Like the minuet/trio movement itself, the small ternary form follows an ABA’ structure. However, the small ternary structure found in the typical *main minuet* is of the *rounded binary* type. That is, while there are three distinct modules—A, B, and A’—they are grouped into two larger sections, each of which is repeated.

主小步舞曲与三声中部往往都是*小型三部*结构。与整个小步舞曲／三声中部乐章一样，小型三部曲式遵循 ABA' 结构。然而，典型*主小步舞曲*中的小型三部结构属于*带再现二部*类型。也就是说，尽管存在三个彼此区分的模块——A、B 与 A'——它们被归入两个更大的部分，而每一部分都加以反复。

The first part in the two-part structure is the *first reprise*, and the second part is the *second reprise*, so called because they each repeat. The first reprise contains the A module of the minuet; the second contains the B and the A’ modules.

这一二部结构中的第一部分是*第一复奏*，第二部分是*第二复奏*，之所以这样称呼，是因为它们各自都要反复。第一复奏包含小步舞曲的 A 模块；第二复奏包含 B 与 A' 模块。

Each section of the minuet’s (or the trio’s) small ternary form has its own formal function attached to it. The formal function exhibited in the A section is called *exposition* function; the B section *contrasting middle* function ; and the A’ section *recapitulation* function.

小步舞曲（或三声中部）小型三部曲式的每一部分都附着着自身的曲式功能。A 部分所呈现的曲式功能称为*呈示*功能；B 部分为*对比中段*功能；A' 部分为*再现*功能。

## Exposition｜呈示

At the least, the exposition module of the minuet typically contains a primary theme: tight-knit (period, sentence, or hybrid), ending with a PAC, or occasionally a HC. It is common, however, for an exposition to have more complex structure, projecting not just primary theme, but secondary thematic function, transition, and closing. Not all of these functions are necessarily present.

至少可以说，小步舞曲的呈示模块通常包含一个主部主题：结构紧凑（乐段式、乐句式或混合式），以完全终止（PAC）结束，偶尔以半终止（HC）结束。然而，呈示部具有更复杂结构也很常见，它不仅投射主部主题，还投射第二主题功能、连接段与结束段。这些功能并非必然全部出现。

**Primary Theme**

**主部主题**

Expositions always have a *primary themes*. A prototypical *primary theme* is (1) tight-knit and (2) ends with a PAC or HC in the tonic key. When an exposition does not modulate, it is understood to exclusively express *primary theme* function.

呈示部总是具有*主部主题*。原型性的*主部主题*（1）结构紧凑，（2）以主调上的完全终止（PAC）或半终止（HC）结束。当呈示部不发生转调时，它被理解为唯一地表达*主部主题*功能。

**Secondary Theme**

**第二主题**

But if the exposition modulates, the functions of *transition*, and *secondary theme* may appear as well. At minimum a *secondary theme* will close with cadential confirmation (usually a PAC) of the subordinate key. Often, it is looser than the main theme—perhaps [expanded or contracted](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/internalExpansions/).

但若呈示部发生转调，*连接*与*第二主题*的功能也可能出现。至少在最低限度上，*第二主题*会以从属调的终止式确认（通常为完全终止 PAC）收束。它往往比主部主题更松散——也许经过[扩充或缩减](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/internalExpansions/)。

**Transition**

**连接段**

If a phrase contains a [pivot chord modulation](/posts/music/harmony/Modulation/) linking the main key to the subordinate key, that phrase is understood to express *transition* function. (If a new phrase begins immediately in the subordinate key through [direct modulation](/posts/music/harmony/Modulation/), there is no transition function.)

若某一乐句包含连接主调与从属调的[中介和弦转调](/posts/music/harmony/Modulation/)，则该乐句被理解为表达*连接*功能。（若一个新乐句通过[直接转调](/posts/music/harmony/Modulation/)立即在从属调上开始，则不存在连接功能。）

Very, very often the transition and the secondary theme are “fused” together (TR==>S) in a single phrase.

非常、非常常见的是，连接段与第二主题在一个乐句中"融合"在一起（TR==>S）。

**Closing Section**

**结束段**

Sometimes the secondary theme closes with a PAC and is followed by a [closing section](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/externalExpansions/).

有时第二主题以完全终止（PAC）收束，其后接一个[结束段](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/externalExpansions/)。

## Contrasting Middle｜对比中段

A contrasting middle section is significantly looser than the exposition. Though it may contain some kind of thematic structure (sentence, primarily), it often does not. Sequences and remote tonal areas are quite typical of the digression section. When doing analysis, your goal should be to identify the melodic/motivic material and understand the tonal structure.

对比中段明显比呈示部更为松散。尽管它可能包含某种主题结构（主要是乐句式），但往往并不包含。模进与远关系调性区域是这一离题段相当典型的特征。进行分析时，你的目标应是辨识旋律／动机材料并理解其调性结构。

Contrasting middle passages end with a I:HC, creating a harmonic interruption. Commonly, the I:HC is followed by a post-cadential “[standing on the dominant](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/externalExpansions/).”

对比中段段落以 I:HC 结束，造成一次和声上的阻断。通常，I:HC 之后接一个终止后的"[长时间停留在属和弦上](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/externalExpansions/)"。

## Recapitulation｜再现

The definitive characteristics of a minuet’s *recapitulation* function are (1) the *return of the basic idea* from the A section at its beginning, (2) the *return of the home key* at its beginning, and (3) a final PAC in the home key.

小步舞曲*再现*功能的决定性特征是：（1）在其开头*回归 A 部分的基本乐思*；（2）在其开头*回归主调*；（3）以主调上的完全终止（PAC）作结。

A recapitulation typically copies the thematic and phrase-structural features of the exposition, but altering the secondary theme so as to end in tonic. Commonly, the recapitulation [*expands* the exposition’s closing phrase](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/internalExpansions/).

再现部通常会复制呈示部的主题与乐句结构特征，但会改动第二主题，使其结束于主调。常见的情形是，再现部[对呈示部的结束乐句加以*扩充*](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/internalExpansions/)。

## Trio｜三声中部

Like the minuet, a trio is typically a rounded or simple binary form. It’s primary job is to establish melodic and harmonic contrast. While contrasting tonally with the *main minuet* is a central feature of the Trio, Trio’s often simply projection modal contrast. When the tonic stays the same, a major-key *main minuet* might be contrasted with a Trio marked *minore.* (The corresponding situation for a minor-key *main minuet* is a *maggiore* Trio.)

与小步舞曲一样，三声中部通常为带再现二部曲式或简单二部曲式。它的首要任务是建立旋律与和声上的对比。尽管与*主小步舞曲*形成调性对比是三声中部的核心特征，三声中部也常常只是投射调式对比。当主音保持不变时，大调的*主小步舞曲*可以与标记为 *minore*（小调）的三声中部形成对比。（小调*主小步舞曲*的对应情形则是 *maggiore*（大调）三声中部。）
