*External Expansions*

A theme is often preceded or followed by material outside of its typical boundaries, but which is clearly dependent on the theme. This material is *external* to the theme itself— “before-the-beginning” or “after-the-end,” as William Caplin as termed it.

主题之前或之后往往出现位于其常规边界之外的材料，但这些材料明显依附于该主题。这类材料对主题本身而言是*外部*的——正如威廉·卡普林所说的“开端之前”或“结束之后”。

## Prefix｜前缀

An *prefix* is a passage—short or [long](/posts/music/form/sonata/sonataFramingModules/#introduction)—that *precedes* a theme. Short prefixes lack distinct motivic material and/or harmonic progression. While connected to the theme that follows, when removed prefixes do not disturb the logic of the phrase itself.

*前缀*是一段位于主题*之前*的段落——可短亦可[长](/posts/music/form/sonata/sonataFramingModules/#introduction)。短前缀缺乏独特的动机材料和／或和声进行。前缀虽与随后的主题相连，但若将其删除，并不扰乱乐句自身的逻辑。

![海顿《第 101 交响曲》谱例](/media/music/graphics/form/sym101.png)

*Prefix: Haydn, Symphony No. 101, "Clock*

*前缀：海顿《第 101 交响曲》“时钟”*

Here, a one-measure, accompanimental prefix prepares the four-measure antecedent phrase that begins in m. 2. The prefix lacks both a characteristic melody and harmonic progression.

此例中，一个一小节的伴奏性前缀为第 2 小节开始的四小节前句做准备。该前缀既无特征性旋律，也无和声进行。

## Suffix｜后缀

A suffix prolongs the harmony that closed the theme. Suffixes are described, and given different names, according to what kind of cadence, PAC or HC, ends the theme to which they are attached.

后缀延长使主题收束的和声。后缀依其所依附的主题以何种终止式（PAC 或 HC）结束而得名，并据此加以描述。

### Closing sections｜结束段

Sometimes called *codettas*, *closing sections* follow themes that end with PACs.

*结束段*有时称为*小尾声*，出现在以 PAC 结束的主题之后。

![莫扎特《C 大调钢琴奏鸣曲》K. 330 谱例](/media/music/graphics/form/k330.png)

*Closing Section: Mozart, Piano Sonata in C major, K. 330*

*结束段：莫扎特《C 大调钢琴奏鸣曲》K. 330*

<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/3ZlIXdp94i6qpfox8Su6Ll" width="100%" height="152" frameborder="0" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

The [compound period](/posts/music/form/theme-types/compoundPeriod/) here ends with a PAC in m. 16. Following the cadence, a five-measure *closing section* serves to confirm it. Notice that the melody is centered around scale degree 1—a common feature of these passages—and that harmonically the ii–V-I cadential progression that ended the theme (see m. 15 and 16) is simply repeated.

此处的[复合乐段](/posts/music/form/theme-types/compoundPeriod/)在第 16 小节以 PAC 结束。终止式之后，一个五小节的*结束段*用以确认该终止。注意旋律以第 1 级音为中心——这是此类段落的常见特征——并且在和声上，结束主题的 ii–V–I 终止进行（见第 15、16 小节）只是被重复了一次。

### Standing on the Dominant｜停留在属音上

When a *suffix* follows a half cadence, we refer to it as *standing on the dominant*. These passages are often signals for thematic entrances (such as the [second theme](/posts/music/form/sonata/sonataThematicModules/#subordinate-theme-module) of a sonata form movement) or thematic returns (such as the recapitulation in a [small ternary](/posts/music/form/theme-types/smallTernary/#three-thematic-functions)).

当*后缀*出现在半终止之后时，我们称之为*停留在属音上*。这类段落往往是主题进入（如奏鸣曲式乐章中的[副部主题](/posts/music/form/sonata/sonataThematicModules/#subordinate-theme-module)）或主题回归（如[小型三部曲式](/posts/music/form/theme-types/smallTernary/#three-thematic-functions)中的再现部）的信号。

![莫扎特《第 40 交响曲》K. 550 谱例](/media/music/graphics/form/sym40.png)

*Standing on the Dominant: Mozart, Symphony No. 40, K. 550, i*

*停留在属音上：莫扎特《第 40 交响曲》K. 550，第一乐章*

<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/04zau0E0VspgiqNvTqCO7u" width="100%" height="152" frameborder="0" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

This famous example begins with a one-measure [prefix](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/externalExpansions/#prefix) followed a [compound sentence](/posts/music/form/theme-types/compoundSentence/). The compound sentence ends with a half cadence in m. 16. (Notice the [augmented sixth chord](/posts/music/harmony/alteredSubdominants/#augmented-sixth-chords) that precedes it.) The succeeding measures *stand on the dominant,* prolonging the cadence by emphasizing dominant harmony with scale degree 5 embellished melodically.

这个著名的例子以一个一小节的[前缀](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/externalExpansions/#prefix)开始，随后是一个[复合乐句](/posts/music/form/theme-types/compoundSentence/)。该复合乐句在第 16 小节以半终止结束。（注意其前的[增六和弦](/posts/music/harmony/alteredSubdominants/#augmented-sixth-chords)。）其后的小节*停留在属音上*，通过强调属和声、并在旋律上装饰第 5 级音，来延长该终止式。
