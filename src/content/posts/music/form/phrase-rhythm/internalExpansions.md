For many different reasons, a great deal of music written in the late eighteenth and nineteenth centuries exhibits metric patterning, at many different levels of [hypermeter](http://courses.music.indiana.edu/rhythm/illustrations/hypermeter.html), that is duple in structure. Such was the tendency in the nineteenth century that William Rothstein, in [*Phrase Rhythm in Tonal Music*](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4649619W/Phrase_rhythm_in_tonal_music), has memorably dubbed this “the Great Nineteenth-Century Rhythm Problem.” It is “a danger,” Rothstein says,

出于许多不同的原因，十八世纪末与十九世纪写作的大量音乐，在许多不同的[超节拍](http://courses.music.indiana.edu/rhythm/illustrations/hypermeter.html)层面上，都呈现出二拍结构的节拍型态。这种倾向在十九世纪如此显著，以至于威廉·罗斯坦在[*Phrase Rhythm in Tonal Music*｜《调性音乐中的乐句节奏》](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL4649619W/Phrase_rhythm_in_tonal_music)中，给这一现象起了一个令人难忘的名字——“十九世纪大节奏问题”。罗斯坦说，它是“一种危险”，

> endemic in 19th-century music, of too unrelievedly duple a hypermetrical pattern, of too consistent and unvarying a phrase structure—the danger, in short, of submitting too complacently to 'the tyranny of the four-measure phrase.'

> 这种危险在十九世纪音乐中普遍存在，即超节拍型态过于一律地呈二拍，乐句结构过于统一而无变化——简言之，就是过于自满地屈从于“四小节乐句的暴政”。

Music in the classical tradition was quite often less subservient to the two- and four-bar units. While this topic has been the subject of a great deal of musical theoretical exploration, here we will concentrate on a simple and common type of *sub-phrase* and *phrase expansion*.

古典传统中的音乐往往并不那么屈从于两小节与四小节的单位。尽管这一课题已被大量音乐理论研究所探讨，此处我们只集中讨论一种简单而常见的*次乐句*与*乐句扩充*。

## Sub-phrase Expansion｜次乐句扩充

When a two-measure basic idea or contrasting idea is expanded, that expansion is often accomplished through motivic repetition within the basic idea: a motive or other small melodic figure is repeated, either exactly or with simple embellishment, causing the overall length of the sub-phrase to be larger than the expected.

当一个两小节的基本乐思或对比乐思被扩充时，这种扩充往往通过基本乐思内部的动机重复来实现：某个动机或其他小型旋律音型被重复，或原样重复，或加以简单装饰，从而使次乐句的整体长度超过预期。

![莫扎特《回旋曲》K. 494 谱例](/media/music/graphics/form/k494.png)

*Sub-phrase Expansion: Mozart, Rondo, K. 494, mm. 1–12*

*次乐句扩充：莫扎特《回旋曲》K. 494，第 1–12 小节*

<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/78LK9tbF0jwOd4PYtk42WD" width="100%" height="152" frameborder="0" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

Mozart’s Rondo in F begins with a [period](/posts/music/form/theme-types/period/). A half cadence in m. 6 is followed by a consequent phrase that ends with a PAC in m. 12. The theme’s exceptional length is caused in part by an *expansion* of the initial basic idea: in m. 3, the piano repeats m. 2. One could imagine a simple recomposition of the passage, omitting m. 3, that would restore the sub-phrase to a normal, 2-bar length.

莫扎特的 F 大调回旋曲以[乐段](/posts/music/form/theme-types/period/)开始。第 6 小节的半终止之后是后句，该后句在第 12 小节以 PAC 结束。主题异常的长度部分源于对最初基本乐思的*扩充*：在第 3 小节，钢琴重复了第 2 小节。我们可以设想对该段落作一次简单的重新写作，删去第 3 小节，即可使次乐句恢复为正常的两小节长度。

The *contrasting idea* is also three bars long. But in this case, it is *less clear* if the greater length is due to *expansion.* There is *no repetition,* and it’s difficult to imagine a two-measure recomposition of the *contrasting idea* that wouldn’t destroy some aspect of its melodic or harmonic structure.

*对比乐思*同样有三小节长。但在此例中，其较长篇幅是否源于*扩充*则*不那么清楚*。这里*没有重复*，也很难设想出一个两小节的*对比乐思*改写方案而不破坏其旋律或和声结构的某些方面。

In other words, [*irregular lengths*](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/internalExpansions/#non-standard-lengths) don’t always indicate that an expansion has occurred.

换言之，[*不规则长度*](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/internalExpansions/#non-standard-lengths)并不总是意味着发生了扩充。

## Phrase Expansion｜乐句扩充

Phrases are often expanded by repetition as well—most often in the concluding portion of a theme as a means to delay cadential arrival.

乐句同样常通过重复加以扩充——最常见的是在主题的结束部分，以此推迟终止式的到来。

![贝多芬《钢琴奏鸣曲》Op. 2 No. 3 谱例](/media/music/graphics/form/op2no3.png)

*Phrase Expansion: Beethoven, Piano Sonata, Op. 2, No. 3*

*乐句扩充：贝多芬《钢琴奏鸣曲》Op. 2 No. 3*

<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/5wi0p2gHWFYSwnjgHSYEP3" width="100%" height="152" frameborder="0" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

The main theme’s first eight measures form a simple sentence. But at the moment of expected PAC in m. 8, the melody find scale degree 3, creating an IAC. At the same time, the bass plays a descending link into a repetition of the continuation that leads to a PAC in m. 13. This phrase expansion, thus, adds five measures to the length of the basic phrase.

主部主题的前八小节构成一个简单乐句。但在第 8 小节预期出现 PAC 之处，旋律落在第 3 级音上，形成 IAC。与此同时，低音奏出一个下行连接，进入继续段的重复，最终在第 13 小节导向 PAC。因此，这一乐句扩充使基本乐句的长度增加了五小节。

---

In the example above, the cadence in m. 8 gave rise to an expansion because as it was weaker than expected. ([Primary themes](/posts/music/form/sonata/sonataThematicModules/#primary-theme-module-p) rarely conclude with IACs.) This is one of the most common musical motivations for a phrase expansion. In addition to IACs, deceptive cadences can lead to expanded phrases, and not uncommonly, an expected cadence never materializes at all—an event often called an “evaded cadence.”

在上例中，第 8 小节的终止之所以引发扩充，是因为它比预期更弱。（[主部主题](/posts/music/form/sonata/sonataThematicModules/#primary-theme-module-p)极少以 IAC 结束。）这是乐句扩充最常见的音乐动因之一。除 IAC 之外，阻碍终止也能导致乐句扩充；而且并不罕见的是，预期的终止式根本没有出现——这一现象通常称为“规避终止式”。

![莫扎特《C 大调钢琴奏鸣曲》K. 309 谱例](/media/music/graphics/form/k309.png)

*Phrase Expansion: Mozart, Piano Sonata in C major, K. 309, III*

*乐句扩充：莫扎特《C 大调钢琴奏鸣曲》K. 309，第三乐章*

<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/7DsGTbl7EABrY5GK6Dza9P" width="100%" height="152" frameborder="0" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

The primary theme of this sonata is a [compound period](/posts/music/form/theme-types/compoundPeriod/#a-compound-period-comprised-of-two-sentences) made from two sentences. Following the half cadence in m. 8, the *large consequent* is expected to conclude with a PAC in m. 16. But the PAC never materializes: the leading tone in m. 15 is not resolved, the pianist’s right hand leaps up to A5 instead. The cadence, in other words, is *evaded*. The next four measures (mm. 16–19) are a varied repeat of mm. 13-15, “one more time,” culminating in a PAC in m. 19.

这首奏鸣曲的主部主题是由两个乐句构成的[复合乐段](/posts/music/form/theme-types/compoundPeriod/#a-compound-period-comprised-of-two-sentences)。在第 8 小节的半终止之后，*大后句*预期在第 16 小节以 PAC 结束。但该 PAC 始终没有出现：第 15 小节的导音没有解决，钢琴右手反而跳进到 A5。换言之，该终止式被*规避*了。接下来的四小节（第 16–19 小节）是第 13–15 小节的变奏重复，“再来一次”，最终在第 19 小节抵达 PAC。

## Non-standard Lengths｜非标准长度
We often find expansions in places where the size of a sub-phrase or phrase has been increased. The phrase expansion described above created an 11-bar continuation, for example. But irregular phrase lengths do not necessarily indicate the presence of a phrase expansion.

我们常在次乐句或乐句规模被扩大的地方发现扩充。例如上文所述的乐句扩充造成了一个十一小节的继续段。但不规则的乐句长度并不一定意味着存在乐句扩充。

For example, listen a few times to the first 30 seconds of the following passage, from Mozart’s String Quintet in C major, K. 515. Its main theme is a [compound sentence](/posts/music/form/theme-types/compoundSentence/). The theme’s [presentation](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/#presentation) has three basic ideas (at 0:00, 0:09, and 0:16), each of which is constructed as a [compound basic idea](/posts/music/form/theme-types/hybridThemes/#compound-basic-idea)that contains a basic idea followed by a contrasting one:

例如，请把下面这段出自莫扎特《C 大调弦乐五重奏》K. 515 的音乐的前 30 秒反复听几遍。其主部主题是一个[复合乐句](/posts/music/form/theme-types/compoundSentence/)。该主题的[呈示](/posts/music/form/theme-types/themeFunctions/#presentation)包含三个基本乐思（分别在 0:00、0:09 和 0:16），每一个都构造成一个[复合基本乐思](/posts/music/form/theme-types/hybridThemes/#compound-basic-idea)，其中含有一个基本乐思及其后的对比乐思：

<iframe style="border-radius:12px" src="https://open.spotify.com/embed/track/0Mkcj2eA2EMqMGfzwCnd0u" width="100%" height="152" frameborder="0" allow="autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" loading="lazy"></iframe>

We hear in this passage that the compound basic ideas are five measures long, each basic idea itself lasting three measures! But *no phrase expansion* has occurred here. The basic idea is simply longer than normal, and perhaps a bit looser feeling as a result. When in doubt, the central question is whether a passage might be recomposed, duple-fied *without* destroying its melodic, harmonic, and motivic structure. If you can, there is a case to be made that an *expansion* is present.

我们在这段音乐中听到，复合基本乐思长达五小节，每个基本乐思本身就有三小节！但此处*并没有发生乐句扩充*。基本乐思只是比通常更长，因而听来或许稍显松散。若有疑问，核心问题是：一段音乐能否被改写、被*二拍化*，而*不*破坏其旋律、和声与动机结构。如果答案是肯定的，就有理由认为存在*扩充*。
