Like sonata form, rondo forms have a long history. Where sonata emanates from Baroque dance movements (coming mostly from Italy), rondo has a longer history whose origins are in a French instrumental genre—the rondeaux—that was characterized by an alternation of refrains and contrasting couplets. Within an instrumental sonata or symphonic work, rondos are generally found in fast finales or in slow second movements. Probably because rondos are lighter in character, they are almost never found as a first movement.

与奏鸣曲式一样，回旋曲式有着悠久的历史。奏鸣曲式源自巴洛克舞曲乐章（大多来自意大利），而回旋曲式的历史更为漫长，其源头是法国的一种器乐体裁——rondeaux（回旋歌），其特征是叠部与对比性的对句（couplet）交替出现。在器乐奏鸣曲或交响作品中，回旋曲式一般出现在快速的终曲乐章或缓慢的第二乐章。大概因为回旋曲式性格更为轻巧，它几乎从不被用作第一乐章。

Conceptually, rondo is quite simple. The form consists of refrains that alternate with episodes. The refrain material is *the same* throughout the course of a movement—sometimes slightly embellished or abbreviated—and is always heard in the tonic key. Episodes contrast with refrains tonally, and usually thematically as well. Unlike refrains, a rondo’s episodes do not have to be the same in the course of a movement. So on a large scale, a typical rondo will articulate an ABAC … A form; A stands for refrain, B and C indicate episodes containing unique thematic material. The ellipsis mark (…) indicates that the precise number of refrains and episodes is variable, though the 5-part (ABACA) rondo is certainly the most common.

从概念上说，回旋曲式相当简单。这一曲式由与插部交替出现的叠部构成。叠部材料在整首乐章的进程中始终是*同一个*——有时略有装饰或删减——并且总是在主调上出现。插部与叠部在调性上形成对比，通常也构成主题上的对比。与叠部不同，一部回旋曲式的各插部在乐章进程中不必彼此相同。因此从宏观上看，典型的回旋曲式会呈现为 ABAC … A 的形式；A 代表叠部，B 和 C 表示包含独特主题材料的插部。省略号（…）表示叠部与插部的确切数量是可变的，不过五部（ABACA）回旋曲式当然是最常见的一种。

Though conceptually simple, in practice, rondo may be the most difficult form to describe theoretically and engage with analytically. This is primarily because different composers used the simple rondo schematic above in different ways. To help you manage these deviations, we will classify rondos into two types: (1) [five-part rondo (ABACA)](/posts/music/form/rondo/fivePartRondo/), and (2) [sonata rondo (ABACABA)](/posts/music/form/rondo/sonataRondo/). The primary difference between the types, which we will discuss in more detail in the other rondo resources,is that in a sonata rondo, the first refrain and first episode constitute a complete sonata exposition that is recapitulated (with the episode transposed to the tonic key) in the third refrain and episode. Learning to recognize the types of [thematic function](/posts/music/form/rondo/thematicFunctionInRondo/) most common in rondo forms is an important first step.

尽管概念上简单，在实际操作中，回旋曲式却可能是理论上最难描述、分析上最难把握的曲式。这主要是因为不同的作曲家以不同的方式运用上述简单的回旋曲式图式。为帮助你应对这些偏离，我们将回旋曲式分为两类：（1）[五部回旋曲式（ABACA）](/posts/music/form/rondo/fivePartRondo/)；（2）[奏鸣回旋曲式（ABACABA）](/posts/music/form/rondo/sonataRondo/)。两类之间的主要差别——我们将在其他回旋曲式资料中更详细地讨论——在于：在奏鸣回旋曲式中，第一个叠部与第一个插部构成一个完整的奏鸣曲式呈示部，并由第三个叠部与插部予以再现（其中插部移至主调）。学会辨识回旋曲式中最常见的[主题功能](/posts/music/form/rondo/thematicFunctionInRondo/)类型，是重要的第一步。
