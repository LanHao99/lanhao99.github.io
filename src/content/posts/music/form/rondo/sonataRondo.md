A sonata rondo (ABACABA) features the same refrain-episode alternation that we find in the five-part rondo, but certain aspects of sonata form are infiltrate this alternation.

奏鸣回旋曲式（ABACABA）具有与五部回旋曲式相同的叠部—插部交替，但奏鸣曲式的某些方面渗入了这种交替之中。

1. Refrain 1 and Episode 1 form a sonata exposition that is recapitulated in Refrain 3 and Episode 3. As is the case in sonata form, the recapitulation contains a “tonal adjustment” so that it ends in the tonic key. That is, Episode 1 and 3 will be the same thematically, but Episode 3 will occur in tonic.／叠部 1 与插部 1 构成一个奏鸣曲式呈示部，并在叠部 3 与插部 3 中予以再现。如同奏鸣曲式中的情形，再现部包含一次"调性调整"，使其结束于主调。也就是说，插部 1 与插部 3 在主题上相同，但插部 3 出现在主调上。
2. Because it resembles sonata form, the first and third episodes are always constructed as “second-theme” complexes.／由于近似于奏鸣曲式，第一与第三个插部总是被构建为"第二主题"群。
3. Episode 2 (C in the short-hand diagram above) may be a development—possibly containing a prep-zone, CAZ, and retransition—or it may be a simpler interior theme.／插部 2（上图中的简写 C）可以是展开部——可能包含准备区（prep-zone）、核心加花区（CAZ）与连接过渡段——也可以是较为简单的内部主题。
