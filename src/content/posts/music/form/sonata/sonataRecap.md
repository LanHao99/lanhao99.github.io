The *recapitulation* is the goal of the sonata, coming after the *exposition* and the *development* (anything that precedes the exposition is *introduction* and anything that follows the recapitulation is *coda*). It answers the expectations set forth by the exposition, and it brings the *essential sonata closure*. Thus, the relationship between the exposition and the recapitulation is the a central focus of a sonata analysis.

*再现部*是奏鸣曲的目标，出现在*呈示部*与*展开部*之后（呈示部之前的任何材料是*引子*，再现部之后的任何材料是*尾声*）。它回应呈示部提出的期待，并带来*必要奏鸣曲收束*。因此，呈示部与再现部之间的关系是奏鸣曲分析的核心焦点。

In light of the importance of this relationship, an analysis of a recapitulation should always take place in light of the exposition.

鉴于这一关系的重要性，对再现部的分析始终应在呈示部的观照下进行。

## Thematic cycle｜主题循环

The recapitulation typically follows the same pattern of modules set forth in the exposition: { P TR ’ S / C } for a two-part exposition, { P TR ⇒ FS / C } for a continuous exposition (the apostrophe stands for the MC, the slash stands for the EEC/ESC).

再现部通常遵循呈示部中提出的相同模块模式：二部式呈示部为 { P TR ’ S / C }，连续式呈示部为 { P TR ⇒ FS / C }（撇号代表 MC，斜线代表 EEC/ESC）。

## Essential sonata closure｜必要奏鸣曲收束

The harmonic goal of the recapitulation (and the sonata movement as a whole) is the *essential sonata closure (ESC)*. The ESC will always be a I:PAC, and will typically occur at the same thematic point in the recapitulation as the EEC in the exposition. It is often the exact same music as in the exposition, but transposed from the secondary key to the home key.

再现部（以及整个奏鸣曲乐章）的和声目标是*必要奏鸣曲收束（ESC）*。ESC 始终是一个 I:PAC，并且通常在再现部中出现在与呈示部中 EEC 相同的主题位置上。它往往就是呈示部中的同一段音乐，只是从副调移到了主调。

## Recomposition｜重新写作

The simplest exposition–recapitulation relationship occurs in a sonata with a I:HC MC in the exposition followed immediately by S in the dominant. In such a sonata, the composer can simply repeat P–TR verbatim in the recapitulation, and then repeat S–C verbatim, but transposed down a fifth. In such a sonata, there is no recomposition. Every bar in the recapitulation directly corresponds to a bar in the exposition, at pitch or tranposed by fifth.

最简单的呈示部—再现部关系出现在这样的奏鸣曲中：呈示部中有一个 I:HC 的 MC，紧接着是属调上的 S。在这样的奏鸣曲中，作曲家只需在再现部中原样重复 P–TR，然后原样重复 S–C，但下移五度。这样的奏鸣曲中没有重新写作。再现部的每一小节都直接对应呈示部中的某一小节，或为同一音高，或移调五度。

In most sonatas, however, some music from the exposition is *recomposed* in the recapitulation, often to “undo” the modulation that happened on the way to a V:HC MC. In such cases, we use the term *correspondence bars* (or *correspondence measures*) to refer to the passages that are the same (or the same transposed) in the exposition and the recapitulation. *Referential bars* make clear reference to specific bars in the exposition, but the material is changed in some non-trivial way. *Alterations* are passages in the recapitulation that have no clear reference or correspondence to passages in the exposition.

然而在大多数奏鸣曲中，呈示部的某些音乐在再现部里被*重新写作*，往往是为了“撤销”在通往 V:HC 的 MC 途中发生的转调。在这种情况下，我们用*对应小节*（correspondence bars，或称 correspondence measures）这一术语来指呈示部与再现部中相同（或作相同移调）的段落。*参照小节*明确参照呈示部中的特定小节，但材料以某种并非无关紧要的方式被改动。*改动*则指再现部中与呈示部段落没有明确参照或对应关系的段落。

Correspondence, reference, and altered material tends to happen in the following order in the recapitulation:

在再现部中，对应、参照与改动材料大体按以下顺序出现：

- correspondence bars (P and perhaps the beginning of TR or TR ⇒ FS)／对应小节（P，或许还有 TR 或 TR ⇒ FS 的开头）
- alterations or referential bars (primarily in TR or TR ⇒ FS)／改动或参照小节（主要在 TR 或 TR ⇒ FS 中）
- correspondence bars (S and C, and sometimes the material immediately preceding the MC, transposed to the home key)／对应小节（S 与 C，有时还有紧接 MC 之前的材料，移调到主调）

### Crux｜转折点

The point in the recapitulation at which alterations give way to renewed correspondence with the exposition is called the *crux*. This point, along with the MC and the ESC, must be determined before most of the rest of the analysis of the recapitulation can take place. Generally speaking, the crux will come before the beginning of S, with S and C comprised primarily of *correspondence bars*.

再现部中改动让位于与呈示部重新建立对应关系的那一点称为*转折点*（crux）。这一点连同 MC 与 ESC，必须在再现部其余大部分分析得以展开之前确定。一般而言，转折点会出现在 S 开始之前，而 S 与 C 主要由*对应小节*构成。

Typically there are no alterations *post-crux*, but when there are, they should not be overlooked in an analysis.

通常转折点之后没有改动，但若确实存在，分析时不应忽视。

## Hermeneutics｜诠释学

A sonata analysis only *begins* with the finding and labeling of these modules, keys, and events. Once you have successfully analyzed the sonata structure, move into interpretation (the scholarly term for the study of musical or textual interpretation is *hermeneutics*). Sonata hermeneutics begins with interpretive questions like:

奏鸣曲分析只是*始于*对这些模块、调性与事件的发现和标记。一旦成功分析出奏鸣曲结构，就应进入诠释（研究音乐或文本诠释的学术术语是*诠释学*）。奏鸣曲诠释学始于如下诠释性问题：

- What expectations does the exposition set up for the recapitulation (and other passages such as the development and, if present, coda)?／呈示部为再现部（以及展开部、若存在则还有尾声等其他段落）设定了哪些期待？
- How does the recapitulation fulfill those expectations?／再现部如何满足这些期待？
- How does the recapitulation thwart or mess with those expectations?／再现部如何阻挠或搅乱这些期待？
- What effects might the expected and unexpected elements in the recapitulation have on listeners?／再现部中预期与意外的要素可能对听者产生什么效果？
- How do the unique elements of the piece in question relate to other pieces in its historical context?／所论作品中的独特要素与其历史语境中的其他作品有何关联？

When writing, presenting, or discussing a sonata analysis, don’t simply catalog structure. Begin with questions like these, and provide structural details only in service of explaining your answers to those questions (and others that are raised by your engagement with the piece).

在写作、报告或讨论奏鸣曲分析时，不要仅仅罗列结构。应以这类问题为起点，并且只在解释你对这些问题的回答（以及与作品接触后被提出的其他问题）时，才提供结构细节。
