The exposition module of a sonata movement comes at or near the beginning of a sonata-form movement. Only an *introduction* (technically, a large [*prefix*](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/externalExpansions/)) might precede it. It exhibits exposition function, and thus has three jobs: *establish the home key*, *move to and establish a secondary key with a cadence*, and *lay out the thematic cycle* that will serve as the foundation for the development and recapitulation.

奏鸣曲乐章的呈示部模块位于奏鸣曲式乐章的开头或近开头处。唯有*引子*（严格说来，是一个大型的[*前缀*](/posts/music/form/phrase-rhythm/externalExpansions/)）可能出现在它之前。呈示部具备呈示功能，因而有三项任务：*确立主调*、*通过终止式转向并确立副调*，以及*铺陈主题循环*——后者将成为展开部与再现部的基础。

There are two main types of sonata expositions: the *two-part* exposition and the *continuous* exposition.

奏鸣曲呈示部主要有两种类型：*二部式*呈示部与*连续式*呈示部。

## The two-part exposition｜二部式呈示部

The two-part exposition is divided into two parts: the first part ends with the arrival of the *medial caesura* (MC, the major cadence, often accompanied by a musical pause, in the middle of the exposition), and the second part follows the MC and ends with the *essential expositional closure* (EEC, the major PAC in the secondary key) and any postcadential/closing material that follows it.

二部式呈示部分为两部分：第一部分以*中间终止*（MC，即呈示部中部的主要终止式，通常伴有音乐上的停顿）的到来而结束；第二部分接续 MC，以*必要呈示部收束*（EEC，即副调中的主要 PAC）以及其后的任何终止后／结束性材料结束。

A two-part exposition typically exhibits the following thematic cycle (in order):

二部式呈示部通常呈现如下的主题循环（按顺序）：

- **Primary theme (P)** – home key／**主部主题（P）**——主调
- **Transition (TR)** – ending with the *medial caesura (MC)*／**连接部（TR）**——以*中间终止（MC）*结束
- **Secondary theme (S)** – secondary key (V or III), ending with the *essential expositional closure (EEC)*／**副部主题（S）**——副调（V 或 III），以*必要呈示部收束（EEC）*结束
- **Closing space (C)** – secondary key; optional／**结束部空间（C）**——副调；可选
- **Retransition (RT)** – recapturing of dominant harmony in home key to prepare repeat of exposition; optional, and only occurs in a first ending／**回归过渡（RT）**——在主调中重新捕捉属和声，为呈示部的反复作准备；可选，且仅出现在第一结尾中

We can abbreviate this cycle:

我们可以把这个循环缩写为：

> **P TR ‘ S / C**

(The apostrophe stands for the MC; the slash stands for the EEC.)

（撇号代表 MC；斜线代表 EEC。）

Each of these terms is defined below.

这些术语将在下文逐一界定。

**A sample two-part exposition: W.A. Mozart, Sonata for piano in B-flat major, K. 333, I.**

**二部式呈示部样例：W. A. 莫扎特《降 B 大调钢琴奏鸣曲》K. 333，第一乐章**

### Multiple MCs｜多个中间终止

Sometimes, a composer sets up a two-part exposition, reaches a satisfactory MC, but the S theme that follows degenerates before it can achieve the EEC. In such a case, the degenerated S theme becomes like a new transition and leads into another MC, followed by an S theme that *can* reach a satisfactory EEC. Hepokoski and Darcy call such a situation a *trimodular block (TMB)*. We will just call it an exposition with two MCs.

有时，作曲家布置了一个二部式呈示部，抵达了一个令人满意的 MC，但其后的 S 主题在达到 EEC 之前便已瓦解。在这种情况下，瓦解了的 S 主题变得像一个新的连接部，引向另一个 MC，随后是一个*能够*达到令人满意 EEC 的 S 主题。赫普科夫斯基与达西称这种情况为*三模块组（TMB）*。我们则直接称之为带有两个 MC 的呈示部。

A two-part exposition with two MCs typically exhibits the following thematic cycle (in order):

带有两个 MC 的二部式呈示部通常呈现如下的主题循环（按顺序）：

- **Primary theme (P)** – home key／**主部主题（P）**——主调
- **Transition (TR)** – ending with the *medial caesura (MC)*／**连接部（TR）**——以*中间终止（MC）*结束
- **“Failed” secondary theme (S?)** that does not produce an EEC, but instead leads to a second MC／**“失败”的副部主题（S?）**——它不产生 EEC，而是引向第二个 MC
- **“Real” secondary theme (S)**, ending with the *essential expositional closure (EEC)*／**“真正”的副部主题（S）**——以*必要呈示部收束（EEC）*结束
- **Closing space (C)** – secondary key; optional／**结束部空间（C）**——副调；可选
- **Retransition (RT)** – recapturing of dominant harmony in home key to prepare repeat of exposition; optional, and only occurs in a first ending／**回归过渡（RT）**——在主调中重新捕捉属和声，为呈示部的反复作准备；可选，且仅出现在第一结尾中

We can abbreviate this cycle:

我们可以把这个循环缩写为：

> **P TR ‘ S? ‘ S / C**

The two MCs tend to adhere to one of the following patterns (the first being by far the most common; H/D, p. 171):

两个 MC 往往遵循以下模式之一（第一种最为常见；H/D，第 171 页）：

- I:HC followed by V:HC／I:HC 接 V:HC
- I:HC followed by V:PAC／I:HC 接 V:PAC
- V:HC followed by V:PAC／V:HC 接 V:PAC
- V:HC followed by V:HC／V:HC 接 V:HC

## The continuous exposition｜连续式呈示部

Unlike a two-part exposition, a continuous exposition has no MC followed by a secondary (S) theme. Instead, the transition (TR) module gives way to a “spinning out” of a series of related, fragmented melodic units. This succession of small, “spinning out” modules is called *Fortspinnung* (Ger., “spinning out”). These fragments are often, but not necessarily, taken from the primary (P) theme. *Fortspinnung* is often associated with TR in general, but in a continuous exposition, the process gets out of control and fails to produce a satisfactory MC. Instead, the motives continue to “spin out” and maintain a high level of energy right up to the EEC.

与二部式呈示部不同，连续式呈示部没有由 MC 引导的副部（S）主题。取而代之的是，连接（TR）模块让位于一系列彼此相关而零碎的旋律单位的“纺出”。这一连串小型的“纺出”模块称为*Fortspinnung*（德语，意为“纺出”）。这些片段往往——但并非必然——取自主部（P）主题。*Fortspinnung* 总体上常与 TR 相关联，但在连续式呈示部中，这一过程失去控制，未能产生令人满意的 MC。动机反而持续“纺出”，并把高度紧张的能量一直维持到 EEC。

The continous exposition follows the thematic cycle:

连续式呈示部遵循如下的主题循环：

- **Primary theme (P)** – home key／**主部主题（P）**——主调
- **Transition–Fortspinnung (TR=>FS)**, ending with the *essential expositional closure (EEC)*／**连接—纺出（TR=>FS）**——以*必要呈示部收束（EEC）*结束
- **Closing space (C)** – secondary key; optional／**结束部空间（C）**——副调；可选
- **Retransition (RT)** – recapturing of dominant harmony in home key to prepare repeat of exposition; optional, and only occurs in a first ending／**回归过渡（RT）**——在主调中重新捕捉属和声，为呈示部的反复作准备；可选，且仅出现在第一结尾中

We can abbreviate it:

我们可以把它缩写为：

> **P TR=>FS / C**

A continous exposition may present no cadences that could be “candidates” for an MC, it may suggest the possibility of an upcoming MC that is evaded, or it may present an MC that fails to produce a satisfactory S theme (and thus is not really an MC). In each case, an EEC is achieved without first arriving at an MC and an S theme.

连续式呈示部可能不呈现任何可充当 MC“候选者”的终止式；也可能暗示即将出现的 MC 却又加以规避；还可能呈现一个未能产生令人满意 S 主题的 MC（因而实际上并不是真正的 MC）。无论哪种情况，EEC 都在尚未先抵达 MC 与 S 主题的情况下得以实现。
