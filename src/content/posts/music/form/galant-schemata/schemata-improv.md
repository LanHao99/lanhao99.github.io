The [classical sentence](/posts/music/form/theme-types/sentence/) lends itself well to *galant* schemata. In fact, we have labeled many of the schemata on our [summary page](/posts/music/form/galant-schemata/schemataSummary/) as “presentation” or “continuation” schemata. Many four-stage schemata tend to appear with the melodic configuration common to the presentation phrase: a basic idea and its varied repetition.

[古典乐句式](/posts/music/form/theme-types/sentence/)很适合与*加兰特*程式配合。事实上，我们已在[总览页](/posts/music/form/galant-schemata/schemataSummary/)上把许多程式标记为"呈示"程式或"继续"程式。许多四阶段程式倾向于以呈示乐句常见的旋律配置出现：一个基本乐思及其变化重复。

Take the opening theme from Haydn’s Piano Sonata in G Major, Hob. XVI:27, third movement.

以海顿《G 大调钢琴奏鸣曲》Hob. XVI:27 第三乐章的开头主题为例。

[![海顿 Hob. XVI:27](/media/music/graphics/HaydnXVI27.png)](/media/music/graphics/HaydnXVI27.png)
*Public domain score excerpted from [IMSLP](http://www.imslp.org). Click image to enlarge.*

*公有领域乐谱，摘自 [IMSLP](http://www.imslp.org)。点击图片放大。*

Bars 1–4 (the presentation phrase) give an embellishment of the Meyer schema. Note the two-bar basic idea that is repeated in mm. 3–4, transposed up a step (for the most part).

第 1–4 小节（呈示乐句）是 Meyer 程式的一种加花装饰。注意那个两小节的基本乐思在第 3–4 小节被重复，并（大体上）向上移高一级。

Bars 5–6 (beginning the continuation phrase) give an embellishment of the modulating Passo Indietro schema. This is followed by a **II6 V7 I** cadence in the key of the dominant. (Of course, it goes by so fast, it can easily sound like a half cadence in the home key.)

第 5–6 小节（继续乐句的开头）是转调 Passo Indietro 程式的一种加花装饰。其后是属调上的 **II6 V7 I** 终止式。（当然，它进行得极快，很容易听起来像主调上的半终止。）

Our beginning improvisations will not be nearly as elaborate as Haydn’s composition, but note a few features that are helpful to mimic:

我们起步阶段的即兴不会像海顿的创作那样精巧，但请注意几个值得模仿的有用特征：

- The Meyer’s typical melody notes come on each downbeat (stage 1 – bar 1, etc.).／Meyer 程式典型的旋律音出现在每个强拍上（阶段 1 —— 第 1 小节，等等）。
- The Meyer’s typical melody notes for stages 1 and 3 also come at the end of the bar, immediately preceding their counterparts in bars 2 and 4.／Meyer 程式阶段 1 与阶段 3 的典型旋律音还出现在小节的末尾，紧接在其第 2 与第 4 小节中的对应音之前。
- Though the harmonic rhythm remains the same, the switch from presentation schema to continuation schema is matched by a change from two-bar melodic “chunks” to one-bar melodic chunks. (This is the *fragmentation* that is common to continuation phrases.)／尽管和声节奏保持不变，从呈示程式切换到继续程式，伴随着由两小节的旋律"块"变为一小节的旋律块。（这就是继续乐句中常见的*分裂*。）
- The continuation phrase follows well from the presentation phrase rhythmically and harmonically, but there is no obvious motivic connection between the two phrases.／继续乐句在节奏上与和声上都与呈示乐句衔接良好，但两个乐句之间并没有明显的动机联系。
- The cadence is relatively formulaic.／终止式相对程式化。

In those ways, this theme is a model example for our simple improvisations: highlight the schema’s typical melody notes on the downbeats and/or across the barline between mm. 1 and 2, mm. 3 and 4; use a more original melodic idea at the beginning and get more formulaic as you progress towards the final cadence; etc.

就这些方面而言，这个主题是我们简单即兴的范本：在强拍上和／或跨越第 1 与第 2 小节、第 3 与第 4 小节之间的小节线上突出程式的典型旋律音；在开头使用较为原创的旋律乐思，随着向最终终止式推进而变得越来越程式化；等等。

## Putting it together｜整合运用

The simplest way to build an entire sentence is to begin with one of the four-stage presentation schemata above, and then follow it with either a 5-stage Prinner (which ends on an IAC) or some kind of continuation–cadence combination. Following are a few examples. You can use these as skeletons upon which to base an improvisation, or you can embellish these interactive examples to try out composing a simple sentence. (See the [Using Trinket](/posts/music/technology/trinket/) page for details.)

构建一个完整乐句式最简单的方法，是先以上述某个四阶段呈示程式开头，然后接一个五阶段 Prinner（以不完全终止 IAC 结束），或接某种继续—终止式的组合。以下是几个例子。你可以把它们当作即兴的基础骨架，也可以在这些交互式例子上加花装饰，试着写作一个简单的乐句式。（详见[使用 Trinket](/posts/music/technology/trinket/) 页面。）

### Presentation – 5-phase Prinner｜呈示 – 五阶段 Prinner

The following framework begins with a Meyer and follows with a 5-stage Prinner. Note that stages 3 and 4 of the Prinner are compressed into a single bar in order to line up with the common 8-bar sentence length. Also note that any presentation schema can be substituted for the Meyer.

以下框架以 Meyer 开头，后接五阶段 Prinner。请注意，Prinner 的阶段 3 与阶段 4 被压缩进一个小节，以配合常见的八小节乐句式长度。还要注意，任何呈示程式都可以替换 Meyer。

### Presentation – Passo Indietro – Cadence｜呈示 – Passo Indietro – 终止式

Often classical composers would begin a Prinner, and then truncate it to make room for the final cadence. The following example does this, beginning with a Jupiter and progressing through a Passo Indietro (a half-Prinner) to a Compound HC.

古典作曲家常常先开始一个 Prinner，然后将其截断，为最终终止式腾出空间。下例即如此：以 Jupiter 开头，经过 Passo Indietro（半个 Prinner）推进到复合半终止（Compound HC）。

<iframe src="https://trinket.io/embed/music/ee80a49382" width="100%" height="300" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen></iframe>

By compressing the last two bars into a single bar, we can convert this into a PAC-ending sentence. (And combined, the two versions could form a 16-bar compound theme.)

把最后两个小节压缩成一个小节，我们就能把它转换为一个以完全终止（PAC）结束的乐句式。（而两版本组合起来，可以构成一个十六小节的复合主题。）

<iframe src="https://trinket.io/embed/music/bcc2fe55de" width="100%" height="300" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen></iframe>

### Presentation – Fast Prinner – Cadence｜呈示 – 快速 Prinner – 终止式

Instead of a Passo Indietro (half of a Prinner), we could use a full Prinner, but twice as fast. This allows the melody to move more in the latter half of the sentence, accomplishing the fragmentation and acceleration of melodic and harmonic rhythm that are common to continuation phrases.

除了使用 Passo Indietro（半个 Prinner），我们也可以使用一个完整的 Prinner，但速度加快一倍。这样旋律就能在乐句式的后半部分有更多运动，实现继续乐句中常见的那种分裂，以及旋律与和声节奏的加速。
