*Circle-of-fifths progression, minor mode*

**||: I – IV – VII – III :||**

Much more will be said about circle-of-fifths progressions in the context of classical music. In pop/rock music, we will focus on the above four-chord progression. This is considered a “descending” circle-of-fifths progression because each chord’s root moves down by fifth to the next root. In pop/rock, this progression often happens in minor beginning on I, moving to the relative major. Like the [“singer/songwriter” progression](/posts/music/pop-rock/harmony/popRockHarmony-sscp/), there is some key ambiguity in this progression, as the starting chord is easily considered tonic, but the motion from VII to III can easily be heard as V–I in the relative major key. And indeed, it can be used to move from the relative minor to the relative major.

关于五度圈进行，在古典音乐的语境中还会有多得多的论述。在流行与摇滚音乐中，我们将聚焦于上述四和弦进行。它被视为一种“下行”的五度圈进行，因为每个和弦的根音都下行五度到达下一个根音。在流行与摇滚中，这一进行常在小调中出现，从 I 开始，走向关系大调。与[“singer/songwriter”进行｜唱作人进行](/posts/music/pop-rock/harmony/popRockHarmony-sscp/)一样，这一进行存在某种调性上的模糊性，因为起始和弦很容易被视为主和弦，但从 VII 到 III 的进行又很容易被听成关系大调中的 V–I。事实上，它可用于从关系小调转向关系大调。

Def Leppard’s “Love Bites” (mildly explicit lyrics) begins with this four-chord progression repeated twice for the verse.

Def Leppard 的《Love Bites》（歌词略有露骨内容）以这一四和弦进行重复两次作为主歌开头。

The verse of Muse’s “Thoughts of a Dying Atheist” begins with a [lament progression](/posts/music/pop-rock/harmony/popRockHarmony-lament/), followed by a four-chord circle-of-fifths progression. This progression immediately repeats, returning to the initial minor key. However, the second time through, this lament–circle-of-fifths pattern leads to a chorus in the relative major (taking the III chord as the new tonic).

Muse 的《Thoughts of a Dying Atheist》的主歌以一个[哀歌进行｜lament progression](/posts/music/pop-rock/harmony/popRockHarmony-lament/)开始，随后是一个四和弦的五度圈进行。这一进行立即重复，回到最初的小调。然而在第二次经过时，这一哀歌—五度圈的模式导向了关系大调中的副歌（把 III 和弦当作新的主和弦）。
