*Harmony in pop/rock music*

Harmony in pop/rock music does not always follow the same norms and patterns of classical-era music. Thus, functional-bass notation does not work for all situations. Instead, we will primarily use Roman numerals for our analysis of harmony in pop/rock music, occasionally using functional bass as a supplement to help us make sense of a particular harmonic pattern or relate it to what we’ve studied in classical harmony.

流行与摇滚音乐中的和声并不总是遵循古典时期音乐的同一套规范与模式。因此，功能低音记谱法并非在所有情况下都适用。作为替代，我们在分析流行与摇滚音乐的和声时主要使用罗马数字，偶尔以功能低音作为补充，以帮助我们理解某一特定的和声模式，或将其与我们在古典和声中所学的内容联系起来。

One key difference between rock and classical harmony is that chords in pop/rock music are almost always root-position triads or seventh chords. This affects the “rules” of harmonic syntax, as 6/3 chords in classical progressions are replaced by 5/3 chords in pop/rock progressions. (For instance, the classical progression **IV–IV6** becomes **IV–VI**. The same bass line does the same work, but by using a 5/3 chord instead of a 6/3 chord, the functional progression changes to something that would “break the rules” of classical syntax.) This can make harmonic analysis a little tricky in pop/rock music, especially since there is no published theory of rock harmony that is equal to Quinn’s functional theory of classical harmony. However, it makes chord labeling and harmonic dictation simpler. Most of the time, all you need is the scale degree of the bass.

摇滚和声与古典和声的一个关键差异在于，流行与摇滚音乐中的和弦几乎总是原位三和弦或七和弦。这影响了和声句法的“规则”，因为古典进行中的 6/3 和弦在流行与摇滚进行中被 5/3 和弦所取代。（例如，古典进行 **IV–IV6** 变为 **IV–VI**。同样的低音线条起着同样的作用，但由于使用 5/3 和弦而非 6/3 和弦，功能进行变成了会“破坏规则”的、超出古典句法之外的东西。）这会使流行与摇滚音乐的和声分析略显棘手，尤其因为目前尚无一部可与 Quinn 的古典和声功能理论相媲美的、已出版的摇滚和声理论。不过，它使和弦标记与和声听写变得更加简单。大多数情况下，你所需要的只是低音的音级。

## Harmonic functions in minor｜小调中的和声功能

Harmonic functions in minor keys of pop/rock songs, particularly in the last couple decades, tend to deviate from classical syntax in different ways than major-key songs. See the [Harmonic functions in minor](https://openmusictheory.github.io/popRockHarmony-minor.html) resource for more details about this.

流行与摇滚歌曲小调中的和声功能，尤其在最近几十年里，往往以不同于大调歌曲的方式偏离古典句法。有关这一点的更多细节，参见 [Harmonic functions in minor｜小调中的和声功能](https://openmusictheory.github.io/popRockHarmony-minor.html) 资源。

## Schemata｜图式

There are a number of common stock chord progressions that recur in many pop/rock songs. Typically, these stock progressions, or schemata, will occur in cyclical patterns; that is, the same progression will repeat multiple times in a row. This is particularly common in choruses of verse-chorus songs, but also happens in verses, strophes, and bridges. This is helpful for identifying harmonies by ear, since in addition to listening for bass scale degrees and considering whether the harmonies are chords of the fifth (5/3 or 7) or chords of the sixth (typically 6/3 or 6/5), we can listen for common patterns that we’ve heard in other songs. Following are a number of common schemata for pop/rock harmonic progressions.

有许多常见的惯用和弦进行在许多流行与摇滚歌曲中反复出现。通常，这些惯用进行，即图式，会以循环的模式出现；也就是说，同一进行会连续重复多次。这在主歌—副歌歌曲的副歌中尤为常见，但在主歌、分节歌与桥段中也会出现。这对于凭听觉辨认和声颇有帮助，因为除了聆听低音音级、并考虑这些和声是五度和弦（5/3 或 7）还是六度和弦（通常为 6/3 或 6/5）之外，我们还可以聆听在其他歌曲中听过的常见模式。以下列出若干流行与摇滚和声进行的常见图式。

## Rock’s “tonal systems”｜摇滚的“调性系统”

Walter Everett has posited six *tonal systems* to which most pop/rock songs belong. The following sections summarize four of these with examples (all but 1 and 6—the most and least tonal). See [Everett’s 2004 article in *Music Theory Online*](http://www.mtosmt.org/issues/mto.04.10.4/mto.04.10.4.w_everett.html) for a more detailed exploration of these “systems.”

Walter Everett 提出了六种*调性系统*，大多数流行与摇滚歌曲都归属于其中。以下各节概述了其中四种并附示例（第 1 种与第 6 种除外——即最具调性与最不具调性的两种）。关于这些“系统”更详细的探讨，参见 [Everett’s 2004 article in *Music Theory Online*｜Everett 2004 年发表于《Music Theory Online》的文章](http://www.mtosmt.org/issues/mto.04.10.4/mto.04.10.4.w_everett.html)。

System 1: Pop/rock tonality that strongly resembles common-practice classical tonality (major and minor keys, with parallel-key borrowing).／系统 1：与共性写作时期的古典调性高度相似的流行／摇滚调性（大调与小调，并带有同主音调的借用）。

[System 2: Pop/rock tonality that draws on diatonic modes](https://openmusictheory.github.io/popRockHarmony-EverettSystem2.html) (such as Dorian, Mixolydian, Aeolian).／系统 2：汲取自然调式的流行／摇滚调性（如多利亚、混合利底亚、爱奥尼亚调式）。

[System 3: Pop/rock tonality that strongly resembles common-practice classical tonality, but which borrows from diatonic modes.](https://openmusictheory.github.io/popRockHarmony-EverettSystem3.html)／系统 3：与共性写作时期的古典调性高度相似，但借用自然调式的流行／摇滚调性。

[System 4: Blues-based pop/rock tonality.](https://openmusictheory.github.io/popRockHarmony-EverettSystem4.html)／系统 4：以布鲁斯为基础的流行／摇滚调性。

[System 5: Minor-pentatonic-based pop/rock tonality.](https://openmusictheory.github.io/popRockHarmony-EverettSystem5.html)／系统 5：以小调五声音阶为基础的流行／摇滚调性。

System 6: Chromatically inflected minor-pentatonic-based pop/rock tonality.／系统 6：带有半音变化的、以小调五声音阶为基础的流行／摇滚调性。
