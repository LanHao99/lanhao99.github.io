*Form in pop/rock music – Formal containers and module structures*

## Formal Containers｜形式容器

### Phrase｜乐句

A phrase is a musical unit that typically lasts for four bars and includes one line of poetry for its lyrical content. Phrases are designated by lower-case letters.

乐句是一种音乐单位，通常持续四小节，其歌词内容包含一行诗句。乐句以小写字母标示。

### Module｜模块

In pop/rock music, a *module* typically spans between 8 and 24 bars and includes 2–4 phrases. (Some auxiliary modules may contain a single phrase.) A module presents a single [formal function](/posts/music/pop-rock/form/popRockForm-functions/) (such as A, B, C, V, P, etc.) and presents a complete 2-, 3-, or 4-part pattern. Modules typically set a stanza of lyrics.

在流行／摇滚音乐中，一个*模块*通常跨越 8 至 24 小节，包含 2–4 个乐句。（某些辅助模块可能只含一个乐句。）一个模块呈现单一的[形式功能](/posts/music/pop-rock/form/popRockForm-functions/)（如 A、B、C、V、P 等），并呈现完整的二部、三部或四部图式。模块通常为一段诗节配乐。

Module boundaries are usually made apparent by poetic structure (end of a group of rhyming lines—couplet or stanza) or surface features of the song (clear rhythmic, harmonic, and melodic arrival; change in instrumentation or volume; return to beginning of a previously heard module; etc.).

模块的边界通常由诗歌结构（一组押韵行的结束——对句或诗节）或歌曲的表层特征（清晰的节奏、和声与旋律到达；配器或音量的变化；回到先前听过的模块的开头；等等）显现出来。

For instance, take the transition from a *verse* module to a *chorus* module at 2:42 in U2’s “Pride (In the Name of Love).” The module boundary is delineated by a number of features simultaneously:

例如，以 U2《Pride (In the Name of Love)》在 2:42 处从*主歌*模块到*副歌*模块的过渡为例。模块边界同时由若干特征勾勒出来：

- The text closes out the verse’s quatrain with a (more-or-less) rhyming lyric (“sky”–”pride”) before beginning a new stanza.／歌词以一句（大体上）押韵的词句（“sky”–“pride”）收束主歌的四行诗节，随后才开创新的诗节。
- The end of the verse is signaled by a drum fill, a common end-of-phrase or end-of-module gesture.／主歌的结束由一段鼓花标示，这是常见的乐句末尾或模块末尾手法。
- The general dynamic gets louder very quickly.／整体力度迅速增强。
- The guitar becomes more active, and is doubled by a second guitar part.／吉他变得更加活跃，并由第二吉他声部叠奏。
- The lead vocals rise in register.／主唱的音区升高。
- Background vocals are added to the lead vocal part.／为主唱声部加入背景人声。

All of these features help delineate the boundary between modules, and most of them also give the new module (the chorus) a higher eneregy level than the previous module (the verse).

所有这些特征都有助于勾勒模块之间的边界，其中大多数还使新模块（副歌）比前一模块（主歌）具有更高的能量水平。

*All module definitions are based on Jason Summach’s (2012) dissertation, “Form in Top-20 Rock Music, 1955–89.”*

*所有模块定义均基于 Jason Summach（2012）的博士论文《Form in Top-20 Rock Music, 1955–89》。*

### Primary module｜主要模块

“A module that contains a song’s principal materials” (Summach, p. 322), such as the title text or the most memorable or climactic music. Primary modules exhibit strophe function (A) or chorus function (C). See [Formal functions](/posts/music/pop-rock/form/popRockForm-functions/) for more details on these specific functions.

“包含一首歌主要材料的模块”（Summach，第 322 页），如标题文本，或最令人难忘、最具高潮性的音乐。主要模块呈现诗节功能（A）或副歌功能（C）。关于这些具体功能的更多细节，参见[形式功能](/posts/music/pop-rock/form/popRockForm-functions/)。

### Secondary module｜次要模块

A secondary module is a core module that creates a contrast with the primary module or draws attention to it (see Summach, p. 322). Secondary modules exhibit bridge function (B), verse function (V), prechorus function (P), or postchorus function (Z).

次要模块是与主要模块构成对比、或将注意力引向主要模块的核心模块（参见 Summach，第 322 页）。次要模块呈现桥段功能（B）、主歌功能（V）、预副歌功能（P）或后副歌功能（Z）。

### Core module｜核心模块

Core modules form the main musical and poetic content of a song. All primary and secondary modules are considered core modules. (See Summach, p. 321.)

核心模块构成一首歌的主要音乐与诗歌内容。所有主要模块与次要模块均被视为核心模块。（参见 Summach，第 321 页。）

### Auxiliary module｜辅助模块

Auxiliary modules help frame the core modules, introducing them, providing temporary relief from them, or winding down from them. They exhibit introduction function (I), outro function (O), or coda function (X). (See Summach, p. 321.)

辅助模块有助于框定核心模块：引入它们、为它们提供暂时的舒缓，或使它们逐渐收束。它们呈现前奏功能（I）、尾奏功能（O）或尾声功能（X）。（参见 Summach，第 321 页。）

### Cycle｜循环

“The characteristic succession of modules in a song form, ending with the song’s primary module type” (Summach, p. 321). A cycle contains one or more modules, typically in the same order (though sometimes with one or more modules omitted, especially toward the end of a song). Curly brackets {} are used to refer to cycles (to differentiate them from names of song form types).

“歌曲形式中模块的特征性连续，并以歌曲的主要模块类型结束”（Summach，第 321 页）。一个循环包含一个或多个模块，通常按相同顺序排列（尽管有时会省略一个或多个模块，尤其是在接近歌曲结尾处）。花括号 {} 用于指称循环（以区别于歌曲形式类型的名称）。

A strophic song’s cycle is {A}. Since there is only one module in a strophic cycle, analysis of the cycles is trivial and can be easily passed over.

分节歌的循环为 {A}。由于分节歌的循环中只有一个模块，对循环的分析无关紧要，可以轻松略过。

A 32-bar song’s cycle is typically {AABA}, though abbreviated cycles are common, especially later in the song. “I Want to Hold Your Hand” is a typical example, where a complete {AABA} cycle is followed by an abbreviated {BA} cycle (see [Pop/rock form overview](/posts/music/pop-rock/form/popRockForm/) for a detailed explanation):

三十二小节歌曲的循环通常为 {AABA}，但缩减的循环也很常见，尤其是在歌曲后段。《I Want to Hold Your Hand》是典型例子：一个完整的 {AABA} 循环之后跟随一个缩减的 {BA} 循环（详细说明参见[流行／摇滚曲式概览](/posts/music/pop-rock/form/popRockForm/)）：

> {AABA}{BA}

A verse-chorus song’s main cycle is typically {VC} or {VPC}. When a bridge occurs in the song, it often replaces V or VP. Postchoruses (Z) can also follow the chorus.

主歌—副歌歌曲的主要循环通常为 {VC} 或 {VPC}。当歌曲中出现桥段时，它常取代 V 或 VP。后副歌（Z）也可以跟在副歌之后。

Bon Jovi’s “Livin’ on a Prayer” (discussed in the [Pop/rock form overview](/posts/music/pop-rock/form/popRockForm/)) contains a moderately complex set of cycles:

Bon Jovi 的《Livin’ on a Prayer》（在[流行／摇滚曲式概览](/posts/music/pop-rock/form/popRockForm/)中讨论）包含一组中等复杂的循环：

> I {VPC} I {VPCZ} {CiPC} O

Note the use of a mid-song introduction between cycles, and the use of an instrumental chorus (guitar solo) to begin the third cycle, where we might also expect a bridge. The outro is chorus-based, simply a repetition of the chorus ― an example of chorusification.

请注意循环之间使用了曲中前奏，以及以器乐副歌（吉他独奏）开始第三个循环——在那里我们可能也会预期出现桥段。尾奏以副歌为基础，只是副歌的重复——这是副歌化的一个例子。

## Module structures｜模块结构

### Two-part (aa’)｜二部（aa’）

A module is two-part when the phrases that make up the module can be grouped into a first half and a second half. In two-part modules, the second half is usually based on the same music as the first half, and thus it is labeled *aa’*. Often these two halves begin the same but have different endings, participating in an antecedent–consequent (weak → strong) relationship.

当构成模块的乐句可以被归为前半与后半时，该模块即为二部模块。在二部模块中，后半通常基于与前半相同的音乐，因而标记为 *aa’*。这两半常常开头相同而结尾不同，构成先行—后续（弱 → 强）的关系。

The chorus to “Livin’ on a Prayer” has an *a a’* structure. The first four-bar phrase (“Oh, we’re half-way there…”) and the second four-bar phrase (“Take my hand…”) have identical melody and harmony (hence the *a*), but different lyrics (hence the *prime*). Note that in many songs, this relationship is not as clear cut. However, if the two phrases *begin* with *similar* musical material, give them the same letter. New lyrics, new musical endings, or musical variations simply warrant a “prime.”

《Livin’ on a Prayer》的副歌具有 *a a’* 结构。第一个四小节乐句（“Oh, we’re half-way there…”）与第二个四小节乐句（“Take my hand…”）具有完全相同的旋律与和声（故为 *a*），但歌词不同（故加*撇号*）。请注意，在许多歌曲中，这种关系并没有这么分明。然而，若两个乐句以*相似*的音乐材料*开始*，就赋予它们相同的字母。新歌词、新的音乐结尾或音乐上的变化，只足以加上一个“撇号”。

### Two-part – ab｜二部——ab

Very rarely a module’s phrases can be grouped into two clear halves based on different music. Such a module is labeled *ab*.

模块的乐句极少能依据不同的音乐被归为两个清晰的部分。这样的模块标记为 *ab*。

### Three-part – aa’b｜三部——aa’b

A module containing three phrases is a three-part module. If the first two phrases are based on the same music, the module is labeled *aa’b*.

包含三个乐句的模块是三部模块。若前两个乐句基于相同的音乐，该模块标记为 *aa’b*。

[12-bar blues progressions](/posts/music/pop-rock/harmony/popRockHarmony-blues/) are the most common example of a three-part *aa’b* module. “Hound Dog” (on the blues-progression page) contains aa’b strophes.

[十二小节布鲁斯进行](/posts/music/pop-rock/harmony/popRockHarmony-blues/)是三部 *aa’b* 模块最常见的例子。《Hound Dog》（见布鲁斯进行页面）含有 aa’b 诗节。

### Three-part – abb’｜三部——abb’

If the second and third phrases in a three-part module are based on the same music, the module is labeled *abb’*.

若三部模块中的第二与第三个乐句基于相同的音乐，该模块标记为 *abb’*。

### Four-part – srdc｜四部——srdc

A module composed of four phrases often contains a [sentential structure](/posts/music/form/theme-types/sentence/) (presentation → continuation → cadential/conclusion). In pop/rock music, this often appears as a basic musical idea in the first phrase, a repetition or “response” to it in the second, contrasting material in the third phrase (often employing fragmentation, acceleration of harmonic rhythm, and movement away from tonic harmony), and a conclusion in the fourth phrase ― either with a return to the basic idea and tonic harmony or with still newer material that forms a strong melodic, rhythmic, and harmonic conclusion. Walter Everett has called such a four-phrase sentential structure in pop/rock music *srdc* (statement, restatement/response, departure, conclusion).

由四个乐句构成的模块常常包含[乐句式结构](/posts/music/form/theme-types/sentence/)（呈示 → 继续 → 终止式／收束）。在流行／摇滚音乐中，这常常表现为：第一个乐句是基本音乐构思，第二个乐句是对它的重复或“应答”，第三个乐句是对比材料（常常运用碎片化、和声节奏加速以及离开主调和声），第四个乐句是收束——或返回基本构思与主调和声，或使用更新的材料，构成强有力的旋律、节奏与和声收束。Walter Everett 将流行／摇滚音乐中这种四乐句的乐句式结构称为 *srdc*（陈述 statement、重述／应答 restatement/response、离题 departure、收束 conclusion）。

In conventional lettering, an *srdc* module could employ an *aaba* structure (with statement material returning as a restatement and again as the conclusion), or *aabc* structure (where the conclusion material is new). Occasionally *abcd* or *abca* are possible, but only if *b* is a clear response to *a*, not simply new material.

按传统字母标记法，一个 *srdc* 模块可以采用 *aaba* 结构（陈述材料作为重述再次出现，并再次作为收束出现），或 *aabc* 结构（其中收束材料是新的）。偶尔也可能出现 *abcd* 或 *abca*，但仅当 *b* 是对 *a* 的明确应答，而非单纯的新材料时才成立。

*srdc* structures tend to divide neatly into halves: *sr* and *dc*.

*srdc* 结构倾向于整齐地分为两半：*sr* 与 *dc*。

Carl Perkins’s “Blue Suede Shoes” contains a clear *srdc* structure in its second strophe (0:19; find a recording and complete module analysis on the [Pop/rock form overview](/posts/music/pop-rock/form/popRockForm/)). It contains four four-bar phrases (following a [16-bar blues](/posts/music/pop-rock/harmony/popRockHarmony-blues/) structure). The first two phrases (statement–restatement) contain the same harmony (tonic prolongation), melodies which begin identically, and though the lyrics differ, the rhythmic and rhyme schemes are the same. The third phrase (departure) brings a new melody, new harmony (move to the subdominant), and the title lyrics (this is a refrain shared with other strophes in the song). The final phrase (conclusion) closes out the poetic unit and the blues harmonic progression.

Carl Perkins 的《Blue Suede Shoes》在其第二个诗节（0:19；录音与完整的模块分析可在[流行／摇滚曲式概览](/posts/music/pop-rock/form/popRockForm/)中找到）中包含清晰的 *srdc* 结构。它包含四个四小节乐句（遵循[十六小节布鲁斯](/posts/music/pop-rock/harmony/popRockHarmony-blues/)结构）。前两个乐句（陈述—重述）包含相同的和声（主和声延续）、开头相同的旋律，且尽管歌词不同，节奏与韵式却相同。第三个乐句（离题）带来新的旋律、新的和声（转向下属方向）以及标题歌词（这是与歌曲中其他诗节共有的叠句）。最后一个乐句（收束）收束了诗歌单位与布鲁斯和声进行。
