*Harmonic functions*

If a musical function describes the role that a particular musical element plays in the creation of a larger musical unit, then *a harmonic function describes the role that a particular chord plays in the creating of a larger harmonic progression*. Each chord tends to occur in some musical situations more than others, to progress to some chords more than others. These tendencies work together to create meaningful harmonic progressions, which can in turn form the harmonic foundation for musical *phrases*, *themes*, and larger formal units.

如果说音乐功能描述的是某一音乐元素在构建更大音乐单位时所扮演的角色，那么*和声功能描述的是某一特定和弦在构建更大和声进行时所扮演的角色*。每个和弦在某些音乐情境中出现的频率高于其他情境，进行到某些和弦的频率也高于其他和弦。这些倾向共同作用，形成有意义的和声进行，而这些进行又构成音乐*乐句*、*主题*以及更大曲式单位的和声基础。

Generally speaking, the function of a chord concerns the notes that belong to it (its *internal characteristics*), the chords that tend to precede and follow it, and where it tends to be employed in the course of a musical phrase.

一般而言，一个和弦的功能涉及属于它的音（其*内部特征*）、倾向于出现在它之前和之后的和弦，以及它在一个音乐乐句的进程中倾向于被用在何处。

A theory of harmonic functions is based on three fundamental principles:

和声功能理论基于三条基本原理：

- Chords are collections of scale degrees.／和弦是音级的集合。
- Each scale degree has its own tendencies.／每个音级都有自身的倾向。
- The collective tendencies of a chord’s scale degrees in combination is the chord’s function.／一个和弦各音级倾向的集合，即该和弦的功能。

(Note the absence of *root* and *quality* from consideration here.)

（请注意，这里并未把*根音*和*性质*纳入考虑。）

Because [tendency is style-specific](/posts/music/voice-leading/four-voice/tendency/), the same chord can have different functions in different musical styles. For instance, the kinds of functions we find in classical music are different from those we find in pop/rock songs from the Billboard charts. And though there are some general harmonic traits that are common to most eighteenth- and nineteenth-century Western composers (what we call the “common practice”), when we look in closer detail, we find some significant differences in the way Bach, Mozart, Brahms, and others compose their harmonic progressions.

由于[tendency is style-specific｜倾向具有风格特定性](/posts/music/voice-leading/four-voice/tendency/)，同一个和弦在不同音乐风格中可以具有不同的功能。例如，我们在古典音乐中看到的功能种类，与我们在《公告牌》榜单上的流行／摇滚歌曲中看到的不同。尽管存在某些为大多数 18、19 世纪西方作曲家所共有的一般和声特征（我们称之为“共性写作”），但当我们更细致地观察时，会发现巴赫、莫扎特、勃拉姆斯等人在写作和声进行的方式上存在一些显著差异。

Our initial exploration of harmonic functions will engage the general “common practice” that is shared by most eighteenth- and nineteenth-century Western composers. As we explore specific genres, composers, and works within that common practice, we will have opportunity to explore the more nuanced differences between composers, as well as to move beyond common-practice Western art music to include other styles, such as pop/rock.

我们对和声功能的初步探讨将涉及大多数 18、19 世纪西方作曲家所共有的总体“共性写作”实践。随着我们在这一共性写作实践内部探究具体的体裁、作曲家与作品，我们将有机会考察作曲家之间更为细微的差别，并超越共性写作时期的西方艺术音乐，纳入诸如流行／摇滚等其他风格。

## The three common-practice harmonic functions｜共性写作时期的三大和声功能

In common-practice music, harmonies tend to cluster around three high-level categories of harmonic function. These categories are traditionally called *tonic* (**T**), *subdominant* (**S** — also called *predominant*, **P** or **PD**), and *dominant* (**D**). Each of these functions has their own characteristic scale degrees, with their own characteristic tendencies. And each of these functions tend to participate in certain kinds of chord progressions more than others.

在共性写作音乐中，和声倾向于聚集成三个高层次的和声功能类别。这些类别传统上称为*主功能*（**T**）、*下属功能*（**S**——也称*属前功能*，**P** 或 **PD**）和*属功能*（**D**）。每个功能都有其自身的特征音级，以及各自特有的倾向。而且每个功能都倾向于参与某些类型的和弦进行，多于参与其他类型。

If you are already comfortable with Roman numerals, you can generally think of **I**, **III**, and **VI** as *tonic*, **II** and **IV** as *subdominant*, and **V** and **VII** as *dominant*. (Though, as you will see below, there is more to it than that.)

如果你已经熟悉罗马数字，一般可以把 **I**、**III**、**VI** 视为*主功能*，把 **II**、**IV** 视为*下属功能*，把 **V**、**VII** 视为*属功能*。（不过，如下文所示，实际情况远比这复杂。）

To visualize these functional categories, think of the usual triads in C major arranged on a circle of thirds. Note that each chord sits between the two triads that share the most tones in common — C major (C, E, G) sits between E minor (E, G, B) and A minor (A, C, E), both of which share two tones in common with C.

为了直观呈现这些功能类别，可以把 C 大调中常用的三和弦设想为排列在一个三度圈上。注意每个和弦都位于与它共同音最多的两个三和弦之间——C 大三和弦（C, E, G）位于 e 小三和弦（E, G, B）与 a 小三和弦（A, C, E）之间，后两者都与 C 共有两个音。

[![C 大调的自然音和弦排列在三度圈上](/media/music/graphics/harmony/circleOfThirds-leadSheet.png)](/media/music/graphics/harmony/circleOfThirds-leadSheet.png)

Convert these chords to Roman numerals (in C major), and we can see the functions. Since the function is determined by the tendencies of the tones that they share, and since on this graph chords are grouped together by notes they have in common, they are also grouped together by function.

把这些和弦转换为（C 大调中的）罗马数字，我们就能看到功能。由于功能取决于它们所共有音的倾向，而在这一图表中和弦又是按共同音分组的，因此它们同时也按功能分组。

[![大调的自然音和弦以罗马数字表示，排列在三度圈上并标出和声功能](/media/music/graphics/harmony/circleOfThirds-functions.png)](/media/music/graphics/harmony/circleOfThirds-functions.png)

*Triads arranged on the circle of thirds, labeled by harmonic functions.*

*三和弦排列于三度圈上，以和声功能标注。*

Interestingly, in common-practice music, a chord’s function can be determined solely by its internal characteristics (the notes that make up the chord). This is not true of all styles. For example, in pop/rock music a **IV** chord can exhibit very different functional tendencies depending on its context. But in classical music, simply knowing the notes in a chord is enough to determine its general harmonic function and the general tendencies of that chord and its individual notes.

有趣的是，在共性写作音乐中，一个和弦的功能可以仅由其内部特征（构成该和弦的音）决定。这并非对所有风格都成立。例如在流行／摇滚音乐中，**IV** 和弦会依语境不同而表现出非常不同的功能倾向。但在古典音乐中，仅知道一个和弦包含哪些音，就足以确定它的一般和声功能，以及该和弦及其各个音的一般倾向。

The syntactic properties of these functions will be covered elsewhere. What follows simply explains how to determine the function of a chord in common-practice music with greater specificity.

这些功能的句法属性将在别处讨论。以下只解释如何更精确地判定共性写作音乐中和弦的功能。

## Finding the function of a chord｜判定和弦的功能

Each of the three harmonic functions — *tonic* (T), *subdominant* (S), and *dominant* (D) — have characteristic scale degrees. Tonic’s characteristic scale degrees are 1, 3, 5, 6, and 7. Subdominant’s characteristic scale degrees are 1, 2, 3, 4, and 6. Dominant’s characteristic scale degrees are 2, 4, 5, 6, and 7.

三大和声功能——*主功能*（T）、*下属功能*（S）与*属功能*（D）——各有其特征音级。主功能的特征音级是 1、3、5、6、7。下属功能的特征音级是 1、2、3、4、6。属功能的特征音级是 2、4、5、6、7。

Ian Quinn (a music theorist at Yale University) further distinguishes these scale degrees, using the categories of functional *triggers*, functional *associates*, and functional *dissonances*. These categories help us understand the functional properties of chords whose scale degrees belong to more than one function, as well as how certain notes behave within a chord. They also help us understand which scale degrees are more or less characteristic of a function ― something that will help determine function when a complete chord is not present.

Ian Quinn（耶鲁大学音乐理论家）进一步区分这些音级，使用功能*触发音*、功能*关联音*与功能*不协和音*这几个类别。这些类别有助于我们理解那些音级归属于不止一个功能的和弦所具有的功能属性，也有助于理解某些音在和弦中的行为方式。它们还有助于我们理解哪些音级对某一功能而言更具特征性或更不具特征性——当和弦不完整时，这有助于判定功能。

| function: 功能 | triggers: 触发音 | associates: 关联音 | dissonances: 不协和音 |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **T** | 1 and 3 | 5 and 6 | 5 (if 6 is also present) and 7 |
| **S** | 4 and 6 | 1 and 2 | 1 (if 2 is also present) and 3 |
| **D** | 5 and 7 | 2 | 4 and 6 |

In terms of moveable-*do* solfège:

用首调唱名法表示：

| function: 功能 | triggers: 触发音 | associates: 关联音 | dissonances: 不协和音 |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **T** | *do* and *mi*/*me* | *sol* and *la*/*le* | *sol* (if *la*/*le* is also present) and *ti*/*te* |
| **S** | *fa* and *la*/*le* | *do* and *re* | *do* (if *re* is also present) and *mi*/*me* |
| **D** | *sol* and *ti*/*te* | *re* | *fa* and *la*/*le* |

To determine the function of a chord, find the function that includes all the scale degrees of a chord (regardless of chromatic alterations ― that is, treat ♯4 the same as regular scale-degree 4). If more than one function contains all the scale degrees, take the function with the most triggers in the chord.

要判定一个和弦的功能，先找出包含该和弦全部音级的那个功能（不管半音变化——也就是说，把 ♯4 与普通的 4 级音同样看待）。若不止一个功能包含全部这些音级，则取该和弦中触发音最多的那个功能。

There is one exception to this (for now): a chord with scale degrees 6, 1, and 3 is a special kind of tonic chord, called a *destabilized tonic*. Quinn uses the special functional label is **Tx**, rather than simply **T**, for this chord.

目前有一个例外：包含 6、1、3 三个音级的和弦是一种特殊的主功能和弦，称为*失稳主和弦*。Quinn 为这一和弦使用特殊的功能标记 **Tx**，而不只是 **T**。

Also note that because the III7 chord’s scale-degrees do not wholly belong to any of the three functions, it can behave similar to **T** and **D** chords, depending on context. It is a rare chord in its diatonic form.

还要注意，由于 III7 和弦的各音级并不完全属于三大功能中的任何一个，依语境不同，它可以表现得类似 **T** 和弦或 **D** 和弦。它以其自然音形式出现时是罕见的和弦。

[This handout](/media/music/graphics/Handouts/HarmoniesByBassScaleDegree.pdf) will help you determine the function of a chord from the bass scale degree and/or the Roman numeral.

[This handout｜本讲义](/media/music/graphics/Handouts/HarmoniesByBassScaleDegree.pdf) 将帮助你依据低音音级和／或罗马数字判定一个和弦的功能。

## Labeling chords｜标注和弦

There are two ways in which we will label chords according to function. The first is to label chords with Roman numerals, thoroughbass figures, and functional labels. When doing so, place the appropriate Roman numeral *below* the bass line, the thoroughbass figure *above* the bass line (since it represents the upper voices), and place a functional label **T**/**S**/**D** below the Roman numeral (no **Tx**; simply call a **VI** chord **T**). For now this label can simply apply T, S, or D to individual chords; in the future, we will alter this practice slightly in order to show [functional prolongation](/posts/music/harmony/harmonicSyntax2/). The first example shows individual chord functions, and the second example shows functional prolongation.

我们将以两种方式按功能标注和弦。第一种是用罗马数字、数字低音标记以及功能标记来标注和弦。这样做时，把相应的罗马数字写在低音声部线*下方*，把数字低音标记写在低音线*上方*（因为它代表上方各声部），并在罗马数字下方写功能标记 **T**／**S**／**D**（不用 **Tx**；**VI** 和弦直接称为 **T**）。目前这一标记可以只是把 T、S 或 D 用于单个和弦；今后我们会稍作调整，以显示 [functional prolongation｜功能延伸](/posts/music/harmony/harmonicSyntax2/)。第一个例子显示单个和弦的功能，第二个例子显示功能延伸。

[![各和弦的功能标注](/media/music/graphics/harmony/RNsIndividualFunctions.png)](/media/music/graphics/harmony/RNsIndividualFunctions.png)

[![功能延伸的标注](/media/music/graphics/harmony/RNsFunctionalProlongation.png)](/media/music/graphics/harmony/RNsFunctionalProlongation.png)

The second way to label a harmonic progression is what Quinn calls *functional bass*. Functional bass symbols combine a chord’s function (**T**, **S**, **D**, or **Tx**) with an Arabic numeral denoting the scale degree of its bass note. A tonic chord with *do* in the bass is **T1**, a dominant chord with *ti* in the bass is **D7**, etc. If the bass note is chromatically altered, use a **+** or **–** to denote raised or lowered (*la* and *ti* in minor do not count, since *le*, *la*, *te*, and *ti* all belong to minor, but you can use +/– for clarity if you like). And if there is a chromatically altered note anywhere in the chord, put the functional bass symbol inside square brackets: **[S6]**, **[S+4]**, **[T–7]**, etc. (See [Chromatically altered subdominant chords](/posts/music/harmony/alteredSubdominants/), [Applied chords](/posts/music/harmony/appliedChords/), and [Modal mixture](/posts/music/harmony/modalMixture/) for more information on common chromatically altered chords.)

标注和声进行的第二种方式是 Quinn 所称的*功能低音*。功能低音符号把一个和弦的功能（**T**、**S**、**D** 或 **Tx**）与表示其低音音级的阿拉伯数字结合起来。低音为 *do* 的主功能和弦写作 **T1**，低音为 *ti* 的属功能和弦写作 **D7**，依此类推。若低音经过半音变化，用 **+** 或 **–** 表示升高或降低（小调中的 *la* 和 *ti* 不计入，因为 *le*、*la*、*te*、*ti* 都属于小调；不过如果你愿意，为清晰起见也可使用 +/–）。若和弦中任何位置有半音变化音，则把功能低音符号置于方括号内：**[S6]**、**[S+4]**、**[T–7]** 等。（关于常见半音变化和弦的更多信息，参见 [Chromatically altered subdominant chords｜半音变化的下属和弦](/posts/music/harmony/alteredSubdominants/)、[Applied chords｜借用和弦](/posts/music/harmony/appliedChords/) 与 [Modal mixture｜调式交替](/posts/music/harmony/modalMixture/)。）

[![功能低音的各类功能](/media/music/graphics/harmony/FunctionalBassFunctions.png)](/media/music/graphics/harmony/FunctionalBassFunctions.png)

Quinn also advocates using what I call *interpreted functional bass*. This nomenclature uses the same symbols, but uses parentheses to denote [*contrapuntal prolongation*](http://openmusictheory.com/harmonicSyntax2.html) and lower-case postscripts to explain the contrapuntal role of the embellishing chord (p for passing, n for neighbor, i for incomplete neighbor, d for divider, e for embellishing — all of these refer to the voice-leading pattern in the bass voice). Following is an example of interpreted functional bass.

Quinn 还提倡使用我称之为*解释性功能低音*的做法。这一命名法使用同样的符号，但用括号表示 [*contrapuntal prolongation*｜*对位性延伸*](http://openmusictheory.com/harmonicSyntax2.html)，并用小写下标说明装饰性和弦的对位作用（p 为经过音，n 为邻音，i 为不完全邻音，d 为分割音，e 为装饰——这些都指低音声部中的声部进行模式）。下面是一个解释性功能低音的例子。

[![解释性功能低音](/media/music/graphics/harmony/InterpretedFunctionalBass.png)](/media/music/graphics/harmony/InterpretedFunctionalBass.png)

In this text, we primarily use the first method of Roman numerals and (prolonged) harmonic functions, since it is the most common in North American music theory. However, functional bass can be helpful for identifying categories of chords that belong together. For example, in a dictation or transcription task, we might hear *re* in the bass but not know what specific chord it is. If context tells us it is likely a dominant chord, rather than subdominant, we can label it **D2**. This rules out **II** (a subdominant chord) but keeps open multiple dominant options like **V6/4** or **VII6** until we are able to make a final determination. Similarly, when composing, there are patterns that might take an **S4**, with the specific chord (**IV** or **II6**) determined by voice-leading rather than harmonic syntax, but where a **D4** chord (**V4/2**) would be syntactically inappropriate, regardless of voice-leading.

在本文中，我们主要采用第一种方法，即罗马数字与（延伸的）和声功能，因为它在北美音乐理论中最为常用。不过，功能低音有助于识别归属于同一类别的和弦。例如，在听写或记谱练习中，我们可能听到低音是 *re*，却不知道具体是什么和弦。若语境告诉我们它很可能是属功能和弦而非下属功能和弦，我们可以把它标为 **D2**。这样就排除了 **II**（一个下属和弦），同时保留了 **V6/4** 或 **VII6** 等多个属功能选项，直到我们能够作出最终判定。同样地，在作曲时，有些模式可以采用 **S4**，其具体和弦（**IV** 或 **II6**）由声部进行而非和声句法决定；而 **D4** 和弦（**V4/2**）则在句法上并不合适，无论声部进行如何。

Thus, when referring to specific chords, we will use Roman numerals to label the chords and functional labels to interpret their role in context. When referring to broader categories of chords, we will more often use functional bass.

因此，在指称具体和弦时，我们用罗马数字标注和弦，用功能标记解释它们在语境中的作用。在指称更宽泛的和弦类别时，我们更常使用功能低音。
