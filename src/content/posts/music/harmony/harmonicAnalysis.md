Analyzing harmony in a piece or passage of music involves more than labeling chords. Even the most basic analysis also involves *interpreting* the way that specific chords and progressions function within a broader context. Ultimately, no analysis is complete until individual musical elements are interpreted in light of the work as a whole and the historical setting in which the piece occurs. But this resource simply walks through the steps of performing a basic harmonic analysis, interpreting each chord and chord progression in light of the musical phrase in which it occurs.

分析一首作品或一个段落的和声，并不仅仅是标注和弦。即使是最基本的分析，也包含*解释*特定和弦与和声进行在更广阔语境中如何发挥作用。归根到底，只有把单个音乐元素置于整部作品以及该作品所处的历史背景中加以解释，分析才算完成。不过，本材料只是逐步介绍进行基础分析的步骤，即依据每个和弦与和声进行所在的乐句来解释它们。

The first step in a harmonic analysis is to *identify phrases*. For the most part, that means beginning by identifying [*cadences*](/posts/music/harmony/cadenceTypes/). However, not every type of phrase ends with a cadence, so sensitivity to theme types is important. In classical instrumental music, that means listening for [period- and sentence-like structures](/posts/music/form/theme-types/classicalThemes/). In classical or romantic music with text, that means listening in particular for the ends of poetic lines and melodic phrases.

和声分析的第一步是*划分乐句*。大多数情况下，这意味着从辨认[*终止式*｜*cadences*](/posts/music/harmony/cadenceTypes/)入手。然而并非每一类乐句都以终止式结束，因此对主题类型的敏感很重要。在古典器乐中，这意味着要听辨[period- and sentence-like structures｜乐段式与乐句式结构](/posts/music/form/theme-types/classicalThemes/)。在带文本的古典或浪漫主义音乐中，这意味着要特别听辨诗行与旋律乐句的结束。

Once you have identified the musical phrases, it can be helpful to perform a harmonic reduction (thoroughbass reduction, for example) for each phrase. Below the score/thoroughbass line, write the appropriate Roman numeral, **T/S/D** label for each chord, and/or an uninterpreted functional bass symbol for each chord (**T1 T3 S4** etc.). [This handout](/media/music/graphics/Handouts/HarmoniesByBassScaleDegree.pdf) can help you determine the functions of chords in the thoroughbass reduction.

划分出音乐乐句之后，为每个乐句做一个和声缩编（例如数字低音缩编）会很有帮助。在乐谱／数字低音声部线之下，为每个和弦写出相应的罗马数字、**T/S/D** 标记，和/或未经解释的功能低音符号（**T1 T3 S4** 等）。[This handout｜本讲义](/media/music/graphics/Handouts/HarmoniesByBassScaleDegree.pdf) 有助于你在数字低音缩编中判定各和弦的功能。

Next identify the general harmonic structure of each phrase. Typical phrases in classical music will do one of the following:

接下来辨认每个乐句的总体和声结构。古典音乐中典型的乐句会做以下三件事之一：

- prolong tonic without a cadence (a classical *presentation* phrase, for example)／延伸主功能而不出现终止式（例如古典的*呈示*乐句）
- progress toward an authentic cadence (ending with **V(7) I**, **D5 T1** in functional bass)／走向正格终止（以 **V(7) I** 结束，功能低音为 **D5 T1**）
- progress toward a half cadence (ending with **V**, **D5** in functional bass)／走向半终止（以 **V** 结束，功能低音为 **D5**）

If the phrase prolongs tonic (no cadence), label the *entire* phrase **T–––.**

若该乐句延伸主功能（无终止式），将*整个*乐句标记为 **T–––。**

If the phrase ends with a cadence, identify the *cadential progression*. This includes the last chord of the tonic zone, optionally followed by a subdominant chord or zone (most often a single chord), and a required dominant zone (most often a single chord or compound cadence formula). Half-cadence phrases end there. Authentic-cadence phrases continue on to a final tonic zone (usually a single chord).

若该乐句以终止式结束，则辨认*终止进行*。终止进行包括主功能区的最后一个和弦，其后可选地接一个下属和弦或下属区（通常只有单个和弦），以及一个必需的属功能区（通常为单个和弦或复合终止公式）。半终止乐句在此结束。正格终止乐句则继续走向最后的主功能区（通常为单个和弦）。

The the **(S) D T** of the cadential progression should be labeled as such. Once the cadential progression is identified, everything before it is labeled as tonic prolongation. Regardless of whether it is contrapuntal prolongation, a subsidiary progression, or a combination of the two, it will be labeled **T–––.** (See [Harmonic syntax – prolongation](http://openmusictheory.com/harmonicSyntax2.html) if those terms are unfamiliar to you.)

终止进行的 **(S) D T** 应照此标注。一旦辨认出终止进行，其之前的一切都标为主功能延伸。无论它是对位性延伸、次级的进行，还是二者的结合，都标记为 **T–––。**（若对这些术语不熟悉，参见 [Harmonic syntax – prolongation｜和声句法：延伸](http://openmusictheory.com/harmonicSyntax2.html)。）

Thus a phrase ending with a half cadence will have a functional analysis that looks like:

因此，以半终止结束的乐句，其功能分析如下所示：

> T—————— (S) D

A phrase ending with an authentic cadence will have a functional analysis that looks like:

以正格终止结束的乐句，其功能分析如下所示：

> T—————— (S) D T

The tonic prolongation is shown below that with a T followed by a line for the duration of the tonic zone. The cadential progression is comprised of the last tonic chord (m. 4) through **S D T** to the **PAC** in m. 6.
