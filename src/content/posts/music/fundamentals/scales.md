A scale is a succession of pitches ascending or descending in steps. There are two types of steps: *half steps* and *whole steps*. A half step (H) consists of two adjacent pitches on the keyboard. A whole step (W) consists of two half steps. Usually, the pitches in a scale are each notated with different letter names, though this isn’t always possible or desirable.

音阶是音高按级进上行或下行的连续排列。级进有两种：*半音*与*全音*。一个半音（H）由键盘上相邻的两个音高构成。一个全音（W）由两个半音构成。通常，音阶中的各个音高都用不同的音名字母记写，尽管这并非总能做到，也并非总是可取。

## The Chromatic Scale｜半音阶

The chromatic scale consists entirely of half steps, and uses every pitch on the keyboard within a single octave. Here is the chromatic scale that spans the pitches C4 through C5.

半音阶完全由半音构成，并在一个八度内用遍键盘上的每一个音高。以下是跨越 C4 到 C5 各音的半音阶。

## The major scale｜大调音阶

A major scale, a sound with which you are undoubtedly familiar, consists of seven whole (W) and half (H) steps in the following succession: W-W-H-W-W-W-H. The first pitch of the scale, called the *tonic*, is the pitch upon which the rest of the scale is based. When the scale ascends, the tonic is repeated at the end an octave higher.

大调音阶——这种音响你无疑十分熟悉——由七个全音（W）与半音（H）按以下顺序构成：W-W-H-W-W-W-H。音阶的第一个音高称为*主音*，是音阶其余部分所依附的音。音阶上行时，主音在末尾以高一个八度重复出现。

Here is the D major scale. It is called the “D major scale” because the pitch D is the *tonic* and is heard at both ends of the scale.

下面是 D 大调音阶。它之所以称为「D 大调音阶」，是因为音高 D 是*主音*，在音阶的两端都能听到。

![大调音阶](/media/music/graphics/majorscale.png)

## Scale degrees and solfège｜音级与唱名

While ISO notation allows us to label a pitch in its specific register, it is often useful to know where that pitch fits within a given scale. For example, the pitch class D is the first (and last) note of the D-major scale. The pitch class A is the fifth note of the D-major scale. When described in this way, we call the notes *scale degrees*, because they’re placed in context of a specific scale. Solfège syllables, [a centuries-old method of teaching pitch and sight singing｜一种有数百年历史的音高与视唱教学法](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solfège), can also be used to represent scale degrees (when used in this way, this system is specifically called movable-*do* solfège).

虽然 ISO 标记法让我们能够标明某个音高所在的特定音区，但了解该音高在某一给定音阶中的位置往往也很有用。例如，音级 D 是 D 大调音阶的第一个（也是最后一个）音。音级 A 是 D 大调音阶的第五个音。以这种方式描述时，我们把这些音称为*音级*，因为它们被置于某个特定音阶的语境中。唱名音节——[一种有数百年历史的音高与视唱教学法｜a centuries-old method of teaching pitch and sight singing](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solfège)——也可用于表示音级（以这种方式使用时，该体系专门称为可动*do*唱名法）。

Scale degrees are labeled with Arabic numerals and carets (^). The illustration below shows a D-major scale and corresponding ISO notation, scale degrees, and solfège syllables.

音级用阿拉伯数字加扬抑符（^）来标记。下图展示了一个 D 大调音阶及其对应的 ISO 标记、音级与唱名音节。

## The minor scale｜小调音阶

Another scale with which you are likely very familiar is the minor scale. There are several scales that one might describe as *minor*, all of which have a characteristic third scale degree that is lower than the one found in the major scale. The minor scale most frequently used in tonal music from the Common Practice period is based on the *aeolian mode* (you’ll read more about modes later), which is sometimes referred to as the *natural minor* scale.

你很可能同样熟悉的另一种音阶是小调音阶。可以被称为*小调*的音阶有好几种，它们都具有一个特征性的第三音级，比大调音阶中的第三音级更低。共性写作时期的调性音乐中最常用的小调音阶基于*爱奥利亚调式*（后文你会读到更多关于调式的内容），有时也被称作*自然小调*音阶。

The natural minor scale consists of seven whole (W) and half (H) steps in the following succession: W-H-W-W-H-W-W. Note the changes in solfège syllables.

自然小调音阶由七个全音（W）与半音（H）按以下顺序构成：W-H-W-W-H-W-W。请注意唱名音节的变化。

[![自然小调的音级与唱名](/media/music/graphics/sdsf-naturalMinor.png)](/media/music/graphics/sdsf-naturalMinor.png)

If you sing through the above example, you’ll notice that the ending lacks the same sense of closure you heard in the major scale. This closure is created in the major scale, in part, by the ascending semitone between *ti* and *do*. Composers often want to have this sense of closure when using the minor mode, too. They’re able to achieve this by applying an accidental to the seventh scale degree, raising it by a semitone. If you do this within the context of the natural minor scale, you get something called the *harmonic minor* scale.

如果你把上例唱一遍，会注意到其结尾缺少大调音阶中那种收束感。在大调音阶中，这种收束感部分来自 *ti* 与 *do* 之间的上行半音。作曲家在运用小调式时，往往也希望获得这种收束感。他们通过在第七音级上添加变音记号、将其升高半音来实现这一点。若你在自然小调音阶的语境中这样做，就得到了所谓的*和声小调*音阶。

[![和声小调的音级与唱名](/media/music/graphics/sdsf-harmonicMinor.png)](/media/music/graphics/sdsf-harmonicMinor.png)

Now the last two notes of the scale sound much more conclusive, but you might have found it difficult to sing *le* to *ti*. When writing melodies in a minor key, composers often “corrected” this by raising *le* by a semitone to become *la* when approaching the note *ti*. When the melody descended from *do*, the closure from *ti* to *do* isn’t needed; likewise, it is no longer necessary to “correct” *le*, so the natural form of the minor scale is used again. Together, these different ascending and descending versions are called the *melodic minor* scale.

现在音阶最后两个音听起来有收束力得多，但你也许发现从 *le* 唱到 *ti* 有些困难。在为小调写作旋律时，作曲家往往通过把这个 *le* 升高半音变成 *la* 来「修正」它，即在接近 *ti* 音时如此处理。当旋律自 *do* 下行时，从 *ti* 到 *do* 的收束已无必要；同样，也不再需要「修正」*le*，于是又恢复使用小调音阶的自然形态。这些不同的上行与下行版本合在一起，称为*旋律小调*音阶。

When ascending, the *melodic minor* scale uses *la* and *ti*.

上行时，*旋律小调*音阶使用 *la* 与 *ti*。

[![旋律小调上行](/media/music/graphics/sdsf-melodicMinorAsc.png)](/media/music/graphics/sdsf-melodicMinorAsc.png)

When descending, the *melodic minor* scale uses the “natural” *te* and *le*.

下行时，*旋律小调*音阶使用「自然的」*te* 与 *le*。

[![旋律小调下行](/media/music/graphics/sdsf-melodicMinorDesc.png)](/media/music/graphics/sdsf-melodicMinorDesc.png)

Truth be told, most composers don’t really think about three different “forms” of the minor scale. The *harmonic minor* scale simply represents composers’ tendency to use *ti* when building harmonies that include the seventh scale degree in the minor mode. Likewise, the *melodic minor* scale is derived from composers’ desire to avoid the melodic augmented second interval (more on this in the [intervals｜音程](/posts/music/fundamentals/intervals/) section) between *le* and *ti* (and some chose not to avoid this!). In reality, there is only one “version” of the minor scale. Context determines when a composer might use *la* and *ti* when writing music in a minor key.

说实话，大多数作曲家并不真的去想小调音阶有三种不同的「形态」。*和声小调*音阶只是反映了作曲家在小调式中构建包含第七音级的和声时倾向于使用 *ti*。同样，*旋律小调*音阶源自作曲家希望避免 *le* 与 *ti* 之间的旋律增二度音程（更多内容见[音程](/posts/music/fundamentals/intervals/)一节）（也有一些人选择不去避免它！）。实际上，小调音阶只有一种「版本」。在小调写作音乐时，作曲家何时使用 *la* 与 *ti*，取决于语境。
