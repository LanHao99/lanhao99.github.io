*Invariance* refers to the preservation of something: intervals, dynamics, rhythms, pitches, and so on. In elementary twelve-tone theory, we are mostly concerned with *intervallic* invariance and *pitch class segmental* invariance.

*不变性*指某种要素的保持：音程、力度、节奏、音高等等。在基础十二音理论中，我们主要关注*音程*不变性与*音级片段*不变性。

**Intervallic Invariance**

**音程不变性**

Any time a row is [transposed](/posts/music/post-tonal/twelve-tone/twelveToneIntervallicStructure/), the ordered intervallic content of the row is unchanged. Thus, transposition always results in intervallic invariance. [Retrograde inversion](/posts/music/post-tonal/twelve-tone/twelveToneIntervallicStructure/) creates retrograde intervallic invariance.

每当序列行被[移位](/posts/music/post-tonal/twelve-tone/twelveToneIntervallicStructure/)，其有序音程内容保持不变。因此，移位总是带来音程不变性。[逆行倒影](/posts/music/post-tonal/twelve-tone/twelveToneIntervallicStructure/)则产生逆行音程不变性。

**Segmental Invariance**

**片段不变性**

When a pitch-class segment of a row is unchanged when that row is transformed, we say that the segment is “held invariant.” Consider the following example, from Webern’s String Quartet, Op. 28:

当序列行的某个音级片段在该序列行被变换后仍保持不变时，我们说该片段被“保持为不变”。看下面这个例子，出自韦伯恩《弦乐四重奏》Op. 28：

[![invariance](/media/music/graphics/postTonal/invariance.png)](/media/music/graphics/postTonal/invariance.png)

The brackets show the [discrete tetrachords](/posts/music/post-tonal/twelve-tone/twelveToneMusicDerivation/) of the row. Notice that these tetrachords are the *same* amongst the to different rows. That is, the tetrachords are *invariant segments.* These segments are held invariant because of they share the same *relationship* with one another that the rows share. Because the tetrachords are related by *T8*, when the row *as a whole* is transposed by *T8*, those tetrachords are “held invariant.” (Think of the process like this: when the first tetrachord [6789] is transposed by *T8*, it becomes the last tetrachord [2345]. And therefore, when the whole row is transposed by *T8, \_the last tetrachord \_becomes* the first tetrachord.)

方括号标出该序列行的[离散四音组](/posts/music/post-tonal/twelve-tone/twelveToneMusicDerivation/)。注意这些四音组在两个不同的序列行中是*相同*的。也就是说，这些四音组是*不变片段*。它们之所以被保持为不变，是因为它们彼此之间具有与两个序列行相同的那种*关系*。由于这些四音组以 *T8* 相关联，当序列行*作为整体*被 *T8* 移位时，这些四音组便“被保持为不变”。（可以这样理解这一过程：当第一个四音组 [6789] 被 *T8* 移位时，它变成最后一个四音组 [2345]。因此，当整条序列行被 *T8* 移位时，最后一个四音组就*变成了*第一个四音组。）

To determine when and if a pitch-class segment of a row will be held invariant:

要判断序列行的某个音级片段何时以及是否会被保持为不变：

(1) Find an equivalent set-class elsewhere in the row. This may be a dyad, trichord, tetrachord, etc.

（1）在序列行中其他地方找到一个等同的集合类。它可能是二音组、三音组、四音组等。

(2) Determine the transpositional or inversional relationship between them.

（2）确定它们之间的移位或倒影关系。

(3) When the row is transposed or inverted by that *same* relationship a segment will be held invariant.

（3）当序列行按*同一*关系被移位或倒影时，便会有某个片段被保持为不变。
