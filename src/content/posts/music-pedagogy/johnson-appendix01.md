### Paper Assignment-_Philadelphia_ ｜作业说明——《费城故事》

Write a short paper (approximately 5-6 pages, double-spaced typed) on the aria “La mamma morta” from Umberto Giordano’s opera _Andrea Chénier_ as used in the film _Philadelphia_. Your paper should focus primarily on musical analysis, but you must also put your analysis into the context of the libretto and the scene from the film in which the aria appears. You may write about broader themes in the film and/or the opera if you wish (but it is not required). In any case I will value specific observations over broad generalizations.

请就翁贝托·焦尔达诺的歌剧《安德烈·谢尼埃》中“La mamma morta”这首咏叹调在电影《费城故事》中的使用，撰写一篇短文（约 5-6 页，双倍行距、打印稿）。你的论文应以音乐分析为主，但同时也必须把分析置于台本以及电影中该咏叹调出现场景的语境之中。如果你愿意，也可以讨论电影和／或歌剧中更宽泛的主题（但并非必需）。无论如何，具体观察所得的评价都高于宽泛的概括。

You should write about specific aspects of the music using appropriate terms that you have learned in theory courses. For example, you might write about harmony, key relationships, modulation techniques, phrase structure, motivic development, melodic shape, texture, orchestration, and so forth. For the scope of this paper, you should choose a few analytical approaches, rather than trying to do a complete analysis of the aria. You should link most (or all) of your analytical comments to the context of the libretto and/or the film scene. But your paper must includes aspects of both the libretto and the film scene in relation to your analysis.

你应当运用理论课程中学到的恰当术语，讨论音乐的具体方面。例如，你可以讨论和声、调性关系、转调手法、乐句结构、动机发展、旋律形态、织体、配器等。就这篇论文的篇幅而言，你应当选择若干分析路径，而不必试图对整首咏叹调做完整分析。你应当把大部分（或全部）分析性论述与台本和／或电影场景的语境联系起来。但你的论文必须同时包含与你的分析相关的台本方面与电影场景方面。

This is not a research paper; there is no need to examine any of the many secondary sources about the use of this aria in the film. Many of these sources likely will lead you away from the goals of this paper; I would suggest that you not read them in conjunction with this assignment. If you do use any ideas other than your own, you must document your sources fully using a consistent style, such as MLA or APA. But I would expect that there would be no need for you to consult any sources (other than the score, recordings, and the film—none of which you need to cite for this assignment—and an English translation, which you will cite in a different way, as described below).

这不是一篇研究论文；无需考察关于这首咏叹调在影片中使用情况的众多二手资料。这些资料中许多很可能会让你偏离本论文的目标；我建议你不要在完成这份作业时阅读它们。如果你确实使用了任何并非出自你本人的观点，你必须以统一的格式（如 MLA 或 APA）完整注明来源。但我预计你无需查阅任何资料（乐谱、录音与影片除外——这些在本作业中均无需引用——以及一份英文译文，后者需要以另一种方式引用，详见下文）。

Please refer to the characters in the movie exactly as follows:

请严格按以下方式称呼影片中的人物：

1.  First use: Andy Beckett (Tom Hanks), Joe Miller (Denzel Washington)／首次提及：安迪·贝克特（汤姆·汉克斯）、乔·米勒（丹泽尔·华盛顿）
2.  Subsequent use: Andy, Joe (if referring to the characters); Hanks, Washington (if referring to the actors)／此后提及：安迪、乔（指人物时）；汉克斯、华盛顿（指演员时）

Please refer to the singer of the aria as Maddalena, not Maria Callas or the singer on the recording you are using.

请把这首咏叹调的演唱者称为玛达莱娜（Maddalena），而不要使用玛丽亚·卡拉斯，或你所使用录音中的歌手之名。

Get a translation separate from any score that you use. Translations in most scores are “singing translations,” which are intended to follow the music, even if the exact meaning is lost. There are many sources of translations (CD liners, online, and so forth—but not _Wikipedia_ please); you can determine if a translation is reasonably close to the meaning by comparing it with the Italian words or ideas that you recognize. In your paper when you write about the meaning of the libretto, use English. If you write about specific phrases from the libretto, use Italian. Use quotation marks when quoting directly. Please identify the source of the translation the first time you use or refer to it, which you can do informally in parentheses at the end of your sentence (trans. Hans Übersetzer, liner notes, CD 60517-2 Sony). When you refer to the opera or the film as a whole, use italics, as demonstrated in my first paragraph above.

请获取一份独立于你所使用任何乐谱的译文。多数乐谱中的译文是“歌唱译文”，其目的是贴合音乐，即便确切含义有所损失。译文的来源很多（CD 内页说明、网络等等——但请不要用 _Wikipedia_）；你可以把一份译文与你所能辨认的意大利语词句或意思相比较，从而判断它是否与含义大致接近。在论文中论述台本含义时，请使用英文。如果论述台本中的具体语句，请使用意大利文。直接引用时请使用引号。首次使用或提及某份译文时请注明其来源，可以在句末以括号形式非正式地标明（trans. Hans Übersetzer, liner notes, CD 60517-2 Sony）。当整体指称这部歌剧或这部电影时，请使用斜体，如我上文第一段所示。

Please see the following additional guidelines you must follow in this paper. Above all, remember, this is a musical analysis paper, and you need to be specific.

请参阅以下你必须在本论文中遵守的补充准则。最重要的是，请记住：这是一篇音乐分析论文，你需要做到具体。

### Some guidelines for format and style:｜格式与风格方面的若干准则：

1. __Title__: Try to be as specific as possible. Refer to the actual topic your paper addresses not just the name of the piece or film. Aim for a title that captures the important feature(s) of your paper. You probably will not be able to title your paper until after you have written it.／__标题__：尽量具体。要指向论文实际处理的论题，而不只是作品或影片的名称。力求拟定一个能够抓住论文重要特征的标题。你很可能要在写完之后才能为论文定题。

2. __Introduction__: The introduction need not be long, but it must successfully introduce the topic and capture the reader's interest. Begin your paper with an _idea_ (or ideas) rather than a fact. Limit the background information you provide (if any) to material that relates directly to the points you will make in your paper. Be sure to provide structural clues that give the reader a sense of what areas you will cover in your paper and in what order.／__引言__：引言不必很长，但必须成功地引出论题并抓住读者的兴趣。请以某个（或某些）_观点_开篇，而不是以某个事实开篇。你提供的背景信息（如果有的话）应限于与你论文将要提出的论点直接相关的材料。务必提供结构性的线索，让读者了解你的论文将涵盖哪些方面、以何顺序展开。

3. __Presentation of the analysis__: Organize your paper around ideas—not based the order of the music. A measure-by-measure catalogue of a piece is unnecessary and usually boring. Instead, refer to specific features of the music as appropriate to support or explain your ideas. Be sure to provide a sufficient amount of detail to defend your ideas. The reader should be “taken by the hand” and led through the points of your paper with every point explained meticulously and supported clearly by the evidence in the music.／__分析的呈现__：围绕观点组织论文，而不要依据音乐的顺序。逐小节罗列作品既无必要，通常也乏味。相反，应视需要援引音乐的具体特征来支持或说明你的观点。务必提供足够的细节为你的观点辩护。读者应当被“牵着手”依次走过你论文的各个论点，每个论点都得到细致的阐释，并由音乐中的证据清楚地支撑。

4. __Conclusion__: Summarize your analysis and discuss its significance. What does your investigation reveal about the piece and/or its use in the film?／__结论__：总结你的分析并讨论其意义。你的考察揭示了这部作品和／或它在影片中运用方式的什么内容？

5. __Style__: This is a scholarly analytical paper and should be written in a generally serious style. Be specific: avoid generalizations that are beyond the scope of the paper. Write in complete, declarative sentences. Pay attention to sentence structure; sometimes complex ideas require complex (but clear and correct) sentences. Read your work aloud. Your “audience” includes students in this course, or similar courses, at all colleges/universities in the country, as well as your instructor.／__文风__：这是一篇学术性分析论文，整体应采用严肃的文风。要具体：避免超出论文范围的概括。用完整的陈述句写作。注意句子结构；有时复杂的思想需要复杂（但清晰而正确）的句子。请把你的作品朗读出来。你的“读者”包括本课程或全国各高校同类课程的学生，以及你的授课教师。

6. __Technical Details__: Refer to measure numbers by abbreviation (m. 25 for a single measure, mm. 25-27 for a range). Number the measures of the aria consecutively, rather than in combination with rehearsal numbers, as is customary. If you refer to individual chords, be consistent. Use uppercase and lowercase Roman numerals for chord numbers as appropriate for chord quality (ii, V) or use diatonic scale-degree names (supertonic, dominant). Indicate inversions with numbers or words (ii6 or first-inversion supertonic). Describe keys and generic chords using capital letters followed by the mode or quality (A major, A minor, _not a minor_). Label the first measure in which Maddalena sings as m. 1 (the measure with a whole note in the accompaniment). ／__技术细节__：小节数请使用缩写形式（单小节写作 m. 25，范围写作 mm. 25-27）。这首咏叹调的小节应连续编号，而不要像通常那样与排练号合并使用。如果你逐一指称某个和弦，请保持一致。和弦级数的罗马数字请按和弦性质使用大写或小写（ii、V），或使用自然音阶的级数名称（上主音、属音）。用数字或文字标示转位（ii6 或第一转位上主音）。描述调性与一般和弦时，请用大写字母后接调式或性质（A major、A minor，而_不是_ a minor）。把玛达莱娜演唱的第一小节标注为 m. 1（即伴奏中带全音符的那一小节）。

7. __Instrumentation__: Although you are using a piano-vocal score, you should refer to the specific instruments or instrument groups employed (as _heard_ on the recording—you do not need to refer to an orchestral score). You should not refer to the melody in the right hand. Instead, you should refer to the melody in the solo cello or the accompaniment pattern in the woodwinds. Listen.／__乐器编制__：虽然你使用的是钢琴声乐谱，但你应当指称实际使用的具体乐器或乐器组（以录音中_听到的_为准——你无需参照管弦乐总谱）。你不应指称右手声部中的旋律，而应指称独奏大提琴中的旋律或木管组中的伴奏音型。要去聆听。

8. __Examples__: You should not provide musical examples for this paper.／__谱例__：本论文不需要提供音乐谱例。

9. __Academic Honesty__: You _may not_ discuss this assignment, any aspect of the music, or any ideas you have about the piece with _any_ person other than your instructor for this course or the staff at the writing center. You must do your own work throughout this project. Collaboration of _any_ kind will not be tolerated.／__学术诚信__：除本课程授课教师或写作中心的教职工之外，你_不得_与_任何_人讨论这份作业、音乐的任何一个方面，或你对这部作品的任何想法。整个项目过程中你必须独立完成自己的工作。_任何_形式的合作都不被容忍。
