### Appendix 1｜附录 1

_A Sample Group Assessment with Collaborative Learning. This assignment was used in a class called "Music Phrases Through the Ages," a first-year seminar for non-music majors._

_一份含协作学习的小组评估样例。该作业曾用于一门名为"历代音乐乐句"的课程，那是为非音乐主修生开设的一年级研讨课。_

For each activity below, each student should be both a performer and a listener. When you have finished all activities, fill out the online form on Sakai for Practicum \#2.

对于下列每项活动，每位学生都应既是表演者又是聆听者。完成所有活动后，请在 Sakai 上填写实操练习 \#2 的在线表单。

### Activity 1: Rhythmic Accompaniment and Rounds｜活动 1：节奏伴奏与轮唱

Listeners:

聆听者：

1.  Sing the tune “Are You Sleeping” (see below). As you sing, the performer will improvise a clapped accompaniment. This tune is also known by its French title,  “Frère Jacques.” You can sing in either English or French.／演唱《Are You Sleeping》这支曲调（见下）。在你演唱时，表演者将即兴配上一段拍手伴奏。这支曲调也以其法语标题《Frère Jacques》著称。你可以用英语或法语演唱。

Performer:

表演者：

2.  As the other two members of your group sing “Are You Sleeping,” clap an improvised rhythmic accompaniment.  Think about the give-and-take of parts.  In other words, as the melody is active, consider a less active accompaniment, and when the melody is static, increase your rhythmic activity.／当小组另外两名成员演唱《Are You Sleeping》时，拍出一段即兴的节奏伴奏。思考各声部之间的此消彼长。换言之，旋律活跃时，考虑用较为平静的伴奏；旋律静止时，增加你节奏的活跃度。

3.  This balance between melody and accompaniment is an important feature of musical texture. ／旋律与伴奏之间的这种平衡，是音乐织体的一个重要特征。

![rifkin 图片 00](/media/music-pedagogy/rifkin-image00.jpg)

Together:

全体一起：

1.  Discuss the texture created by the performer’s improvisation. What did you like? What didn’t you like? Why?／讨论表演者即兴所创造出的织体。你喜欢哪些地方？不喜欢哪些地方？为什么？

2.  This tune can also be performed in a round! The round parts are numbered 1–4 above. The first person starts the tune. Just as the first person is about to sing “Brother John”, the second person starts the song. Just as the second person is about to sing “Brother John,” the third person starts the song. Another way to think about this: the second person starts singing after the first person has sung 2 measures, and the third person starts to sing after the first person has sung 4 measures.／这支曲调也可以用轮唱方式表演！轮唱的声部已标在上方 1–4 处。第一人起唱。就在第一人即将唱到 "Brother John" 时，第二人起唱。就在第二人即将唱到 "Brother John" 时，第三人起唱。另一种理解方式是：第二人在第一人唱完 2 小节后起唱，第三人在第一人唱完 4 小节后起唱。

3.  If you can’t keep the round together, try simplifying the task to only rhythms.  Clap the rhythms of the song in round.／如果你们无法把轮唱配合整齐，试着把任务简化为只做节奏。以轮唱方式拍出这首歌的节奏。

4.   What do you notice about how the parts interact? Do they all move at the same time? Are some parts slower/faster than others?／你注意到各声部如何互动？它们是否同时进行？是否有些声部比其他声部更慢/更快？

### Activity 2: More Phrase Phun.｜活动 2：更多乐句之趣

Everyone together:

全体一起：

1.  Listen to the first phrase of Mozart’s theme from his Flute Quartet, K. 298, mvt. 1.  A recording of the first phrase is available on Sakai.／聆听莫扎特《长笛四重奏》K. 298 第一乐章主题的第一个乐句。第一个乐句的录音可在 Sakai 上获取。

2.   Sing along with the first phrase multiple times, until you feel as if you have memorized it. Conduct along with the recording as you sing.／跟着第一个乐句反复演唱多次，直到你感觉自己已经背下来。演唱时随着录音一起指挥。

3.  Next, without the recording, sing the first phrase.／接下来，不用录音，演唱第一个乐句。

4.   As a group, analyze the melody: What meter is it in?  How many measures are there? Does it end on an open or closed cadence?／以小组为单位分析这段旋律：它是什么拍子？有多少小节？它结束于开放终止还是收束终止？

Listener:

聆听者：

1.  When both listeners feel confident, sing the first phrase of Mozart’s tune.／当两位聆听者都感到有把握时，演唱莫扎特曲调的第一个乐句。

Performer:

表演者：

1.  After the listeners have completed their phrase, improvise a PARALLEL CONSEQUENT phrase that completes the period.  As we discussed in class, the parallel consequent phrase will start off the same but will end differently.  The ending will change to accommodate a closed cadence; your melody should end on 1 (do.)／在聆听者们唱完他们的乐句之后，即兴一个完成该乐段的平行后句。正如课堂上讨论过的，平行后句的开头相同，但结尾不同。结尾将改变以配合收束终止；你的旋律应结束在 1（do）上。

Together:

全体一起：

1.  Discuss the performer’s improvised consequent phrase. Did it result in a parallel period? What was similar to the antecedent? What was different? What other options could there have been?／讨论表演者即兴的后句。它是否构成了平行乐段？与先行句相比，哪些地方相似？哪些地方不同？还可能有哪些其他选择？
