*The “Technology Tools” Session at FlipCamp Music Theory*

For those who attended the [FlipCamp Music Theory](http://flipcampmt.wordpress.com/home/) conference at Charleston Southern University on June 17–18, 2013, the “Technology Tools” session provided valuable insight into some of the innovative ways that music instructors are incorporating technology into their teaching methodologies. What follows is a short summary of the topics covered during the session, including links to computer applications and online services that were discussed, as well as articles and blog posts for those wishing to explore particular topics in greater depth. Hopefully, this brief overview will serve as a jumping-off point for instructors who are looking to take advantage of the many technological resources that are out there, but who are not quite sure where to begin.

对那些参加了 2013 年 6 月 17–18 日在查尔斯顿南方大学举办的 [FlipCamp Music Theory](http://flipcampmt.wordpress.com/home/) 研讨会的人来说，“技术工具”专场就音乐教师把技术纳入自身教学方法的一些创新做法提供了有价值的见解。以下是对该专场所涉话题的简要总结，包括会上讨论过的计算机应用程序与在线服务的链接，以及供希望更深入探究特定话题者参考的文章与博文。希望这份简要综述能成为一个起点，帮助那些想要利用现有众多技术资源、却又不太确定从何入手的教师。

Before getting into specific topics, it is worth making a few observations about the general tone of the session (and FlipCamp Music Theory as a whole). First, no one at the conference advocated the use of technology simply *for the sake of using technology*. Every participant seemed acutely aware that technology is only pedagogically valuable when it meets two fairly basic criteria: 1) helping students utilize their time inside and outside the classroom as efficiently and effectively as possible; 2) helping students learn material and master skills more quickly and thoroughly than traditional teaching methods allow.

在进入具体话题之前，有必要就该专场（以及整个 FlipCamp Music Theory）的总体基调谈几点观察。第一，会上没有人主张仅仅*为了使用技术而使用技术*。每位与会者似乎都清醒地意识到，技术只有在满足两条相当基本的标准时才具有教学价值：1）帮助学生尽可能高效、有效地利用课内与课外时间；2）帮助学生比传统教学方法更快、更扎实地学习内容并掌握技能。

Second, no one at the conference suggested that instructors should start using every piece of technology at their disposal . . . *immediately!* Several of the resources and strategies listed below have steep learning curves, and will likely require a significant investment of time (not to mention trial and error) before they can be comfortably incorporated into your pedagogical apparatus. Learning a new computer application, teaching students how to use it, and of course “troubleshooting” will invariably eat up more of your time (and sanity) than you expect, so it’s important to pace yourself. As a general rule, you probably don’t want to deal with more than one big technological change in your life per semester.

第二，会上没有人建议教师应当把手边的每一项技术都用起来……*马上就上！* 下文列出的若干资源与策略学习曲线陡峭，很可能需要投入大量时间（更不用说反复试错），才能顺利地纳入你的教学装备之中。学习一款新的计算机应用程序、教会学生使用它，当然还有“排障”，无一例外会比你预想的更耗时间（也更耗心神），因此把握节奏十分重要。作为一般原则，你大概不希望每学期在自己的生活里同时应付超过一项重大的技术变革。

Finally, no one at the conference pretended to live in an academic utopia. We are all, to some extent, constrained by the financial resources, infrastructure, and student culture of the departments and institutions at which we teach, so questions of feasibility and practicality will be at the front of anybody’s mind when it comes to technological decisions. However, these constraints are often less of an obstacle than we realize, given the plethora of readily-available technology that can be utilized—or “hacked”—to meet our students’ pedagogical needs. Thus, feasibility and practicality are as much a question of our willingness to explore and experiment as they are of institutional limitations.

最后，会上没有人假装自己身处学术乌托邦。在某种程度上，我们所有人都受制于所在院系与院校的经费、基础设施和学生文化，因此在做技术决策时，可行性问题与实用性问题必然摆在每个人的心头。不过，鉴于有大量现成的技术可供利用——或者可供“改造”——以满足学生的教学需求，这些限制往往并不像我们以为的那样构成障碍。因此，可行性与实用性既取决于院校的局限，也同样取决于我们探索与实验的意愿。

With these considerations in mind, we can turn to some of the specific topics that were covered during the “Technology Tools” session.

带着这些考量，我们可以转向“技术工具”专场中涉及的一些具体话题。

### Screencasting｜屏幕录制

A screencast is a video recording of your computer screen’s output, usually accompanied by an audio commentary that you record simultaneously (or add in later). Although many people think of screencasting primarily as a way to create highly polished video lectures, it is important to recognize that screencasting is a flexible tool that can be used for a diverse range of pedagogical tasks. In addition to mini-lectures, it can be used for pre-class preparation (e.g., [JiTT](/posts/music-pedagogy/hughes/) assignments), post-class follow-up (e.g., tying up loose ends after an in-class discussion), homework assignments or class projects (including having students make their own screencasts), answering student questions, or providing feedback to students on homework and exams. (Kris Shaffer discusses the use of screencasting software for video grading [here](http://kris.shaffermusic.com/2012/04/video-grading-for-transcription-arranging-projects/). A general introduction to screencasting for educational purposes can be found [here](http://www.educause.edu/ero/article/screencasting-engage-learning).)

屏幕录制是对计算机屏幕输出画面的视频记录，通常配有同期录制（或后期添加）的音频讲解。尽管许多人主要把屏幕录制视为制作高完成度视频讲座的方式，但重要的是要认识到，它是一种灵活的工具，可用于多种多样的教学任务。除了微讲座之外，它还可用于课前准备（例如 [JiTT](/posts/music-pedagogy/hughes/) 作业）、课后跟进（例如在课堂讨论之后收尾）、家庭作业或课堂项目（包括让学生自己制作屏幕录制）、回答学生提问，或就作业与考试向学生提供反馈。（Kris Shaffer 在[此处](http://kris.shaffermusic.com/2012/04/video-grading-for-transcription-arranging-projects/)讨论了使用屏幕录制软件进行视频评分。[此处](http://www.educause.edu/ero/article/screencasting-engage-learning)可找到面向教育用途的屏幕录制入门介绍。）

As Phil Duker explained during the “Technology Tools” session, there are a few things to keep in mind when creating screencasts:

正如 Phil Duker 在“技术工具”专场中所解释的，制作屏幕录制时有几点需要注意：

- Not every screencast has to be the most beautiful thing you’ve ever created. Sometimes, quick and dirty videos can be just as informative as those on which you’ve spent hours and hours. Ask yourself: is the video you’re creating something that you’ll want to use every semester (e.g., a lesson on first-species counterpoint), or will it serve a one-time purpose (e.g., answering a specific student’s question)? If it’s the former, then you may want to invest a fair amount of time. But if it’s the latter, you’ll want to spend as little time as necessary.／并非每一段屏幕录制都必须是你能做出来的最精美的作品。有时，粗糙但快速做出来的视频与耗费大量时间制作的视频同样具有信息量。问一问自己：你正在制作的视频是每个学期都要反复使用的东西（例如一节关于一类对位法的课），还是只服务于一次性的目的（例如回答某个学生的问题）？如果是前者，你或许值得投入相当多的时间；如果是后者，则应尽量少花时间。
- It can be tempting to overload individual screencasts with a lot of information. But short videos dealing with relatively narrow topics are what students tend to find the most useful. Indeed, some people argue that screencasts should be no more than 2–7 minutes, which means dividing larger topics (e.g., those that might be covered in a single 50-minute lecture) across several videos.／给单段屏幕录制塞进大量信息是很诱人的。但学生往往觉得，处理相对狭窄话题的短小视频最有用。事实上，有人认为屏幕录制不应超过 2–7 分钟，这意味着要把较大的话题（例如本可放在一节 50 分钟讲座中讲完的内容）拆分到多个视频中。
- Some screencasting programs (e.g., Camtasia and ScreenFlow 4) are quite elaborate (and expensive), and provide a lot of recording and editing options. Others are quite basic (and free). If you want to spend a lot of time learning a complicated program in order to produce really high-quality videos, great! But recognize that you have other options.／有些屏幕录制程序（例如 Camtasia 和 ScreenFlow 4）相当精细（也昂贵），提供大量录制与剪辑选项。另一些则相当基础（且免费）。如果你愿意花大量时间学习一款复杂程序，以制作真正高质量的视频，那很好！但要认识到，你还有其他选择。

### Student Response Technology｜学生应答技术

Student response systems (a.k.a. “clickers”) facilitate real-time assessment of student understanding in the classroom. Instructors can pose true or false, multiple choice, or short answer questions to their students (among other possibilities, depending on the program), see their students’ answers almost immediately (as well as collated data regarding correct/incorrect responses), assess their students’ understanding of a particular concept, and tailor the organization of their remaining class time accordingly. At FlipCamp Music Theory, there were several demonstrations of web-based applications (i.e., virtual clickers) and remote devices (i.e., physical clickers) that conference participants have used. Links to the websites for these applications/devices are given below. (Again, be aware that your school may already use one of these services.) In addition, you can read about Phil Duker’s use of clicker technology to help his students develop their aural analysis skills [here](/posts/music-pedagogy/duker/).

学生应答系统（又称“clickers”，即答题器）有助于在课堂上实时评估学生的理解程度。教师可以向学生提出判断题、选择题或简答题（视程序不同还有其他题型），几乎即时看到学生的作答（以及关于答对/答错的汇总数据），评估学生对某一概念的理解，并据此调整剩余课堂时间的组织安排。在 FlipCamp Music Theory 上，有多场演示展示了与会者使用过的基于网络的应用程序（即虚拟答题器）与手持设备（即实体答题器）。这些应用程序/设备的网站链接如下。（再次提醒，你的学校可能已经在使用其中某项服务。）此外，你可以在[此处](/posts/music-pedagogy/duker/)读到 Phil Duker 如何利用答题器技术帮助学生发展听觉分析能力。

### Music Streaming｜音乐流媒体

在为课堂上的学生提供音频示例方面，只能依赖音乐图书馆里的录音（再以个人 CD 收藏作补充）的日子早已过去。如今不仅大多数高校都订阅了 [Naxos Music Library](http://www.naxosmusiclibrary.com/home.asp?rurl=%2Fdefault.asp) 或 Alexander Street Press 的 [Classical Music Library](http://clmu.alexanderstreet.com/) 这类音乐流媒体服务，而且无数录音以视频形式存在于 [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/) 上，任何能上网的人都可以获取。

### Sight-Singing Assessment｜视唱评估

The need to assess student singing on a frequent and regular basis is one of the perennial challenges for those of us who teach aural skills classes. At FlipCamp Music Theory, Gretchen Foley discussed her use of [SmartMusic](http://www.smartmusic.com/) to assess sight singing exercises that her students perform and record each week outside of class. She explained how the program can provide instant feedback to students on the accuracy of their performances in terms of pitch and rhythm, as well as an approximate grade. Gretchen (or her teaching assistants) can then access students’ sound files both to confirm (or adjust) their grades and to evaluate the accuracy of their solfège. Not only does this free up a significant amount of class-time, it also helps students establish a more consistent routine for practicing their sight singing.

对于我们这些教授听觉技能课的人来说，需要频繁而定期地评估学生的演唱是一项长期挑战。在 FlipCamp Music Theory 上，Gretchen Foley 介绍了她如何使用 [SmartMusic](http://www.smartmusic.com/) 来评估学生每周课外演唱并录制的视唱练习。她解释了该程序如何就学生演唱在音高与节奏方面的准确性即时给出反馈，并提供一个大致的分数。随后 Gretchen（或她的助教）可以调取学生的音频文件，既用以确认（或调整）成绩，也用以评估其视唱唱名（solfège）的准确性。这不仅释放出大量课堂时间，也有助于学生建立更稳定的视唱练习习惯。

Other programs that conference participants have used to collect audio recordings of students’ sight singing exercises include [DropVox](http://www.irradiatedsoftware.com/dropvox/) and [SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/). However, these programs are not designed specifically for music educators, and do not offer the extensive recording/grading features that make SmartMusic such an effective tool.

与会者用来收集学生视唱练习录音的其他程序还包括 [DropVox](http://www.irradiatedsoftware.com/dropvox/) 和 [SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/)。不过，这些程序并非专为音乐教育工作者设计，不具备使 SmartMusic 成为如此高效工具的那些丰富的录制/评分功能。

(Nevertheless, even fairly basic recording technology can still be very useful, as Trevor de Clercq explains [here](/posts/music-pedagogy/deClercq/).) 

（尽管如此，即便是相当基础的录音技术仍然可以非常有用，正如 Trevor de Clercq 在[此处](/posts/music-pedagogy/deClercq/)所解释的那样。） 

### Social Media｜社交媒体

While most of the “Technology Tools” session was geared toward the use of software specifically designed for educational/professional purposes, several individuals mentioned how social media (e.g., Twitter, Facebook, blogs), which most of our students use in their everyday personal lives, can be adapted to serve a pedagogical function—for instance, as an alternative to the online discussion forums provided by learning management systems. (See below for more on discussion forums.)

虽然“技术工具”专场的大部分内容都针对专为教育/专业目的设计的软件，但有几位与会者提到，社交媒体（例如 Twitter、Facebook、博客）——我们的多数学生在日常生活中都在使用——可以被改造用来承担教学功能，例如作为学习管理系统所提供的在线讨论论坛的替代方案。（关于讨论论坛的更多内容，见下文。）

In particular, Kris Shaffer discussed his use of Twitter as part of an "inquiry-based" approach to teaching species counterpoint. Kris explained how his students would tweet their observations in real time as they attempted to figure out the rules for each species, after which Kris would consolidate their tweets and fill in any gaps in their collective understanding. Your can read about Kris's experiment (and about inquiry-based learning) [here](/posts/music-pedagogy/shafferpt3/).

其中，Kris Shaffer 介绍了他如何把 Twitter 用作种类对位“探究式”教学法的一部分。Kris 解释了他的学生如何在对每种对位法的规则进行探究的过程中实时发布推文记录观察，随后 Kris 会汇总这些推文，并补足他们集体理解中的空缺。你可以在[此处](/posts/music-pedagogy/shafferpt3/)了解 Kris 的实验（以及探究式学习的相关内容）。

(Also, you can read about Alexander Ludwig's use of Twitter in music history classes as an alternative to traditional listening journals [here](/posts/music-pedagogy/ludwig/).) 

（另外，你可以在[此处](/posts/music-pedagogy/ludwig/)读到 Alexander Ludwig 如何在音乐史课上用 Twitter 替代传统的聆听日志。）

你可能熟悉 Finale 或 Sibelius 这类商业记谱程序，但要知道，你并不需要花上几百美元购买软件才能制作数字乐谱。对于许多作业（例如对位项目与范例创作）而言，下列程序（每一个都可以让你和你的学生免费下载或在线使用）很可能足以满足你的教学需求：

- [MuseScore](http://musescore.org/)
- [Noteflight](http://www.noteflight.com/login) (This is a web-based music notation program. ### Free Audio Editing Software｜免费音频剪辑软件

Like music notation software, audio editing software can cost a small fortune ... but it doesn't have to. [Audacity](http://audacity.sourceforge.net/) is a free audio editor that performs many of the basic functions available in professional editing programs. It's a convenient way for you (and your students!) to edit sound files for presentations, assignments, and class projects. (Crystal Peebles discusses her use of Audacity-based activities to help students improve their aural analysis skills [here](/posts/music-pedagogy/peebles/).)

与记谱软件一样，音频剪辑软件可能花费不菲……但并非必须如此。[Audacity](http://audacity.sourceforge.net/) 是一款免费的音频编辑器，具备专业剪辑程序中的许多基本功能。对于你（以及你的学生！）来说，它是为演示、作业和课堂项目剪辑声音文件的便捷方式。（Crystal Peebles 在[此处](/posts/music-pedagogy/peebles/)讨论了她如何利用基于 Audacity 的活动帮助学生提升听觉分析能力。）

[Variations Audio Timeliner](http://variations.sourceforge.net/vat/) 是一款免费程序，可用于创建带标注的音乐作品曲式图示，并与内嵌的音频录音同步。关于该程序的入门视频可在[此处](http://vimeo.com/42041355)找到。

### Discussion Forums｜讨论论坛

Although we did not spend a considerable amount of time on the topic of discussion forums, we did briefly discuss an [article](http://www.hybridpedagogy.com/Journal/files/Discussion_Forum_is_Dead.html) from the online journal [*Hybrid Pedagogy*](http://www.hybridpedagogy.com/index.html) on the limits of discussion forums provided by learning management systems. (For an additional critique of learning management systems, see Pete Rorabaugh, “[Hack the LMS: Getting Progressive](http://www.hybridpedagogy.com/Journal/files/Learning_Management.html),” Hybrid Pedagogy (January 5, 2012), accessed July 21, 2013.)

尽管我们没有在讨论论坛这一话题上花很多时间，但确实简要讨论了在线期刊 [*Hybrid Pedagogy*](http://www.hybridpedagogy.com/index.html) 上的一篇[文章](http://www.hybridpedagogy.com/Journal/files/Discussion_Forum_is_Dead.html)，内容关于学习管理系统所提供的讨论论坛的局限。（关于对学习管理系统的另一篇批评，参见 Pete Rorabaugh，[“Hack the LMS: Getting Progressive](http://www.hybridpedagogy.com/Journal/files/Learning_Management.html)”，Hybrid Pedagogy（2012 年 1 月 5 日），2013 年 7 月 21 日访问。）

In addition to social media sites like Facebook and Twitter, several participants mentioned [Piazza](https://piazza.com/) as a useful alternative. This is an online discussion forum with wiki-style Q&A in which students and instructors can edit answers (Wikipedia style) rather than create lengthy and cumbersome discussion threads, and instructors can endorse student answers that are especially accurate or insightful.

除了 Facebook 和 Twitter 这类社交网站之外，还有几位与会者提到 [Piazza](https://piazza.com/) 是一个有用的替代方案。这是一个带有维基式问答的在线讨论论坛，学生与教师可以（以维基百科的方式）编辑答案，而不必创建冗长繁琐的讨论串，教师还可以认可学生格外准确或有见地的回答。
