*Flipping the Classroom: Three Methods*

“Flipping the class” is a hot topic in education today. In fact, the “flipped classroom” has become somewhat of a buzzword in some education—and especially education technology—circles. However, as some use this relatively new term uncritically, and others seek to co-opt it to hawk a product, confusion has grown around it, and many are unclear as to what a “flipped” class is.

“翻转课堂”是当今教育领域的热门话题。事实上，在一些教育界——尤其是教育技术界——的圈子里，“翻转课堂”已经在某种程度上变成了一个流行词。然而，一些人未经审视地使用这个相对较新的术语，另一些人则试图把它挪用过来兜售产品，围绕它的混乱因此不断加深，许多人不清楚“翻转”课堂究竟指什么。

In the following three essays, Bryn Hughes and Kris Shaffer offer some clarification about the “flipped” or “inverted” class illustrating three pedagogical models from the flipped-class movement that have proven successful in higher-ed settings, with examples from university-level music courses. Those three models are the [*basic flip*](/posts/music-pedagogy/shafferpt1/), [*peer instruction* with *just-in-time teaching*](/posts/music-pedagogy/hughes/), and [*inquiry-driven* learning](/posts/music-pedagogy/shafferpt3/). Though each of these models are fast becoming buzzwords in their own right, they each have much to offer the university instructor who seeks to increase their pedagogical effectiveness and help students take charge of their own learning. And though these three models can look quite different in practice, they all have the same underlying objectives: to put the student, rather than the instructor or the progression of content, at the center of the class, and to use in-class and out-of-class time as effectively as possible to help students meet the course goals.

在接下来的三篇文章中，Bryn Hughes 与 Kris Shaffer 对“翻转”课堂或“倒置”课堂略作澄清，阐述翻转课堂运动中三种在高等教育环境中已被证明行之有效的教学模式，并以大学音乐课程为例。这三种模式是[*基础翻转*｜*basic flip*](/posts/music-pedagogy/shafferpt1/)、[*同伴教学*｜*peer instruction*结合*即时教学*｜*just-in-time teaching*](/posts/music-pedagogy/hughes/)以及[*探究驱动*｜*inquiry-driven*式学习](/posts/music-pedagogy/shafferpt3/)。尽管这三种模式本身也正迅速各自成为流行词，但它们都能为那些希望提高教学效能、帮助学生自主掌控学习的大学教师提供许多助益。这三种模式在实践中可能看起来相去甚远，但它们有着共同的底层目标：把学生、而非教师或内容的推进置于课堂的中心，并尽可能有效地利用课内与课外时间，帮助学生达成课程目标。

The inverted class is certainly not the only way to accomplish these objectives, but it is an effective framework for helping *individual* students meet their educational goals in the variety of ways that are best to them, even in the context of a whole-class setting.

倒置课堂当然不是达成这些目标的唯一方式，但它是一个有效的框架，能帮助*每一个*学生以最适合自己的多种方式实现其学习目标，即便是在整班教学的语境之中。
